# convex/following

Following and the feed (spec/14).

- `follows.ts`: follow requests, accepting, unfollowing, removing and blocking a follower.
- `feed.ts`: a manager's feed: followed managers' moves and swaps, series points and big Carries.
- `lines.ts`: the truck lines database: adding, approving and seeding lines, the no-player check.
- `fit.ts`: the lines that fit a receiver's week, weighted, and filling a line's placeholders.
- `trucks.ts`: sending coffee and protest trucks, where they park, and sponsor trucks.
