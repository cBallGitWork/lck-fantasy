# convex/gameweeks

Gameweeks, deadlines and settling a gameweek.

- `weekPoints.ts`: a manager's gameweek points by the game's own scoring, with the Captain's and the buff's shares.
- `starAndFlop.ts`: effective ownership, and the gameweek's star and flop (spec/12, Home).
- `yourWeek.ts`: a manager's own week on Points: three Stars, the Flop, three Killers and both Overall tier gaps (spec/12, Points; spec/08).
