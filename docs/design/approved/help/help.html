HelpRules

Flol is a free fantasy game where you pick real LCK players, from Korea's League of Legends league, and score points from how they play in their actual games.

You start by building a squad, choosing your starters, picking a Captain each week, and then managing your team across the whole season. You play against everyone else on Flol, and you can make leagues to take on your friends.

There's a lot to take in at first. Start with the basics below, then use Rules for the full detail.

Flol in 60 seconds

You get ₩100.0m of game money to build a squad of 9 LCK players. They score points for what they do in real games, such as kills, assists and staying alive.

Flol runs one LCK week at a time. Each week, 7 of your squad score for you: one player in each of the five positions, a Flex, and a whole LCK team in your coach slot. The other 2 sit on your bench. You pick a Captain, whose points count one and a half times.

Transfers let you swap players in and out as the season goes on. Four buffs, played one at a time, give your score a boost.

The aim is to score as many points as you can over the season and climb the rankings, while taking on other people in leagues.

How a week works

A Flol week is one LCK week. It usually runs Wednesday to Sunday, and in the regular season each of the 10 LCK teams plays two series in it, at about 17:00 and 19:30 Korea time.

  • A series is one match between two teams. Regular-season series are best of 3 games; playoff series are best of 5.
  • The deadline is 15 minutes before the week's first series. Any changes to your team must be saved by then. Home shows the next deadline in your own time.
  • Your points go up live while the games are played. They're confirmed about 3 hours after each series ends, and the league tables move then.
  • In the playoffs, a team might play no series in a week, or several.
Step 1: Build your squad

Pick 9 players on Pick Team, spending no more than ₩100.0m. You need at least one player in each of the five positions, and no more than 2 from one LCK team. You'll be asked to sign up when you save.

A good first squad usually has:

  • Players who start every series for their team.
  • Good players in every position, not just Mids and Bots. Tops and Supports need fewer kills to score, so they can keep up.
  • A team in your coach slot that wins a lot, since winning a series is worth 3 points.
  • Cheaper players on your bench, so more of your money goes on the 7 who score.
  • One or two players who score well week after week, to captain.
Step 2: Pick your starters

Each week you choose 7 starters from your 9 on Pick Team. To swap two players, tap one, then tap one of the players that lights up. Press Save team when you're done; nothing changes until you do.

Your 2 bench players sit in an order you choose. If a starter plays no games that week, your first bench player comes on for him if he fits, then your second.

When picking your starters, ask:

  • Who is sure to start for his team?
  • Whose team has an easy week, or two series instead of one?
  • Is your bench in the right order?
Step 3: Choose your Captain

Your Captain is your biggest call each week, because his points count one and a half times. Tap a starter on Pick Team, press Make Captain, then save.

Pick someone who is very likely to play and who usually scores well. There's no vice-captain, so if your Captain plays no games that week, nobody gets the extra points.

Step 4: Understand how points are scored

Players score in every game of a series, and their best 2 games count. The main ways to score are:

  • Kills and assists
  • Not dying, or dying little
  • Having more gold than the player opposite at 15 minutes
  • Solo kills, first blood, multikills and stealing monsters

Your coach slot scores for what the whole team does: the first tower, killing more dragons and barons than the other team, quick wins, and winning the series. A team can lose points for losing a series.

Step 5: Make transfers to improve your squad

Transfers let you change your squad during the season, on Transfers. Use them to:

  • Replace a player who has lost his place to a stand-in.
  • Bring in players in good form, or whose teams have an easy week.
  • Move money between positions.

You get 1 free transfer a week. Each extra one takes 6 points off that week's score, often called a hit. If you don't use your free transfer, it carries over, and you can save up to 3.

Step 6: Use buffs at the right time

Buffs are boosts for big weeks. You get each one once a split, and can play only one in any week.

  • Remake: as many free transfers as you like, to rebuild your squad for good.
  • Limitless: one week with no budget or team limit, then your old squad comes back.
  • Bench Boost: your bench players score too.
  • Double Captain: a second Captain, so two players score one and a half times.

Play Remake and Limitless on Transfers; they're final once you confirm. Play Bench Boost and Double Captain on Pick Team; you can cancel them until the deadline.

Step 7: Join leagues and take on your friends

You're put in three leagues when you join: Overall, your country, and fans of your favourite LCK team.

To play your friends, go to Leagues and press Create a league, then send them its code or link. If a friend sends you a code, press Join a league on Leagues and enter it.

Your weekly checklist

Before each deadline, check:

  • Have you picked your 7 starters?
  • Have you picked your Captain?
  • Is your bench in the right order?
  • Is any of your players not starting, or is his team not playing?
  • Do you need a transfer?
  • Do you want to play a buff?

Selecting your squad

Squad size
  • Your squad is 9 players: 7 starters and 2 on the bench.
  • The 7 starters are one player in each position (Top, Jungle, Mid, Bot and Support), a Flex, and a coach.
Positions
  • Your squad needs at least one player for each position. Beyond that, your Flex and bench can be anyone.
  • A player counts as the position his team lists him in, even if he plays somewhere else for a game.
Budget
  • Your squad must cost no more than ₩100.0m of game money. In the playoffs you get more.
Players per team
  • You can have no more than 2 from one LCK team, counting the coach. In the playoffs it's 3.

Managing your squad

Choosing your starters
  • Before each deadline, choose your 7 starters on Pick Team. All your points for the week come from them, unless one doesn't play and a bench player comes on.
  • A starter in a position can swap with a bench player of the same position, or with your Flex if the Flex plays that position.
The Flex
  • Your sixth starter can be a player from any position, so a second Mid is fine. The coach can't be your Flex.
The coach 감독
  • Your seventh starter is a whole LCK team, shown by its head coach (감독 is Korean for coach). It scores for what the team does, not for its players' points.
  • If that team changes head coach during the season, your pick stays and shows the new one.
Captain
  • C Each week, pick one of your 6 starting players as Captain. His points count one and a half times. Half points round towards zero, so 9 becomes 13 and −3 becomes −4. The coach can't be Captain.
  • There's no vice-captain. If your Captain plays no games that week, nobody gets the extra points, even if a bench player comes on for him.
  • If you move your Captain to the bench yourself before the deadline, the player who takes his place becomes Captain.
Bench order and automatic substitutions
  • If a starter plays no games that week, a bench player comes on for him once the week is over.
  • Your bench players are tried in the order you set. Each one fills the first gap he fits: his own position first, then the Flex.
  • A bench player who also played no games is skipped.
  • Say your Top laner doesn't play. Your first bench player is a Support and your second a Top laner, so your second one comes on.
  • Nobody comes on for your coach. If that team doesn't play that week, it scores 0.

Transfers

Transfer rules
  • Before a split's first deadline, transfers are free and unlimited. In its second week you have 2 free transfers, then 1 a week.
  • If you don't use a free transfer, it carries over. You can save up to 3.
  • Each transfer beyond your free ones takes 6 points off that week's score.
  • You can swap a player for one in another position, as long as you still have one in every position. A coach can only be swapped for another coach.
  • In the playoffs, transfers are unlimited and never cost points.
Undoing transfers
  • Each time you confirm transfers, you can undo that whole batch until the deadline, latest first. Your old players come back at their old prices, and any free transfers and points it used are given back.
  • Selling a player and buying him back before the deadline costs nothing.
  • Transfers made with Remake or Limitless can't be undone.
Between the splits
  • Once Split 1 ends, transfers are locked until 2 weeks before Split 2's first deadline. Then they're free and unlimited.
  • Any extra money from the Split 1 playoffs is taken back when Split 2 starts, so your bank can go below zero. Your squad must be back within budget, and back to 2 per team, by the deadline.
  • If it isn't, Flol fixes it for you at no points cost, selling your most expensive extra players and buying the cheapest who fit.
Player prices
  • Every player and coach has a price. Prices change once a week, at the deadline.
  • Scoring well lately is what mostly pushes a price up, and scoring badly pulls it down.
  • If lots of people playing Flol buy a player, his price goes up a little; if lots sell, it goes down a little.
  • A player hardly anyone picks slowly gets cheaper. No price drops below a set minimum.
  • When you sell a player, you get his full current price.
  • Say Faker, T1's star Mid, scored 22 in Week 2, more than any other Mid, and lots of people bought him. At the Week 3 deadline his price went up ▲ ₩0.5m, from ₩18.0m to ₩18.5m.

Scoring

How players score
  • Players score in every game of a series, and their best 2 games count. In a 2–0 both games count; in a longer series the weaker ones are dropped.

Live and final points
  • Points go up live while a game is played, for the things the live feed shows: kills, deaths, assists, gold at 15 minutes and more. They're marked Live until confirmed.
  • A series' points are final about 3 hours after it ends, and the league tables move then.
  • If the live feed is down, there are no live points, but final points still arrive.

Leagues

Leagues you're in
  • When you join, you're put in three leagues: Overall, which is everyone; one for the country you picked when you signed up; and one for fans of your favourite LCK team.
  • You can change your favourite team or country until your first week's deadline.
Tables and splits
  • Every league is ranked on total points, with a table for the whole season and one for each split.
  • In Split 2, Overall is cut in two. The top half from Split 1 go into Legend and the rest into Rise, and everyone keeps their points. If you join in Split 2, you start in Rise.
Private leagues
  • Anyone can start a private league and invite friends with a code or link. You can be in up to 25, and there's no limit on how many people join one.
  • When you make a league, choose the week it starts counting from: this week or a later one.
  • The person who made it can rename it, remove someone or delete it. Anyone else can leave.
Ties
  • If two people have the same points, the one who made fewer transfers goes ahead. Transfers made with Remake or Limitless don't count.
Ranks
  • Your place in Overall is shown as a League of Legends rank, from Iron at the bottom to Challenger at the top. Only Overall has ranks.
  • Everyone is Unranked until the first week's points are final. After that, your rank can change whenever a series is confirmed.
  • Iron to Diamond each have four steps, IV up to I, and I is the best, as in League.
