# LEC crests

Flol's badge for each of the ten LEC teams, Pro Evolution Soccer style: the real club at a glance, redrawn as a European football badge. The LEC game is an idea in [docs/to-grill.md](../../../to-grill.md), not specced; these wait for it. Made through [/badges](../../../../.claude/skills/badges/SKILL.md).

**Approved:** Craig, 17 Nov 2026 ("Sure it's fine"), round 6, with G2's ring changed to España.

## How they came

Six rounds. Craig ditched the first two: meme icons that "mean nothing", one invented (a chain for Fnatic). Then a describer wrote each real logo shape by shape ([docs/research/lec-team-logos.md](../../../research/lec-team-logos.md)), two designers drew blind, an esports historian checked every fact, and a fresh-eyes UX designer made them one set, then gave each team its own format (Craig: "all the same shape and format").

## Craig's calls

- G2 is Spanish: nothing about it is German. The LEC's studio is in Berlin, which makes no team German. GIANTX is Málaga.
- SK is purple and white, from its kit; Telekom's magenta is the sponsor's.
- The full team name on every badge.
- No star for Fnatic's 2011 Worlds, out of respect.
