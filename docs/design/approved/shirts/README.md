## Where they show

Craig's rules (16 Nov 2026), held in [spec/13](../../../../spec/13-korean-touches.md#shirts-and-crests):

- A player in your squad on a pitch or lineup gets a shirt: the Rift, the bench, Home's lineup. Never under 24px.
- A team on its own gets a crest: fixtures, results, standings, the Series scorebug, the Teams tab, the coach, a supporter league.
- An empty portrait slot takes the crest until photos exist.
- A row a role icon already leads takes no team mark: Stats, the Transfers list, Live, feeds, history.
- A colour fill only where a crest won't fit, as Live's stream scorebug.

## Rights

Our own drawings: no real kit, logo or sponsor copied. The sponsor words are made up.
