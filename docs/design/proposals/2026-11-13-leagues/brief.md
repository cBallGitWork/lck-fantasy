# Leagues: brief

For the next `/design` round. From the spec only; Craig draws and approves the picture in a watched session.

**Screen:** **Leagues** in the top bar (spec/12); also **All leagues ›** on Home and Points. Two views: the list of my leagues, and one league's table.

**Jobs** (spec/08 unless noted):
1. See every league I'm in, with my rank and its move: Overall, my country, my team, then my private leagues ("The Leagues page").
2. Open a league's table: rank, move, team and manager, gameweek points, total.
3. Switch the table between the season and each split (tabs).
4. In Split 2, see Legend or Rise beside the whole Overall table ("Legend and Rise").
5. Make a private league: name, the gameweek scoring starts from; get its code and link ("Private leagues").
6. Join a private league with a code or a link.
7. As its maker: rename, remove a manager, delete. As a member: leave.
8. Open a manager's team from a row (spec/08; Manager profile, spec/12).
