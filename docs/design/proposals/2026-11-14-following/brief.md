# Following: brief

**The screen.** The Following page. Reached from **Following ›** on Home's Following card
(spec/14, Where it shows; spec/12, The pages). Not in the top bar.

**Jobs**

1. See how the managers I follow are doing this week: each series' points, big Carrys, their
   deadline transfers and buff, each swap's running score (spec/14, The feed).
2. Truck one of them from a feed item: coffee or protest, a preset line that fits their week,
   one truck per manager followed per gameweek (spec/14, Trucks; Truck lines).
3. See the trucks parked on my profile this gameweek, with each sender's name; sponsor trucks too
   (spec/14, Trucks).
4. Accept or decline follow requests (spec/14, Following).
5. See who I follow and unfollow; see my followers and remove or block one (spec/14, Following).

**Craig's calls, 14 Nov 2026 (this round):** a follow request is sent from the manager's profile
only, no search; the feed is one timeline, newest first, split by gameweek; following has a cap,
held as data (drawn as 50). Trucks go on the live gameweek's items, or the previous gameweek's until the next one starts (drawn mid-Week 3, so Week 2 rows have no Truck).
