# Live: brief

**The screen.** The Live page: a stream beside the manager's live points (spec/12, Live). Reached from **Live** in the top bar (its dot shows while a series is live) and **Live ›** on Home's stream (spec/12, Home). In the beta.

**Jobs**

1. Watch a series: the stream embedded, Twitch and YouTube only in the beta (spec/12 Live).
2. Pick the stream from a list: the official LCK broadcasts in each language and approved co-streamers, each with name, language, platform and channel. Streams not live show greyed. It opens on the official English stream, then remembers the last pick (spec/12 Live).
3. Follow your points beside it: live and provisional, only the lines the feed carries; solo kills and steals land at final (spec/11 Live).
4. Nothing live: see the next series and a countdown (spec/12 Live).
5. Feed down: the points panel says so; final points follow (spec/12 Live, spec/11 Final).

**Data:** live points #34; final points, Average, Highest #33; series #36; the streams list is admin data (spec/12), no issue yet; the embed is a placeholder.

**Made-up moment:** the Points picture's: LCK Cup Week 3, Saturday, T1 v DK game 2 live at 14:20, Baron Steal FC on 94 not final, Faker Carry. Every player's points match Points. Nothing live: Sunday morning before KRX v BRO. Feed down: the same moment with no live feed.
