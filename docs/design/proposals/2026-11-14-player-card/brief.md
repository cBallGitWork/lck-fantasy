# Player card: brief

**The screen.** The full player card: what opens when a manager taps a player's name or "Full card ›" anywhere (Pick Team, Transfers, Points, Stats, a league's team). spec/12, The player card (grilled 13 Nov 2026). The short card on Pick Team's right column is already approved; this is the full one, drawn as a sheet over the page that opened it.

**Jobs**

1. Decide on a player: price and its move, form with rank, season with rank, ownership, transfers in and out this week once live (spec/12 Header).
2. Read the admin news line when there is one (spec/12 News).
3. See the last 3 gameweeks' points and the next series (spec/12 Front).
4. Tabs: This week (each series and game, champion, lines, points, the 2 that count), Fixtures, History (each week's points and price, then past seasons), Champions (each champion, games, average points) (spec/12 Tabs).
5. Season stats: the scoring lines only, per game and total (spec/12; spec/05 lines).
6. Act by where it opened: Make Carry, Make Vice-carry, substitute, transfer in or out; Compare in the beta (spec/12 Buttons, Compare).
