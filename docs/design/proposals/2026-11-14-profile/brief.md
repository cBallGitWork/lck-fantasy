# Manager profile: brief

**The screen.** Any manager's public page (spec/12, The pages: "public, as in FPL. Any manager's points, history and transfers open from a league table"). Reached from a league table row, a feed row's name (spec/14) and the Following lists. FPL splits this into the entry's Points view, Entry History and Transfer History, with a manager sidebar on each; Flol keeps them as three tabs of one page.

**Jobs**

1. See their team and points for a gameweek, once its deadline has passed (spec/12; FPL).
2. See their season week by week: points, ranks, transfers, hits, buffs (spec/12, "history"; FPL Entry History).
3. See every transfer they made, by gameweek (spec/12, "transfers"; FPL Transfer History).
4. Ask to follow them, or see that I follow them (spec/14, Following: a request goes from the profile).
5. See the trucks parked on them this gameweek, with senders; sponsor trucks too (spec/14, Trucks).
6. See their hangul banner in their favourite team's colours, if they set one (spec/13, Hangul banner).
