## How to use

Each screen's brief, in `2026-11-16-<screen>/brief.md`, lists every detail no spec line settles, under "Not settled by the spec", with an example and a recommendation from FPL. That makes 73 questions in all. Below, for each screen, are the five that block drawing, ready for one grill batch. The rest can wait until the picture raises them. Craig took every recommendation below on 16 Nov 2026 ("Recs for all"); they are now in spec/12. The rest wait in each brief.

## Perfect game

1. Is it the 7 starter slots with one player as Carry? Recommend: yes.
2. Does the Carry's ×1.5 count in the total? Recommend: yes.
3. Does the budget apply, as the team cap does? Recommend: cap yes, budget no. Note that FPL's Team of the Week ignores both, and has no captain.
4. Does it build live with a Not final tag, or only once the week is final? Recommend: live, with the tag.
5. Where is it reached? Recommend: links on Points and Home.

## Series page

1. A page with its own URL, or a sheet like the player card? Recommend: a page.
2. Game tabs (as on gol.gg), or one grid per team with games as columns (as in the Points drawer)? Recommend: the grid.
3. Which game facts show? Recommend: winner, length and champions only.
4. What shows before the series starts? Recommend: the time (yours and KST), the format, both rosters and Watch ›.
5. Where does it open from? Recommend: every series row, anywhere.

## Stats

1. Players and coaches in one list, or a Players | Coaches switch? Recommend: the switch.
2. Does a line show points or the raw count (+5, or 14 kills)? Recommend: the raw count, with points on the player card.
3. Totals or per game? Recommend: totals, with a per-game toggle.
4. Which filters? Recommend: role, team, max price and search.
5. Which time span? Recommend: season, with the gameweek as an option.

## Compare

1. A sheet over the card, or its own page? Recommend: a sheet.
2. How is the second player picked? Recommend: a search, with the same role listed first.
3. Can any role be compared with any role, and a coach only with a coach? Recommend: yes to both.
4. Is the better number in each row marked, with no overall verdict? Recommend: yes.
5. How many next series show? Recommend: 3, as on the player card.

## Price changes

1. How is it reached, with no slot in the top bar? Recommend: links on Stats and Transfers.
2. Only the last week, or ‹ week › arrows? Recommend: the arrows.
3. Rises and falls side by side, or one list? Recommend: side by side.
4. A rise in Korean red and a fall in blue, or the West's colours? Recommend: Korean red for a rise.
5. What shows before the first move? Recommend: one short line, "No moves yet".

## Settings

1. How is it reached? Recommend: the team name at the right of the top bar.
2. One page, or tabs? Recommend: one page.
3. Do changes save at once, or with a Save button? Recommend: a Save button.
4. What is the cap on the team name and the hangul banner? Recommend: 20, as in FPL.
5. Does deleting an account delete the private leagues it made? Recommend: hand them to the next member.
