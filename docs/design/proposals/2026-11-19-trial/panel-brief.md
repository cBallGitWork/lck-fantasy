# Brief: Pick Team, six pictures

Flol is a free, season-long fantasy game for the LCK, built on FPL's structure. Pick Team is where a
manager sets his starters, bench order and Captain, and plays a buff, before the week's deadline.
Reached from Pick Team in the top bar.

The six pictures, A to F, draw the same screen on the same made-up data. Judge them against each other.

## The moment

Split 1 Week 4, Tuesday evening, 9h 45m before the deadline. The manager has tapped Faker: his card is
open on the right, and the one player he can swap with (Bdd, the bench Mid) is marked; the others are
dimmed. No changes yet, so Save team is off. No buff played.

## Jobs, with their spec lines

1. See the seven starters in their places on the Rift: Top, Jungle, Mid, Bot, Support, a Flex who may
   play any role, and a Coach (spec/01). See who is Captain (spec/04).
2. Swap a starter with a bench player: tap one, the ones he can swap with light up, tap one (spec/12).
3. Make a player Captain from his card (spec/04).
4. Play one buff a week: Bench Boost, Double Captain, Coach Diff (spec/06).
5. Save before the deadline; the deadline in your time and KST (spec/12).
6. Check the week's ten series (spec/12).

## Data shown

Each starter's kit, name and the week's two opponents; the Captain's C; a news flag on Siwoo (not
starting Wed); the bench in order with its roles; the tapped player's price and last move, form, owned,
season points with ranks, last 3 series; the deadline and countdown; the week's series in your time and
KST; sponsor slots (made up).

## The files

Each letter has four shots: `X-1440.png` (the first screen at 1440 × 900, as a manager lands),
`X-1440-full.png` (the whole page), `X-390.png` (the first screen on a phone, 390 × 844) and
`X-390-full.png` (the whole phone page). The Save bar sticks to the foot of the window.

## Three questions, answered for every picture

1. The Rift and the space round it: is the map given too much of the screen, or too much empty ground
   round it? Say which picture spends that space best.
2. The bench: is the space it takes too big for two players? Which picture's bench works best?
3. Buffs: do icons on the buff tiles help you find and play one, or are names enough? Where icons
   appear, can you tell what each means without its name?
