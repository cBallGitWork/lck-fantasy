# LoL esports broadcast graphics

From the video Craig sent on 19 Nov 2026 for how the esports broadcast draws a game: the Worlds 2026
final, GEN v BLG game 4, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=edKda_6TJCg, captured at 16:37. Riot's
graphics, kept to learn from: never copied or shipped.

- `worlds-2026-final-frame-2026-11-19.jpg`: the whole frame. Three graphics sit on the game: the scoreboard bar at the top centre, the lineups strip across the foot with a player camera at each end, and the minimap bottom right. Sponsor and event mark bottom left.
- `worlds-2026-final-scoreboard-2026-11-19.png`: the scoreboard bar. Each team's crest and code at its end, blue side left in blue and red side right in red, with its league and seed under the code (`LPL #2`, `LCK #1`); towers, gold and kills mirrored about the centre; the series score either side of the event mark; the game clock under it; drakes and grubs as small counts on a second row. Square, dark, ruled, heavy condensed capitals for the codes.
- `worlds-2026-final-lineups-2026-11-19.png`: the lineups strip. Five rows, one per role in the fixed order Top, Jungle, Mid, Bot, Support, the two teams mirrored about a centre column that shows each lane's gold difference with an arrow to the side ahead. Each row: champion, level, name, CS in gold, K/D/A, health and mana bars, items, summoner spells.

What Flol can take: the role order as five mirrored rows; the code with league and seed under it; one centre column carrying the comparison; the two sides held to blue and red.
