# Pick Team references: how fantasy games draw the team page

Looked at on 19 Nov 2026. The benchmark for the reskin trial
(`docs/design/proposals/2026-11-19-trial/`), on Craig's word: these sites are the bar.

The FPL and UCL pages were shot signed in, so those shots show an account's own leagues and rank and
are not kept here (Craig, 23 Nov 2026): this repo is public. What each page does is written below;
to see one, open its address signed in. The Bundesliga shot is the league's own published picture.

- **FPL's Pick Team**, at 1440. A green pitch with white lines in slight perspective, on the league's deep purple page; real kits on a faint card, a white name plate, the fixture under it; C and V as small round marks; the bench in a paler band under the pitch with `1. DEF`; chips in one row over the pitch. From https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/my-team
- **UCL Fantasy's My team**, at 1440. A teal pitch tilted hard in perspective on the competition's midnight blue; kits over a bright blue name plate and a dark fixture line; the matchday row over it, the day's matches to the right, Subs and Transfers in a bar stuck to the foot. From https://gaming.uefa.com/en/uclfantasy/my-team
- `bundesliga-app-2026-11-19.jpg`: Bundesliga Fantasy Manager's app shots. A green pitch, kits on white shield-shaped cards, the league's red, black and heavy italic capitals around it. From https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/app/fantasy-manager

- **FPL's Pick Team at phone width (390).** The four chips sit in one row of small square tiles, each an icon, its name and one status line, about 85px tall; the pitch starts on the first screen; the bench is a pale band under the pitch, its shirts on cards headed `1. DEF`. Seen in a 390-wide frame of the same page.
- **UCL Fantasy's My team at 390.** No chips on this screen at all: the matchday row, the team's name and deadline, then the pitch; Subs and Transfers as two big keys stuck to the pitch's foot; the day's matches under it. Seen the same way.

What the three share: the playing surface is drawn as the real thing, in its own colour, and is the biggest object on the page; the league's brand colours sit around it, never on it.
