# Summoner's Rift references

The Rift is drawn from these, never from memory, and is the same drawing in every theme (Craig,
19 Nov 2026: "the rift is sacred"). They are Riot's pictures, kept to draw from: never shipped.

- `summoners-rift-minimap-2026-11-19.jpg`: the game's own minimap. A near-black ground; the lanes and the jungle's paths in one pale khaki, so the walls are the dark left between them; the river winding corner to corner in blue, fading out where it meets mid lane, with a round alcove for each pit (Baron's upper left of centre, Dragon's lower right); a grey-blue stone base in each corner with three arms to the lanes, the red nexus top right and the blue bottom left; the side lanes one rounded band round the edge, with a small loop at the two far corners. Captured 19 Nov 2026 from https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/images/Summoner%27s_Rift_Minimap.png
- `summoners-rift-map-preview-2026-11-19.jpg`: the whole map from above, as the game shows it: the same plan in its real colours, dark forest and teal river, blue light on the bottom-left base and red on the top-right. Captured 19 Nov 2026 from https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/File:Summoner%27s_Rift_Map_Preview.jpg
- `game-minimap-map11-16.23.1.png`: the minimap file the game itself ships, patch 16.23.1, 512 square, straight from Riot's own data server: the first source to draw from (Craig, 19 Nov 2026: look at the game itself). The same plan as the wiki's copy, sharper. Fetched 19 Nov 2026 from https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.23.1/img/map/map11.png
- `minimap-in-play-solo-queue-2026-11-19.png`: the minimap during a real match, from the video Craig sent (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YAbOB8hY2pE, at 5:26). On the plan above it adds what a player sees: each tower a small shield with its plates' count, camps as small diamonds and dots, champion portraits in rings, the camera's white box, the bases lit in each side's colour.
- `minimap-in-play-worlds-final-2026-11-19.png`: the same on the broadcast of the Worlds 2026 final, GEN v BLG game 4 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=edKda_6TJCg, at 16:37): blue side's towers and base bottom left in blue, red side's top right in red, the frame's edge tinted to each side.
- `map-art-in-play-2026-11-19.jpg`: the map itself in play, from the same solo-queue video at 5:26: the art style Craig means (19 Nov 2026: not the minimap alone, "maybe use the art style of the map itself too"). Painted, soft-edged ground in deep greens and brown earth; pale mossy stone for walls and ruins; warm orange light at a brazier; teal water. `../broadcast/worlds-2026-final-frame-2026-11-19.jpg` shows the same style on a lane with a tower.
