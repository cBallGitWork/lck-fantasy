# Privacy

The text of Flol's Privacy page, a plain page linked from Settings and the sign-up sheet
([spec/12](../../spec/12-pages.md), Settings). A first draft for the beta: a lawyer has not read it.
Words in [brackets] are placeholders.

---

**Who we are.** Flol is a free fantasy game for the LCK, run by [owner's name], [country]. Write
to [privacy email] about anything here.

**What we keep.**

- From Google or Discord, when you sign up: your email address and an account id. Never a
  password.
- What you give us: your team name, hangul banner, favourite team and country, and your settings.
- What you do in the game: your squads, transfers, Carry picks, buffs, points, leagues, follows
  and trucks.
- When you agreed to these pages, and which version.

**Why.** To run the game you signed up for: score your team, rank it in leagues and show your
profile. Emails are sent only if you turn them on in Settings, and you can turn them off there
at any time. No marketing, no ads, no selling your data.

**What is public.** Your team name, favourite team, country, banner and points show on league
tables and your profile. Your email never does.

**Who else handles it.**

- Convex: our database and sign-in.
- Vercel: serves the pages.
- Resend: sends the emails you turn on.
- Google or Discord: whichever you sign in with.

They hold the data only to do that work for us.

**Cookies and storage.** Only what the game needs: the sign-in cookie, and in your browser your
guest squad, your theme and the views you last used. No analytics and no tracking, so there is
no cookie banner.

**How long.** Until you delete your account. Delete account in Settings erases everything at
once; our backups clear within 30 days.

**Your rights.**

- See and copy your data: Download my data in Settings.
- Correct it: Settings.
- Delete it: Delete account in Settings.
- Object, or ask anything else: [privacy email].
- Complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that is the ICO.

**Age.** Flol is for people aged 16 and over.

**Changes.** If this page changes in a way that matters, we tell you in the game first.

Last updated: [date].
