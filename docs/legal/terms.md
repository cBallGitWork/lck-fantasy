# Terms

The text of Flol's Terms page, a plain page linked from Settings and the sign-up sheet
([spec/12](../../spec/12-pages.md), Settings). A first draft for the beta: a lawyer has not read it.
Words in [brackets] are placeholders.

---

**The game.** Flol is a free fantasy game for the LCK. There is nothing to pay and no cash
prizes. These terms apply when you sign up; the [Privacy](privacy.md) page says what we do with
your data.

**Not official.** Flol is a fan game. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Riot Games, the
LCK or any team. Team and player names are used to describe the real matches. Flol was created
under Riot Games' "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does
not endorse or sponsor this project. Player photos are the LCK's.

**Who can play.** You must be 16 or over.

**Your team.**

- Team names and banners must not be offensive or pretend to be someone else. We may change or
  remove one that is.
- Your favourite team and country are fixed once your first gameweek starts.

**Points.** Points come from the matches' own data. Live points are provisional, and a series is
final about three hours after it ends. We correct mistakes when we find them, and ranks follow.

**The rules.** Prices, points and buffs are set for each competition and are on the Help page.
We may change them between competitions, and say so before we do.

**The beta.** Split 1 of 2027 is a test. Things may break or change, and the game is offered
as it is, with no promise that it will always be available.

**Ending.** You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We may close an account that
cheats, abuses others or breaks these terms.
