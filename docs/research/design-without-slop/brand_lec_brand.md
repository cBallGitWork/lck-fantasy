## 1. Logo, rebrands and who made them

### Takeaway
The LEC has had one real identity, launched for 2019 by the London agency DesignStudio (merged into a group called Further in Sept 2025), the same agency that rebranded the Premier League. Nothing found shows a new logo since then: not for the 2023 rename to "EMEA Championship", not for 2024 to 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Riot renamed the EU LCS the League of Legends European Championship (LEC) and unveiled the brand in a one-minute sizzle reel on 20 Nov 2018; it took effect with the 2019 season — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)
- DesignStudio had rebranded the Premier League (2016) and was picked after a pitch in Berlin; the brief was something distinct for Europe's community, readable to casual viewers, and sellable to sponsors — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)
- The brief was a "new identity which set it apart from its North American counterpart and push it beyond traditional sport cliches" — [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/designstudio-league-of-legends-rebrand-graphic-design-211118)
- The logo is two competing teams locked together to form one crown and a circular arena; the agency called it "an explosion of triumph and celebration". In motion it rises from the ground and twists to show its depth — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/)
- The proposition was "Unleash Together"; Engadget says it developed into a design language called "clash" — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html); [Further](https://further.group/work/riot-games-europe)
- Further's own case study (same work, the agency's later name) says the LEC had borrowed sports-TV clichés (anchors, suits, desks) and the brand set out to drop them; the team noted "the humour of both fans and shoutcasters". It claims 1.5m unique viewers (+50% year on year) and about 300k concurrent viewers (+40%) for 2018 to 2019. It went on to design each year's finals concept from the host city: Athens 2019 (an "underground rebellion", protest graphics), Malmö 2022 (Midnight Sun), Montpellier (stone columns and a flying crab) — [Further](https://further.group/work/riot-games-europe)
- Further says the work ran about five years and extended to other Riot leagues: LCK, LPL, LLA and VCS — [Further](https://further.group/work/riot-games-europe)
- In Nov 2022 the league became the "League of Legends EMEA Championship", keeping the LEC initials, to widen eligibility as Turkey, CIS and MENA merged in — [Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/league-legends-lec-emea-championships-full-format-change); [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/league-of-legends/riot-announces-new-emea-league-and-structural-changes-to-lec/)

### Inferences
- The crown mark seems to have survived the 2023 rename. I found no reveal of a new mark, and searches for 2024, 2025 and 2026 graphics refreshes found nothing.
- Further worked on the LCK too, so the LCK's 2021 look and the LEC's 2019 look share an agency lineage. That fits the repo's note that press compared the LCK's 2021 look to the LEC's 2019 one. Their sameness is structural; the difference is in the dressing. That is the same split Flol wants: one core, a brand layer per region.

### Gaps
- The 2023 EMEA logo was not read from a primary source, so I cannot confirm it is unchanged.
- No source names who designs the LEC's current broadcast graphics. A 2023 dev-tracker post says the LEC had used Viz Trio for show graphics and built its own in-game HUD system — [devtrackers.gg](https://devtrackers.gg/leagueoflegends/p/7d3aacdd-how-does-the-lec-seem-to-have-broadcast-game-overlay-issues-every-week) (an unofficial mirror; treat it as a lead).

## 2. Colour, type, texture and motion

### Takeaway
The LEC look is high-contrast pairs (orange or magenta against black, teal or turquoise against black), two opposed sans faces (a clean one and Druk, a condensed poster face cut with slashes), angled grids, and five named "energy" textures used as wipes. It is loud, poster-like and European-boxing-bill in feel, the opposite of the LCK's calm "sky" minimalism.

### Cited Findings
- The graphics usually pair two vivid contrasting colours, such as orange and black or white and teal, chosen to suggest the game's red and blue sides — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)
- The image captions show turquoise-to-purple and orange-to-magenta gradients behind a black crown; orange on dark for mobile; turquoise lettering and icons on black for billboards; teal and orange accents in broadcast — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/)
- Two typefaces are set against each other. One is a clean sans; the other is **Druk**, cut by Commercial Type with slashes "as if it has lived through a battle". The pairing is "reminiscent of old European boxing posters" — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/); [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/designstudio-league-of-legends-rebrand-graphic-design-211118)
- The clean face's name is spelt three ways: "Gatica" ([Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/)), "Gattica" ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)) and "Gratica" ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/designstudio-league-of-legends-rebrand-graphic-design-211118)). Not settled.
- There are five textures, named Rush, Surge, Strike, Blaze and Blast after in-game energies. They are used as high-contrast grounds and as broadcast wipes, with a tribal-tattoo look — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)
- The freelancer Daggers for Teeth drew 141 tattoo-flash-style champion illustrations, for the stream and for merchandise — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html); [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/designstudio-league-of-legends-rebrand-graphic-design-211118)
- The broadcast uses an angled grid, dynamic type and data visualisation. The brand covers previews, champion select, replays and analysis, so a viewer joining mid-match knows it is the LEC. Riot got a toolkit to run it in-house — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)

### Inferences
- An LEC brand layer could swap Flol's gold accent for one hot accent (orange) plus one cold (teal) on near-black, and swap the title face for a heavy condensed poster face. Druk itself is a paid Commercial Type font; a free condensed face (for example Anton or Bebas Neue on Google Fonts) gives the same poster weight without copying the LEC's cut.
- The angled grid and textured wipes are the LEC's most recognisable traits. Plate's "Never" list bans gradients and glow. A brand layer could carry one hard diagonal or a single flat texture in banners without breaking it, but gradients would.

### Gaps
- No official hex values were found. The only hexes online (an Issuu student manual) are not Riot's and are left out.
- I could not confirm whether the 2019 palette and fonts are still used unchanged in the 2026 broadcast. That needs a look at 2026 VODs.

## 3. Studio, venue and live events

### Takeaway
The LEC is Berlin-based, arena-first and touring. It plays in the Riot Games Arena with a crowd, adds a broadcast-only Studio Stage from Summer 2026, and runs "Roadtrips" to team cities (Paris area, Madrid) where club fan bases fill arenas.

### Cited Findings
- In 2024 the broadcast moved to the Riot Games Arena in Berlin (previously the LEC Studio) — [esportsadvocate / search summary](https://esportsadvocate.net/2024/01/lg-ultragear-lec-partnership-extended-for-two-years/) (secondary)
- From Summer 2026 the Berlin building has two stages. The ticketed Arena Stage hosts most LEC weeks; the new Studio Stage is "broadcast-first", with no tickets, and the LEC uses it for only one week of the Summer Split. The split lets the LEC and VCT EMEA run at once, and "each league can have its own on-air look" — [rft.gg](https://rft.gg/news/riot-games-unveils-dual-stage-berlin-setup-lec-production)
- The LEC will be played "primarily" at the Riot Games Arena in Berlin in 2027, per Riot EMEA's Maximilian Peter — [rft.gg](https://rft.gg/news/riot-emea-shuts-down-rumors-of-fully-online-lec-in-2027)
- In 2026, LEC Versus (a 12-team invitational in Berlin) replaced the Winter Split; Spring and Summer followed — [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/wiki/tournaments/lec); [Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/de/articles/lol-riot-announces-the-format-for-the-2026-lec-featuring-two-erl-teams-in-the-first-tournament/en)
- 2026 Roadtrips: Karmine Corp in Évry (24 to 26 Apr) and Paris Adidas Arena (24 to 26 Jul); Movistar KOI at the Madrid Arena (8 to 10 May, and early September). Sources disagree on some dates — [lolesports](https://lolesports.com/it-IT/news/2026-lec-spring-roadtrips); [Sortir à Paris](https://www.sortiraparis.com/en/what-to-do-in-paris/gaming/articles/340281-lec-roadtrip-2026-by-karmine-corp-the-esports-event-making-its-return-to-evry-arena); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/lec-spring-2026-roadtrips-schedule-teams-venues-and-ticket-details)
- For the 2019 relaunch, Riot rebuilt the Berlin set with the set designer Misty Buckley (Coldplay) — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html)

### Inferences
- The LCK lives in one fixed home (CHZZK LoL Park, Seoul). The LEC is a travelling, multi-country league whose home games happen in club cities. A Flol LEC layer could show a venue or city tag on a fixture (Berlin / Paris / Madrid) the way football apps show a stadium.

### Gaps
- The 2027 format (three splits from 15 Jan 2027, in the repo's memory) was not re-checked here.

## 4. Personality: cringe, skits, casters

### Takeaway
The LEC's defining trait is self-aware silliness: casters' music videos, rap battles, skits and memes, embraced on purpose since the 2019 rebrand. The LCK reads as serious, prestige sport; the LEC reads as a mate's joke at your expense.

### Cited Findings
- European broadcasts are "anything but" serious: comedy music videos, bizarre memes, and a 10,000-seat finals opening with a Rock, Paper, Scissors fight mocking a rumoured feud between two casters — [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/gaming-features/how-weaponised-cringe-helped-riots-lec-find-its-identity-3310750)
- Trevor "Quickshot" Henry says the silliness started tentatively at the 2019 rebrand, and after fans responded "that got weaponised, and now we have a division of weaponised cringe creatives" — [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/gaming-features/how-weaponised-cringe-helped-riots-lec-find-its-identity-3310750)
- In lockdown the casters made a music video from their bedrooms on phones. Henry describes a "spend your credit" rule for bold bits, such as an 80s music video — [NME](https://www.nme.com/features/gaming-features/how-weaponised-cringe-helped-riots-lec-find-its-identity-3310750)
- Drakos and Vedius have made rap battles (the "Mediocre Rap Battle of LEC"), music videos and comedy skits — [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/esports/how-league-of-legends-casters-became-unexpected-rockstars-1743279/); [NME on Drakos](https://www.nme.com/features/daniel-drakos-explains-how-music-became-the-defining-aspect-of-europes-league-of-legends-scene-3001845)
- Vedius says segments with "immaturity" help the LEC stand out: "Some people like it, some people don't" — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/9091/vedius-shares-why-segments-with-immaturity-are-a-great-way-for-the-lec-to-stand-out-as-a-broadcast)

### Inferences
- For Flol's words layer (the Broadcast voice in `words.ts`), the LEC voice could be cheekier and more British-casterish: puns, deadpan, a wink at a team's collapse. The LCK voice stays crisp, respectful and Korean-terminology-led. Structure and labels stay the same; only empty states, toasts and banners change voice.
- Don't copy named bits or caster catchphrases; they belong to the people and to Riot.

### Gaps
- No 2025 or 2026 source on how the casting line-up or tone has changed (Quickshot, Vedius and Drakos rosters by year were not checked).

## 5. Team brands as culture

### Takeaway
LEC clubs behave like football clubs with ultras, and several are lifestyle and fashion brands. The fan bases (KC's Blue Wall, the G2 Army) are part of the league's look: blue smoke, chants, memes. The crest and kit detail is already in the repo.

### Cited Findings
- Karmine Corp began in March 2020 (as Kameto Corp). Its colour is blue. The "Blue Wall" started when fans set blue walls as profile pictures; the club then made an official version. KC's communications lead compares the ultras to a football "sixth man" — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/14219/when-we-saw-the-movement-we-knew-we-shouldnt-let-it-fade-the-making-of-kcorp-and-the-blue-wall)
- G2's CEO describes the brand voice as a superfan, "excited" before and "devastated" after; it is meme-led and "isn't for everyone". G2 ran a "WHEN DOES G2 PLAY?" campaign — [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/esports/g2-alban-dechelotte-interview/); [Mynewsdesk](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/uk/swipe-right/pressreleases/g2-takes-the-internets-favourite-question-offline-with-when-does-g2-play-3458712)
- G2's 2025 Smiley collaboration was described as "optimistic, irreverent, and proudly self-aware" — [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/g2-and-smiley-tap-into-gen-zs-gaming-fashion-nexus-with-collab/2025071782873)
- 2026 kits are mostly by Fulllife (G2, Fnatic, KC, Vitality, Heretics) and Kappa (Movistar KOI, Shifters): see `docs/research/lec-team-kits.md`, sourced there from [full.life](https://full.life/en/products/2026-main-jersey)

### Inferences
- Club identity matters more in the LEC than in the LCK, where corporate owners (T1/SKT, KT, Hanwha) dominate. A supporter league per club, and club colours on fixture rows, would land harder in Europe. Flol already plans supporter leagues.
- Country is a live identity layer in Europe: Spain (KOI, Heretics, GIANTX), France (KC, Vitality), Germany (SK). A small country code on a team or player row is a plausible LEC-only motif.

### Gaps
- No primary source for Fnatic's, Vitality's or Heretics' brand voice was read this pass.

## 6. European fantasy and sports-app habits

### Takeaway
The LEC already had an official fantasy: LVP's card-based SuperFantasy (2020), closer to FIFA Ultimate Team than to FPL. European football fantasy splits into FPL-style budgets (UK) and market-and-auction games (Kickbase in Germany, MPG in France, Biwenger in Spain).

### Cited Findings
- LEC SuperFantasy (June 2020) was run with LVP (MEDIAPRO). You picked a coach plus five players, one per role, and could change them until five minutes before the day's broadcast. Rarer cards held item slots, and consumable event cards boosted points. You could join up to five lobbies, including one per LEC team. KitKat became the LEC's title sponsor at the same time — [ESTNN](https://estnn.com/league-of-legends-lec-launches-superfantasy)
- Paid card packs are not confirmed. ESTNN does not mention them, and the esports.net guides read on 18 Nov 2026 say only that packs came on game days (by performance) and from daily rewards — [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/superfantasy-lec/); [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/superfantasy-lol/)
- Kickbase (Bundesliga, LaLiga, MLS) is free with a paid membership; its season mode runs on bidding in a transfer market — [Kickbase help](https://kickbase-faq-en.groovehq.com/help/what-is-kickbase-and-how-does-it-work); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id678241305)
- MPG (Mon Petit Gazon), France's leading Ligue 1 fantasy since 2011, was bought by the LFP in 2022. Its "mercato" is a 500M-budget squad market open all season, with no money betting — [Sambadigital](https://sambadigital.com/the-lfp-and-mon-petit-gazon-an-eye-on-the-next-generation); [Konbini](https://www.konbini.com/sports/quelle-est-la-meilleure-strategie-a-adopter-pour-enfin-gagner-une-ligue-mon-petit-gazon/)

### Inferences
- European players will recognise FPL's structure (Flol's core) at once. Football words used across Europe, such as "mercato" and "transfer window", are a cheap way to localise the LEC layer's words. Market-auction mechanics are a different game and stay out of scope unless Craig says otherwise.
- SuperFantasy's collectible boost cards (and paid packs, if it had them) are the thing to avoid. Flol is free with no paid modifiers, which is already its stance.

### Gaps
- Biwenger's and Comunio's mechanics were not read from primary pages. Whether SuperFantasy still runs in 2026 is unconfirmed.

## 7. LEC versus LCK, and what is protected

### Takeaway
LCK means calm, prestige, sky-bright minimalism, Korean terms, one home arena and corporate dynasties. LEC means loud poster type, hot-and-cold colour clashes, angled cuts, self-mocking humour, touring arenas, club ultras and many countries. Both sit on the same Riot game and a shared agency lineage, which is why one core with a brand layer is natural. Riot's and the league's logos and marks stay off Flol.

### Cited Findings
- The LCK 2021 brand had "contrast, minimalism, atmosphere", bright sky colours and a boxy sans — `docs/research/lck-visual-research.md` §1, from Inven Global, Jan 2021
- The LEC brand is "clash": orange or teal against black, boxing-poster type, explosive textures — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html); [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/)
- Riot's "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy says "you may not use any of our logos or trademarks anywhere in your Project". It also prohibits Riot IP "in games and apps", unless the project has a written licence or a valid Riot API key and follows the API terms, and requires a disclaimer line — [Riot Games legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal) (quoted in `docs/research/lck-visual-research.md` §4)

### Inferences
- **Protected, never in Flol:** the LEC crown mark and the "LEC" logotype, the animated crown, the Rush/Surge/Strike/Blaze/Blast textures, Commercial Type's custom-cut Druk, the 141 Daggers for Teeth illustrations, team crests and sponsor marks. The plain words "LEC" and team names, used to describe real fixtures, are a different matter (nominative use), but that is my reading, not legal advice.
- **Brand-layer levers that are Flol's own:** one accent pair (warm/cold) per region; a title face per region (a calm boxy sans for the LCK, a heavy condensed poster sans for the LEC); one motif per region (LCK: hangul titles, red rises and blue falls; LEC: a hard diagonal cut, country codes, a venue tag); a voice per region in `words.ts`; and PES-style made-up crests and kits, already researched.
- **Watch the rise/fall colours:** red for a rise is Korean market convention. European apps use green up, red down, so the LEC layer would likely flip them. This is a per-region data token, not a core rule.

### Gaps
- No source compares the LEC and LCK brands directly. The contrast above is my synthesis from the two sets of sources.

## Checked

Further's own news page and MediaCat say DesignStudio merged into Further in Sept 2025. "No new logo since, including 2023": **unverified**. Nothing found shows one, but no primary source says the mark is unchanged.
2. **Two teams locked into one crown and a circular arena; "Unleash Together"; "clash": confirmed** (Creative Boom, Engadget, Further).

4. **Gatica / Gattica / Gratica and a slashed Druk by Commercial Type, boxing posters, five textures, 141 illustrations: confirmed.** Creative Boom names Gatica, Engadget Gattica, It's Nice That Gratica. The 141 illustrations come from Engadget and It's Nice That, not Creative Boom. "Tattoo-flash" style was not re-checked: **unverified**.
5. **Further also worked on the LCK, LPL, LLA and VCS; finals in Athens 2019, Malmö 2022, Montpellier; +50% unique viewers: confirmed** on Further's page (no year given for Montpellier). No second source found: these are the agency's own claims.
6. **"Weaponised cringe", music videos, skits, Rock, Paper, Scissors at a 10,000-seat final: confirmed** (NME, 15 Sept 2022, read through curl). Rap battles are from Dexerto, not NME.
7. **Berlin through 2027, Studio Stage from Summer 2026, own on-air look, Roadtrips to KC (Évry, Paris area) and KOI (Madrid): confirmed, with fixes.** Riot says "primarily" in Berlin in 2027, and the LEC uses the Studio Stage for only one week of the Summer Split. Body updated.
8. **KC's Blue Wall began with fans' blue profile pictures, ultras, the "sixth man": confirmed** (Inven Global). The "sixth man" line is the interviewer's, which KC's Kotei accepts.
9. **SuperFantasy: coach plus five role players, boost cards, lobbies incl. per team: confirmed** (ESTNN). **Paid packs: unverified.** ESTNN says nothing of them, and the esports.net guides read today do not say so either; only a search summary did. Body updated.

"Prohibits its IP in games and apps": wrong as stated.** The same section allows games and apps with a written licence or a valid Riot API key and API-terms compliance. The conclusion holds for the logo: the LEC crown, textures and custom type stay out of Flol either way, since the logo rule has no API-key exception. Body updated.
