## 1. How Riot layers its brands: umbrella, event, region

### Takeaway
Riot runs a three-layer system that maps almost exactly onto Flol's "one core, a brand layer per region": the game brand (LoL, hextech gold), an esports umbrella (LoL Esports: one blue, two typefaces, a modular grid) that deliberately does not standardise the leagues, and per-league identities (LCK, LEC, LCP, LCS) each with its own logo, type and colours. Worlds itself is built as an evergreen foundation with seasonal elements added on top each year.

### Cited Findings
- LoL Esports was launched as one brand and logo over all twelve pro leagues; visuals "inspired by the aesthetics of Summoner's Rift" — [win.gg, 22 Jul 2020](https://win.gg/news/riot-games-debuts-new-league-of-legends-esports-branding/)
- Riot's own launch post: the brand "will be a tool" for elevating the regional leagues, and "the regional league identities are part of our secret sauce" — each league keeps its own brand. Colour and iconography are pulled "from our sport's legendary arena" — [lolesports.com, Welcome to LoL Esports](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/welcome-to-lol-esports)
- LoL Esports identity refresh by The New Company (San Francisco): logomark kept but simplified and heavier, based on the shape of Summoner's Rift with arrows for movement across it; palette cut to a single blue the studio calls "The Iceborn blue"; type Compacta (primary) and Shapiro (secondary, extended); a modular grid to stay consistent across teams, players and sponsors, with team colours sitting alongside the core palette. The article positions it as avoiding "typical fantasy gaming tropes" — [The Brand Identity, 23 Aug 2021](https://the-brandidentity.com/project/the-new-companys-energetic-identity-system-for-lol-esports-avoids-typical-fantasy-gaming-tropes). Date conflict: win.gg dates the umbrella launch July 2020; The Brand Identity piece is August 2021 (likely the agency write-up of the same or a refreshed system). No hex for Iceborn blue was published.
- Worlds (BUCK, 2025): Riot asked for "less myth, more sport"; BUCK built a system rooted in "honor, precision, and prestige" with an "evergreen foundation" on which "each year, seasonal elements build" (the 2025 seasonal expression is BUCK's), a modular responsive grid across broadcast, stage, social and print, and champion-derived macro textures (a glaive rendered as crystal) — [BUCK, World Championships](https://buck.co/work/riot-games-world-championships)
- MSI 2025 (BUCK): theme "ignite the fire within"; moves away from fantasy visuals toward sports branding, bold kinetic type, adaptable grids, layered graphics, electric forms with hand-drawn gestures; still "unmistakably tied to the brand". No Vancouver-specific motifs described — [BUCK press release](https://buck.prowly.com/414648-a-new-energy-for-league-of-legends-msi-2025)
- Worlds 2022 (Tendril): identity plus an editable toolkit for Riot's in-house teams; colour combinations and densities change by tournament stage so each stage reads at a glance — [Stash Media](https://www.stashmedia.tv/tendril-ramps-up-riot-worlds-2022-with-branding-toolkit/)

### Inferences
- The industry direction since 2020 is the same at every layer: sport first, fantasy second. The esports umbrella, Worlds and MSI all moved away from the game's ornate gold-and-magic look. Flol's current plate (hextech gold `#C89B3C` on navy, from the game client CSS) reads as "the LoL client", not "LoL esports" or "LCK". That is a choice worth putting to Craig: the core can borrow League's familiarity, but the esports brands themselves no longer use client gold as their base.
- Riot's pattern to copy (in structure, not look): core = grid, density, type scale, component shapes, stat formatting; brand layer = logo, display face, two or three colours, one texture/motif, voice. Worlds' "stage colour shifts" are a precedent for Flol varying accent per stage (Cup / Rounds / Playoffs) without touching layout.

### Gaps
- No Riot brand-guide page with esports colour values or usage rules was found; `brand.riotgames.com` redirects to downloads (per existing repo note).
- No public Worlds 2026 key-art or identity write-up found; search only surfaced the True Damage anthem and the "New Dawn" game update teaser for 2027.

## 2. What each region overrides

### Takeaway
Each league owns its logo, display type, colour pairs and a motif; the in-game data vocabulary (scoreboard contents, player panels, gold graph, draft) is common because it is one game, though layouts differ by production and Riot moved its own scoreboard in 2025. LCK = minimal, sky-inspired colour, boxy sans, stats "with no distractions". LEC = crown mark, two-colour clashes, a condensed poster face, tattoo-style textures. LCP = custom multi-script typeface.

### Cited Findings
- Worlds is produced on a world-feed model: one feed curated in Los Angeles, 22 partners in 21 languages; Riot's global events team supplies a kit of "Worlds-branded graphics, music, and videos"; regions change language, casters, stories, post-game interviews and previews; the article does not say whether regions may alter the branded graphics — [Riot Games, Worlds 2022 custom broadcasts](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/riot-esports-delivering-custom-global-broadcasts-worlds-2022)
- LCK 2021: eagle replaced by two stylised isosceles triangles; a "sans serif boxy font" to match; "simplified shapes and bright colors, inspired by the sky and the atmosphere"; minimalist motion graphics giving stats "with no distractions"; players and the game as "the real stars of the show"; LoL Park restyled to match — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/12982/the-new-era-of-lck-is-here-a-visual-journey-into-the-new-broadcast-and-lol-park). (Palette hexes are already in the repo, from Namu.)
- LCK event graphics are made by Korean studios per event, not by a global vendor: 2grey (2021 Summer Finals title), Banana Culture (2024 Spring/Summer Finals AR openings), Super Very More Studio (LCK Cup 2025 opening title) — [Behance, 2grey](https://www.behance.net/gallery/140415625/League-Of-Legends-LCK-2021-Summer-Final-Title); [Banana Culture](https://bananaculture.asia/2024-lck-spring-finals-opening-ceremony-ar); [Marvelous Designer spotlight](https://www.marvelousdesigner.com/learn/userspotlight/post/2aa0a767e6c0464c80b132d6a403f49a)
- LEC 2019 (DesignStudio): crown-derived mark that animates rising from the floor; bold two-colour pairings (orange/black, white/teal) that "nodded to the red and blue teams"; type Gattica (clean sans; Creative Boom and Design Week spell it "Gatica", spelling unverified) and Druk (display, after old European boxing posters, with custom cuts by Commercial Type); five textures named rush, surge, strike, blaze, blast, drawn from abilities and resembling tribal tattoos; 141 champion illustrations in tattoo-flash style by Daggers for Teeth; delivered as a toolkit Riot adapts in-house. Purpose: stop Europe looking like NA's "little brother" and sell the LEC to sponsors as its own product — [Engadget, 18 Jan 2019](https://www.engadget.com/2019-01-18-riot-lec-league-esports-design-rebrand.html); concept "Unleash Together" — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/work/designstudio-and-riot-games-rebrand-of-the-league-of-legends-european-championship/)
- LCP 2025 (Koto Sydney): platform "What We're Made Of"; logo "The Pinnacle", a molten emblem of five players; custom typeface LCP Ignite built for multiple languages; modular gameplay-inspired visual language across digital, broadcast, live; tone "raw, energised, and focused" — [Mediaweek, 21 May 2025](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/koto-sydney-leads-apac-esports-rebrand-for-league-of-legends)
- LCS (Troika with Devlin): package included graphics for each team in that team's colours — [Promax brief](https://oldbrief.promax.org/article/troika-devlin-riot-lcs-broadcast-package)
- Common overlay elements (LHM says "Not every production needs to display all these fields"; it does not say they are the same in every league): top bar (team names/logos, series score, game number, timer, kills, gold or gold difference, towers, objectives, side indicators); side panels (champion portrait, player, role, level, KDA, summoner spells, items, gold, ultimate status); gold-difference-over-time graph; Dragon/Baron timers; a draft (pick/ban) overlay incl. Fearless. Side must not rely on colour alone, since a red team can play blue side — [LHM.gg](https://lhm.gg/blog/what-makes-a-good-lol-broadcast-overlay). Riot's own HUD changed at First Stand 2025: the top scoreboard moved to a vertical block top-left and per-player gold was removed (per search summary of [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/league-of-legends/new-lol-esports-hud-debut-first-stand-2025); page itself 403)

### Inferences
- For Flol, the overlay list is the "core" vocabulary (shared data, not a shared layout), and the league identity is the "skin". That matches Craig's ask directly.
- LEC brand layer cues to note now for later: crown, high-contrast two-colour pairs, condensed poster display type, aggressive texture. LCK brand layer: angular/geometric (triangles), cool sky palette with violet-blue and red, quiet data-first motion. These are opposite temperaments, which is a good test that the core is neutral enough.
- LCP's multi-script custom face is a precedent for picking a core face with Hangul and Latin parity (the plate's IBM Plex Sans KR already does this).

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 LEC identity refresh found; 2025 coverage was team rebrands (KOI, NAVI). Whether the 2019 DesignStudio system is still the live LEC look is **unverified**.
- No written spec of the LCK's 2026 in-game overlay layout or colours (repo notes the same).

## 3. The visual language players read instantly as "League"

### Takeaway
Players recognise League by its data shapes more than its colours: K/D/A written with slashes, CS, gold, role order top-jungle-mid-bot-support, champion square portraits with items in a row, the ten ranked tiers with their winged crests, and blue vs red sides. These are conventions of the game; Flol can use the conventions while drawing its own icons.

### Cited Findings
- Ranked tiers, low to high: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster, Challenger. Iron to Diamond have divisions IV (low) to I (high); Master+ have none and share a ladder. A division is 100 LP. Each tier's theme comes from a winged crest; trivia links tiers to Runeterra regions (e.g. Gold = Shurima, Challenger = Demacia) — [League of Legends Wiki, Rank](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Rank_(League_of_Legends))
- Emerald was added between Platinum and Diamond from 2023 Split 2 — [mp1st](https://mp1st.com/news/league-of-legends-dev-update-new-emerald-ranked-tier-lee-sin-teemo-visual-reworks-slated-for-2024-clash-changes-and-more); crests replaced ranked borders at the start of Season 12 — [AFK Gaming](https://afkgaming.com/esports/news/riot-games-adds-new-profile-borders-in-league-of-legends-pbe)
- Pro stat vocabulary and formats: KDA ratio = (K + A) / D, where 4.0 is solid and 6.0 excellent; KP %; CS/m (solo laners 8-9 at top level); GD@15 written with sign and comma, "+1,800", over +1,000 means the lane is won; CSD15, XPD15; VS/m; DMG/m (ADC 600-750); DMG %; solo kills — [RFT.GG stats guide](https://rft.gg/news/league-of-legends-esports-stats-guide)
- Riot's esports data (LCS, LEC, LCK, MSI, Worlds) is distributed through one official data partner (Bayes Esports, per the search result summary) — [search result: RFT.GG / wecoach listing, unverified primary](https://rft.gg/news/league-of-legends-esports-stats-guide). **Unverified**: GRID is now named for LCK 2026 data in the repo; the current partner should be checked in `docs/research/live-data-sources.md`.

### Inferences
- K/D/A as `3/1/7` with slashes and per-player CS and gold are the most universal League tells; use them in the core, identical for every region.
- Flol already has LoL-tier shields for Overall (spec/08). The tier names are game terms, not logos; the crest art is Riot's. Draw own shields (plate already does: one cream fill).
- Gold shown as `12.3k` is common on broadcast scoreboards but I found no written source for it; RFT uses commas (`+1,800`). Treat the `k` form as **unverified convention**.

### Gaps
- No primary source describing the crest art (materials, colours per tier) was readable; only images.
- No written source for champion-portrait conventions (square, circle on map) or item-row layout beyond the LHM overlay list.

## 4. What is protected: Riot's "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy

### Takeaway
Fan projects get a revocable, non-commercial licence to Riot IP (art, icons, maps, items), must carry a fixed notice, and may not use Riot logos or trademarks at all without a written licence. Apps using Riot IP on the App Store or Google Play need a written licence or a valid API key. This binds Flol's brand layer: no Riot, LoL Esports, LCK or LEC logo, no Beaufort/Spiegel, own icons.

### Cited Findings
- Grant: personal, non-exclusive, revocable licence to use and make derivative works of Riot IP for non-commercial community use; Riot can deny use at any time — [Riot Games Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal)
- Required notice: "[Project] was created under Riot Games' 'Legal Jibber Jabber' policy using assets owned by Riot Games. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor this project." — [Riot Games Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal)
- Riot logos and trademarks may not be used in a project, its site, ads or videos without a written licence; no domains or social accounts using Riot names or trademarks; not as search keywords or tags — [Riot Games Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal)
- Commercial projects (paywall, crowdfunding, run by a company) need a written licence; exceptions: passive ads by individuals, streaming, and API projects on a valid API key under Riot's API terms — [Riot Games Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal)
- Games and apps using Riot IP are barred from the Apple App Store and Google Play without a written licence or valid API key — [Riot Games Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal)
- The policy (as fetched) is dated August 2018 — [Riot Games Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal)

### Inferences
- The "sponsor" slot in Flol's top bar and any company running Flol could push it into "commercial"; worth a legal check before beta (fits the existing note in `fantasy-onboarding-standards.md`).
- A future native app needs Riot's written licence or an API key, which affects the "maybe a native app later" plan.
- Whether LCK/LEC league names and team crests count as "Riot trademarks" under this policy is not spelt out; league marks are Riot's, team crests belong to the teams. Flol's own drawn crests (per `feedback-crests-club-badges`) stay the safe path.

### Gaps
- The policy text does not mention fonts; Beaufort and Spiegel are licensed Monotype cuts, so the bar comes from font licensing, not the policy.
- The policy does not say whether a fantasy game using player names and stats (no Riot art) is "using Riot IP"; needs a direct read or legal advice.
- "New Dawn" (a 2027 game update teased at Worlds 2026, said to modernise the client) may change the client's gold-and-blue look; the article was blocked here, so the effect on "what reads as League" is **unknown**.
