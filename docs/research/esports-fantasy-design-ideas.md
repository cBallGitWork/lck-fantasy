# Other fantasy games show what an LCK game should borrow

The most useful lessons for an FPL-style LCK game come from three places: **HLTV's Counter-Strike fantasy**, which shows how to score short and long series fairly and how to add event-long roles and one-match boosters; **FPL, F1 and World Cup fantasy**, which show how to name, limit and reset chips; and **Riot's own CBLOL relaunch and Valorant's Pick'Ems**, which show that cosmetic missions, achievements and fan-base factions keep people playing. Outside Counter-Strike, the research found **no esports game with a coach slot or FPL-style named chips**, and **no major salary-cap game in any sport that pays points for achievements**. So three of the user's decisions (the coach slot, the themed chips and the points-bearing achievements book) are new territory: they set the game apart, but there is nothing proven to copy. The ideas most worth stealing are these: name each chip after what it does (the F1 and Dota lesson); add a **"No Negative" insurance chip** (F1, and HLTV's "Chicken Out"); score the coach slot like FPL's **Assistant Manager** (a win, maps won and an upset bonus); give **sweep and "Invincible" bonuses** on the team side (Rocket League); add a **"2nd Chance" table that starts at Split 2** (Bundesliga); run **streamer-led factions and a fan-base contest** (Valorant, CBLOL); and treat the playoffs as **a separate stage game with "padding" for byes** (HLTV). The ideas to avoid are paid or collectible modifiers that replace base scoring (Dota, Valve), boosters that rarely trigger, and novelty chips that last a single season. **A caveat on method: this session's network blocked most primary sources (Liquipedia, HLTV, esports.gg, BLAST, the Premier League site, lolesports and others) and all of Reddit, so every finding below comes from search-result snippets rather than full pages.** Treat each exact number as something to confirm before it goes into the game. Everything marked "Suggestion (inference)" is design reasoning, not a sourced fact.

**Contents:** method and limits · chips and their naming · split resets · flex, coach and team slots · roles and boosters · achievements · short versus long series · pricing in won · league types · playoffs and the LCK Cup beta · ideas to steal and to avoid · conclusion.

## Chips: keep four, and name each after what it does

FPL is the template the user has already chosen, and its chip rules settled in 2025/26. Each manager gets **two sets of the four chips** (Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost, Triple Captain). The first set must be used before the GW19 deadline and does not carry over; the second opens from GW20 ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4362027)). The rules are **unchanged for 2026/27**, which suggests the Premier League judged the split a success ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679879/whats-happening-with-fpl-chips-in-202627)). FPL's one experiment, the **Assistant Manager "Mystery Chip"** of 2024/25, lasted one season and was then dropped ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/07/18/fpl-2025-26-double-chips-as-assistant-manager-gets-the-boot)).

Other games use more chips, with more personality. **F1 Fantasy has six, each usable once a season and only one per race weekend** ([RotoBaller](https://www.rotoballer.com/how-to-play-f1-fantasy-formula-1-overview-guide-tips-rules/1552086)):

| F1 chip | Effect |
|---|---|
| Extra DRS Boost | One driver scores 3× (the normal weekly boost is 2×) |
| Autopilot | Moves the 2× boost to the team's top scorer after the event |
| No Negative | The team cannot score below zero that weekend |
| Limitless | Unlimited transfers with no budget for one weekend, then the team reverts |
| Wildcard | Unlimited transfers within the budget, kept afterwards |
| Final Fix | One swap after qualifying, before the race |

Sources: [Motorsport Magazine](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/f1-fantasy-chips-what-they-are-and-when-to-use-them/); [FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/final-fix-chip-explained).

The **2026 World Cup game has five boosters**: Wildcard, **12th Man** (one extra player for a round, outside the budget and nation caps), **Maximum Captain** (the highest-scoring starter gets the captain's points), a Qualification Booster, and a **Mystery Booster** revealed before the Round of 32 ([RotoWire](https://apps.rotowire.com/soccer/article/2026-world-cup-fantasy-booster-guide-best-chip-strategy-116926); [ChaseYourSport](https://www.chaseyoursport.com/fantasy-football/fifa-world-cup-fantasy-2026-boosters-guide/12273)). IPL fantasy names its chips after the competition's identity: **"Indian Warrior"** doubles Indian players' points and **"Foreign Stars"** doubles overseas players' ([IPL Fantasy Faceoff](https://iplfantasyfaceoff.vercel.app/posts/beyond-the-basics)). UEFA's Champions League game **bans chips in weeks when transfers are already unlimited** (MD1 and the first legs of the knockout rounds) ([RotoBaller](https://www.rotoballer.com/uefa-champions-league-fantasy-overview-guide-tips-chips-and-rules-2025-26/1679019)).

Esports adds a naming lesson. Dota's War Banner titles work because the name hints at the bonus, and a guide singles out the "Octopus" title for its near-guaranteed multiplier ([egamersworld](https://egamersworld.com/blog/dota-2-ti-2025-compendium-fantasy-guide-ZKV8AzF9_I)). HLTV names its boosters with scene puns: **"Chicken Out"** (after the chickens on CS maps) guarantees a player 0 points for a match, which works as insurance ([HLTV on X](https://x.com/HLTVorg/status/1835721206516858959)), and **"Skin in the game"** doubles a player's team-result points to +6 for a win and −3 for a loss ([HLTV on X](https://x.com/HLTVorg/status/1924430073156747601)).

**Suggestion (inference).** Keep FPL's four chips per split, and give each an LCK name that tells a newcomer what it does. "**Remake**" fits the Wildcard: in League a remake restarts a game, and the chip rebuilds a squad for good. "**Stand-in**" fits the Free Hit: a one-week squad that reverts, like a substitute. "**Full Five**", or "Sixth Man" after the LCK's sixth-man culture, fits the Bench Boost. "**Baron Buff**" fits the Triple Captain, the way F1's "Extra DRS" names a stronger version of the weekly boost. Faker's embraced nickname, "Unkillable Demon King", is a natural name for an optional fifth chip copied from F1's **No Negative** ("**Unkillable**": no player can score below zero that week). Because the user's scoring is kept low and deaths take points away, that insurance chip has real value. Avoid naming chips after sponsors or team owners, as the earlier research found. Copy UEFA's blackout and forbid chips in the first gameweek of each split, when every squad is fresh.

## Split resets: copy FPL's use-or-lose deadline and add a second-chance table

FPL's half-season reset is the only proven model, and it creates a moment of mass activity. Even under the older single-set rules, **427,000 managers played their first Wildcard in GW19 of 2024/25**, part of a record 5.4 million ([AllAboutFPL](https://allaboutfpl.com/2026/07/number-of-people-who-played-fpl-each-season-how-many-play-fpl/)). Bundesliga Fantasy resets the standings side as well: an automatic **"2nd Chance" league starts after the winter break** ([Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/de/bundesliga/news/offizieller-fantasy-manager-saison-2025-26-regeln-punkte-32650)). Call of Duty's Pick'em **resets its leaderboard every stage**, which gave five chances to win in 2021 ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/news/call-of-duty-league-pickem-is-live-for-the-2021-season)). Riot's LTA Fantasy let each league choose **continuous or limited scoring** ([lolesports](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)).

**Suggestion (inference).** Reset chips at the Split 1 / Split 2 boundary, with no carry-over, and send a "use it or lose it" reminder in the last week of Split 1. Run three tables in parallel: the season table, a Split 1 table, and an automatic **Split 2 "second chance" table**, so a manager who started late or had a bad first split still has something to play for. The LCK's long mid-season break (MSI and EWC) is the natural place for one free squad rebuild that does not use up the Split 2 Remake.

## Flex, coach and team slots: score the coach on results, not on players

Slots that are not individual players are established outside esports. **F1 fields 5 drivers and 2 constructors under a $100M cap**. A constructor scores its two drivers' combined points plus constructor-only items such as pit-stop time bands ([RotoBaller](https://www.rotoballer.com/how-to-play-f1-fantasy-formula-1-overview-guide-tips-rules/1552086)). FPL's Assistant Manager scored **win 6, draw 3, 1 per goal, 2 for a clean sheet, plus a "table bonus" for beating a side at least five places higher** ([Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/articles/mystery-chip-fpl-revealed-assistant-manager)). Two esports games come closest to the user's design. BLAST's Rocket League fantasy adds **a team slot that scores 20 per game won, +20 for a sweep and +100 "Invincible" for a 3–0 stage**, alongside three players and a captain ([BLAST.tv](https://blast.tv/rl/news/how-to-play-rl-fantasy)). CBLOL's 2026 game added a **coach slot scored as the average of the coach's team** ([Pichau Arena](https://www.pichauarena.com.br/lol/fantasy-lol-o-que-mudou-cblol/)). DraftKings' LoL team slot pays turret +1, dragon +2, Baron +3, first blood +2, win +2, and **+2 more for a win under 30 minutes** ([FanSided](https://fansided.com/2019/05/12/draftkings-league-legends-introduction/)).

For the flex slot, the closest sourced precedent is the World Cup's **12th Man**, a temporary extra slot outside the caps ([ChaseYourSport](https://www.chaseyoursport.com/fantasy-football/fifa-world-cup-fantasy-2026-boosters-guide/12273)). Fantasy Bundesliga's **"Top 11"** counts only the best scorers that fit the formation, which works like an automatic flexible bench ([Bulinews](https://bulinews.com/the-new-bundesliga-fantasy-everything-you-need-know)). The standard NFL "FLEX" slot could not be sourced this session.

**Suggestion (inference).** Score the coach like FPL's Assistant Manager rather than CBLOL's team average. An average makes the coach a sixth copy of players the manager may already own, and it feeds the one-team stacking problem the earlier research found with Gen.G. A results-based coach should pay for a series win, each map won, a **sweep bonus**, a **fast-win bonus** (matching the user's toggle) and an **upset bonus** for beating a team placed several spots higher. That last bonus makes a cheap coach from a mid-table team a real decision. Count the coach toward the per-team cap. The flex slot will drift to whichever role scores most, so it doubles as a check on role balance: if almost everyone puts an ADC in the flex during the LCK Cup beta, the role weights need tuning.

## Roles and boosters: HLTV's event-long bets are the best esports idea

HLTV Fantasy, free for years, has **no captain**. It uses two other levers instead ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/34907/fantasy-tips-and-tricks-for-iem-rio)):

- **A role per player, set for the whole event.** Depending on how well the player fits it, the role pays **+5, +2 or −2**. The 2022 roles were Leader, Support, Main AWP, Entry Fragger, Defender, HS Machine and Multifragger.
- **About 17 boosters, each usable once for one match.** A booster pays **+5 if it triggers and costs nothing if it fails** ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/39764/new-fantasy-booster-starts-with-epl-groups-a-and-b)).

The triggers are concrete. **Leader** needs a series win without dropping a map ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/36599/hltv-fantasy-updated-for-fall-season-notifications-roles-boosters-and-more)), and **Main AWP** needs 0.35 AWP kills per round. Forum users complain that rule changes in the game itself made the Main AWP target hard to reach ([HLTV forum](https://www.hltv.org/forums/threads/2824765/are-awpers-viable-in-fantasy-anymore)). HLTV removed the "Choke" booster because it rarely triggered and depended on a team result ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/36599/hltv-fantasy-updated-for-fall-season-notifications-roles-boosters-and-more)).

Valve's old CS:GO fantasy game (2015–16) used themed roles too: **Clutch King** (+4 per kill as the last surviving teammate), **Eco Warrior** (+2 per pistol kill) and **Commando** (a bonus equal to the kill-death difference) ([Valve](https://www.counter-strike.net/pickem/cluj2015)). Dota goes furthest. Each slot carries an upgradable War Banner, and its emblems boost single stats by **10% to 150%** ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/dota-2/the-international-2024-fantasy-guide-for-newbies/)). But points come only from the stats on the banner, so "having a bad stat for a player is game-breaking" ([Dotabuff](https://www.dotabuff.com/blog/2025-08-26-the-international-fantasy-guide)).

**Suggestion (inference).** The user's design already has a captain and chips, so a full HLTV booster pool would be one system too many. HLTV's role idea, though, fits the user's "scoring by position" decision in a lighter form: one optional **role tag per split** that the manager picks for a player, with a +2 / +1 / −1 band, scaled down to suit low scoring. Possible tags are "Shotcaller" (team wins 2–0), "Lane Kingdom" (gold lead at 15 above a threshold) and "Vision Lord" (top vision score in the match). HLTV's experience gives two rules for any trigger the game adds: trigger it often enough to matter, and avoid tags that depend mainly on the team's result. Keep everything as a bonus on top of base scoring, never a replacement for it.

## Achievements: no game pays points for them, so cap them hard

No major official salary-cap game was found to award points for badges or achievements. What exists is cosmetic. CBLOL's 2026 relaunch added **missions that unlock profile titles and icons, plus achievements and a competition between fan bases** ([CBLOL on X](https://x.com/CBLOL/status/2011166468579467604)). Valve's Major Pick'Em grades coins as **Bronze, Silver (4–7 correct), Gold (8–10) and Diamond (all 11)** ([BLAST.tv](https://blast.tv/major/pickems)), and at Budapest 2025 **only 0.03% of entries won Diamond** ([escorenews](https://escorenews.com/en/csgo/news/74432-only-0-03-of-pick-ems-won-diamond-coin-at-budapest-major-2025)). Valorant's Pick'Ems hand out a spray for any pick, a gun buddy for the top 50%, a golden buddy for the top 20% and a **"100%" title for a perfect bracket** ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/valorant/valorant-champions-2025-pickems/)). Dota pays rewards to the **top 10%, 25% and 50% each day** ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/dota-2/a-complete-guide-to-fantasy-dota/)). HLTV turns repeat success into news: rickastley123's **record third top-10 season finish** got its own headline ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/44988/paker-games-wins-fantasy-spring-2026-rickastley123-makes-third-top-10-placement)). Rocket League's named team bonuses, such as "Invincible", show how a milestone becomes a story.

**Suggestion (inference).** The user's "Ryu sad face" model, where your player wins a solo 1v1, is a player-event achievement. It sits well beside two other kinds:

| Kind | What triggers it | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Player event | Something your player does in a game | A solo kill ("Ryu sad face"), a pentakill, a Baron steal, a deathless series |
| Manager decision | A choice you made | Captaining that week's top scorer, a Stand-in week that beats your normal squad, owning a cheap player before his price rises |
| Collection and history | A long-run record | Every LCK team owned at some point, three splits in a row in the top 10%, a Telecom War week with players from both T1 and KT |

Borrow Valve's tiers so a few entries are almost impossible, and Valorant's percentile badges so ordinary players still earn something. Because no other game pays points for achievements, the user's idea is untested, so cap the effect: at most about **3 achievement points a gameweek**, shown in their own column, and a tiebreak rule that ranks base points first. That way a meme can never decide a league. Two practical checks are needed before launch. Solo kills must be derivable from the data source (the earlier research found the free live feed lacks some stats, so solo-kill achievements may need to be settled after the match). And meme achievements should name the moment in text, not use a real player's photo or likeness, keeping to the earlier advice to celebrate people rather than mock them.

## Short versus long series: halve, average or bonus, never just sum

Counter-Strike faces the same problem with Bo1s and Bo3s, and HLTV answers it with a **0.5× rating multiplier for Bo1 matches**. HLTV also notes that a Bo3 gives two or three maps in which to trigger a booster, so boosters are harder to hit in a Bo1 ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/41435/europe-mrq-season-fantasy-live-with-padding-adjustment)). Riot's LCS Fantasy **averages each player's points across the games played that weekend** ([LCS Fantasy rules](https://lcsofficial.gg/en/rules)). DraftKings Pick6 counts **"Guaranteed Maps only"**, meaning maps 1–2 of a Bo3 ([DraftKings Pick6](https://pick6.draftkings.com/pick6-rules-and-scoring-league-of-legends)). Two games reward a quick result directly: Rocket League's **+20 sweep bonus** ([BLAST.tv](https://blast.tv/rl/news/how-to-play-rl-fantasy)) and HLTV's **Leader role, which needs a win without dropping a map** ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/36599/hltv-fantasy-updated-for-fall-season-notifications-roles-boosters-and-more)).

**Suggestion (inference).** The user's aim is to avoid rewarding long games. Score each game, average a player's points within each series, sum across a week's series, and pay sweep and fast-win bonuses on the coach and team side. Averaging stops a 2–1 loss from outscoring a 2–0 win, and the sweep bonus makes the 2–0 the better week. The user's "1 point per 3 kills+assists" toggle already blunts kill-heavy long games. Check it against the 2026 LCK data, though, because a single 40-minute game still produces more kills and assists than a 25-minute one. If the gap is too large, a per-minute rate is the fallback.

## Pricing in won: a fixed scale, one top-up and stars at about a fifth of the budget

HLTV gives **$1,000,000 for five players**, at most two from any team ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/37975/fantasy-spring-season-2024-set-to-start-with-10000-in-prizes)). Stars cost **$200,000–240,000**: donk was $240,000 and still on about a third of squads ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/41644/donk-the-most-picked-fantasy-player-for-astana-groups-despite-240k-price-tag)). That forces one or two premium picks plus value picks, close to FPL's balance. HLTV sets prices per event to reflect the strength of the field, and users have complained that most players are too expensive for many combinations to work. The OWCS fan game uses **a 100-point budget, fixed prices for the whole event and a +6 budget top-up when the mid-event transfer window opens** ([owtv.gg](https://owtv.gg/news/overwatch-fantasy-rules-and-format)). Rocket League drops the budget altogether: managers pick one player from each of three tiers, and lower tiers carry bigger multipliers (**1.2×, 1.6× and 2.0×**) ([BLAST.tv](https://blast.tv/rl/news/how-to-play-rl-fantasy)). Riot's LTA game used **a 50-gold budget with prices that changed weekly on performance** ([Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2025/04/lta-fantasy-sign-up-rules)).

**Suggestion (inference).** No source covered Korean-style display, so this part is entirely design. Show prices in **억 (100 million won)** units, for example a ₩100억 budget with stars at about ₩20–24억, mirroring HLTV's ratio, and a one-line explainer for overseas users ("1억 = 100 million won; game money only"). Do not convert won to any real currency, and do not echo real player salaries; both would blur the no-cash line. With about 50 starters, demand-driven price moves are noisy, so weekly performance-based moves in small steps (the LTA approach), plus one OWCS-style budget top-up at the split break, are simpler than FPL's transfer-driven model. Watch HLTV's complaint during the beta: if only a few squads are affordable, flatten the price curve.

## League types: automatic leagues, factions and a fan-base contest

FPL enters every team automatically into **Overall, a country league and a favourite-club league**, adds invitational classic and head-to-head leagues by code, and runs an **Overall Cup knockout from GW15 to a final in GW38** ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2174934); [Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/articles/what-is-the-fantasy-premier-league-cup)). Its cup ties break on goals scored, then goals conceded, then a coin toss. Valorant's Champions 2025 Pick'Ems put fans into **factions hosted by creators and ex-pros** such as TenZ and Tarik ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/valorant/valorant-champions-2025-all-factions/)). HLTV runs a **celebrity leaderboard of pros and casters** with its own small prize pool ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/45198/blast-bounty-season-2-finals-kicks-off-10000-fantasy-fall-season)). Rocket League **enters every locked squad into a Global and an Event league automatically** ([BLAST.tv](https://blast.tv/rl/news/how-to-play-rl-fantasy)). Scale stays modest without a publisher behind it: OWCS stages draw **about 1,300 to 2,000 entrants** ([owtv.gg](https://owtv.gg/fantasy/owcs-emea-na-stage-3-2026/leaderboard)), and early Fantasy RLCS reached **2,609 teams** ([rocket-league.com](https://www.rocket-league.com/news/fantasy-rlcs-the-numbers)).

**Suggestion (inference).** The user's global and team-supporter leagues should be automatic on sign-up, alongside a **country league** to suit a worldwide audience. Add the CBLOL idea on top: a **supporters' table that ranks the ten fan bases** by their members' average score, so a T1 fan cares how Gen.G fans are doing. A creators' or casters' table, like HLTV's, gives English-language streamers a reason to promote the game. With no publisher distribution, they are the realistic route to reach. Private leagues should offer classic and head-to-head play and a "this split only" option, as LTA did.

## Playoffs and the LCK Cup beta: run knockouts as stage games

HLTV avoids messy knockout weeks by **running each stage as its own game, re-picked from scratch**. Seeded teams that skip a round get **"padding" points** (+6 for skipping a quarter-final in one 2026 example), eliminated teams earn elimination points, and the biggest stages count about **50% more toward the season table** ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/45436/packed-six-team-blast-playoffs-and-three-more-partner-fantasy-games-open-to-play); [HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/38555/elimination-stage-fantasy-goes-live-with-16-teams-and-increased-points)). Rocket League also asks for a new pick for each stage ([BLAST.tv](https://blast.tv/rl/news/how-to-play-rl-fantasy)). Football games keep one squad but loosen the rules: UEFA **raises the per-club cap through the knockouts, up to 8 for the final** ([RotoBaller](https://www.rotoballer.com/uefa-champions-league-fantasy-overview-guide-tips-chips-and-rules-2025-26/1679019)), and the World Cup holds its Mystery Booster back for the Round of 32.

**Suggestion (inference).** For the undecided playoffs, the HLTV model fits best. Close the main season at the end of the regular rounds, then open a separate **"LCK Playoffs" game** with a fresh squad and budget, padding for teams with byes, a looser team cap as teams drop out, and its own table. Its points can count for a weighted share of the season table, or for nothing but its own badge, which is the choice the user still has to make. This keeps the blank weeks of a knockout bracket from punishing managers in the main game. The LCK Cup beta can use the same stage-game shell, which gives it a trial run before Split 1. Keep the Cup separate from the season table, because rosters and prices will still be settling.

## Ideas to steal, and ideas to avoid

| Steal | From | Why it fits (inference) |
|---|---|---|
| Name each chip after what it does; "Unkillable" as a No Negative chip | F1, Dota, HLTV | New players learn chips faster; insurance suits low scoring with deaths |
| Coach scored on results, with upset, sweep and fast-win bonuses | FPL Assistant Manager, Rocket League | Makes the coach a separate bet and discourages one-team stacks |
| Split 2 "second chance" table and a use-or-lose chip deadline | Bundesliga, FPL | A natural activity spike; re-engages managers who fell behind |
| Average within a series, sweep bonus on the team side | Riot LCS, Rocket League, HLTV | Stops long series from paying more |
| Tiered and percentile cosmetic badges, a few near-impossible | Valve, Valorant, Dota | Something to earn for everyone, bragging rights at the top |
| Fan-base contest and creator factions | CBLOL, Valorant, HLTV | Gives fans a team to play for and streamers a reason to promote it |
| Playoffs as a stage game with padding | HLTV | Removes knockout blanks from the main game |
| One budget top-up at the split break | OWCS | A cheap way to allow upgrades without busy pricing |

The ideas to avoid are just as clear. **Modifiers that replace base scoring**, such as Dota's emblem-only points, make one bad roll ruinous. **Collectible or paid gating**, such as Valve's sticker ownership and paid Viewer Pass ([Valve](https://www.counter-strike.net/pickem/columbus2016)), conflicts with a free game. **Boosters that rarely trigger or that hinge on the team's result** were removed by HLTV itself. **Chips that last one season**, such as Assistant Manager, cost design time and leave no lasting system. **Six or more chips** (F1) crowd a game that already has a flex slot, a coach and achievements. **Sponsor-branded prizes** such as HLTV's skin-site boosters run against the no-cash, no-sponsor-echo stance. Finally, **re-picking from scratch every stage** suits the playoffs but should not replace FPL-style transfers in the regular season.

## Conclusion

The research changes how the user's riskiest decisions look. Points-bearing achievements and a coach slot are not mistakes that other games made and abandoned; nobody has tried them in esports. The precedents nearest to each (Assistant Manager's result scoring, CBLOL's cosmetic achievements, Valve's tiered rarity) suggest building both as small, capped bonuses whose weight can be raised later, not designs that can only be trimmed after they distort a league. The LCK Cup beta should be used to measure exactly that: how many points achievements and the coach add per week, which role fills the flex slot, and whether fan bases rally around a supporters' table.

The deeper lesson from Counter-Strike is that the hardest scoring problem, uneven series and knockout blanks, is best solved by structure, not by tuning the points table: average within a series, pay for a sweep, and run the playoffs as their own game. Those are cheap rules to write now and costly to retrofit once a season's league data is saved. Every number here still needs checking against the primary pages this session could not open.
