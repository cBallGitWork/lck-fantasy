# Esports fantasy and fan games: page and screen inventory

## Contents
- Top navigation and page grouping
- How per-series and per-game points are shown
- What's unusual (role locks, team slots, coach, draft leagues, pick'em brackets)
- App-only features
- Korean LCK fan games

Research run 13 Nov 2026. Several primary sites refused automated fetches (ltafantasy.com/en/rules, hltv.org/fantasy/rules, sheepesports.com, esportsinsider.com, dotesports.com all returned HTTP 403), so page-level detail is thinner than wanted. No screenshots were reachable. Everything below is cited; what could not be confirmed is in Gaps.

## Top navigation and page grouping

### Takeaway
Riot's current official LoL fantasy (LTA Fantasy, 2025, at ltafantasy.com, not lolesports.com) is the closest live analogue to FPL, and its named pages are few: Home (with weekly Dream Team and top scores), Market (pick and sell players), My Team, Leagues, Rules, plus a Favourite Team setting. HLTV Fantasy, VCT Pick'Em and Korean LCK fan games are event or match prediction products with even smaller page sets. No official LCK, LEC or CBLOL season-long fantasy was found.

### Cited Findings
**Riot LTA Fantasy (live 2025; LTA North and South only)**
- Launched as a beta for LTA Split 2 2025; described by Riot as a free manager-style game where you build a team of LTA pros and score from their weekend matches — [Riot support: LTA Fantasy FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq); [Sheep Esports guide, 8 Apr 2025 (search snippet)](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/lol-a-guide-to-lta-fantasy/en)
- Onboarding: sign in with a Riot ID, then create a team with a name, logo and tricode — [Sheep Esports guide (search snippet)](https://www.sheepesports.com/us/articles/lol-a-guide-to-lta-fantasy/en)
- Budget of 50 gold per week; any LTA North or South player; prices change each weekend on performance — [Sheep Esports guide (search snippet)](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/lol-a-guide-to-lta-fantasy/en); [Esports Insider (search snippet)](https://esportsinsider.com/2025/04/lta-fantasy-sign-up-rules)
- Pages named in the FAQ: the game site, a Rules page (ltafantasy.com/en/rules), a "My Team" tab (rename team), Leagues, and the Market. No captain, no settings page and no standalone app are mentioned — [Riot support: LTA Fantasy FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq)
- Leagues: up to 13 per account; renaming yes; at beta, no delete, no kick, no leave; joining late means earlier weeks don't count; a new league counts from its creation date — [Riot support: LTA Fantasy FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq)
- League types reported: general leaderboard, friends' leagues, a streamers' league; one roster per account in beta — [Sheep Esports guide (search snippet)](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/lol-a-guide-to-lta-fantasy/en)
- Prizes: top 100 in each conference league at the end of Split 2 got a Spirit Blossom skin (Yone North, Yasuo South); players in both conferences get one reward based on their favourite team — [Riot support: LTA Fantasy FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq)
- Split 3 (23 Jul 2025) UI changes: a "Sell All Players" button, a Favourite Team change once per stage, a revised Market player view, and the weekly Dream Team and top scores on the Home page. Leagues gained a description, renaming, deletion, leaving, and a choice of continuous or limited scoring — [lolesports.com: LTA Fantasy Split 3, what's new](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)
- The lolesports.com fantasy news hub lists only the LTA Split 3 article; it references no CBLOL, LEC or LCK fantasy — [lolesports.com/news/fantasy](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)

**Fantasy LCS (Riot, 2014–2018; archived, shut down Dec 2018)**
- Beta launched summer 2014; leagues had to finish their draft before the Summer Split began — [GameSkinny (search snippet)](https://www.gameskinny.com/news/league-of-legends-to-offer-official-fantasy-lcs-beta/)
- Riot's FAQ: only regular-season matches counted, not playoffs; you could rename your team but not your league — [Riot support: Fantasy LCS FAQ (search snippet)](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/fantasy-lcs-faq)
- Shut down for Spring 2019; Riot said the user base had fallen over 90% from year one, and it had not invested in it for two years — [win.gg](https://win.gg/news/1331/fantasy-lcs-and-lec-postponed-beyond-the-2019-summer-split); [Upcomer](https://upcomer.com/riot-games-changing-fantasy-lcs); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/riot-games-plans-to-have-something-in-place-for-fantasy-lcs-for-spring-2020)

**E-GO (Sweden; LoL fantasy launched 2024)**
- First LoL fantasy season was the LEC Winter Split 2024, built on GRID data, free, prize pool at least $500 — [Esports Legal News, 18 Jan 2024](https://esportslegal.news/2024/01/18/e-go-app-partners-with-grid-to-elevate-league-of-legends-fantasy-esports-experience/)
- E-GO runs short stage-based tournaments rather than full seasons — [Mobafire tournament page (search snippet)](https://Mobafire.Com/lol-fantasy-tournament)
- KBOOM (not E-GO) offers a free fantasy league across LEC, LCS, LCK and LPL — [Sheep Esports (search snippet)](https://www.sheepesports.com/de/articles/kboom-brings-fantasy-league-to-league-of-legends-tier-1/en)

**HLTV Fantasy (CS2; live)**
- Five players, $1,000,000 budget, at most two from one team; each player assigned a role (Multi Fragger, AWPer, Support, Leader); boosters such as Clutch King, Multi-Kill Expert — [pley.gg, 23 Jan 2025](https://pley.gg/cs2/hltv-fantasy-season-kicks-off-with-blast-bounty-finals/)
- Fantasy is per event (one lineup per tournament), grouped into seasons such as Spring 2025 — [pley.gg](https://pley.gg/cs2/hltv-fantasy-season-kicks-off-with-blast-bounty-finals/)

**VCT Pick'Em (Valorant; live)**
- Pick'Ems are per event; Champions Paris 2025: in groups, pick two teams promoted and two eliminated per group; in playoffs, pick winner and loser of every series to the grand final — [Dot Esports guide (search snippet)](https://dotesports.com/valorant/news/vct-champions-2025-pickems-guide)
- Leaderboard grouped by community factions; only the top 75% of a faction count to its average — [valorantesports.com Champions Paris Pick'Ems leaderboard (search snippet)](https://valorantesports.com/en-US/pickems/113482263742879102/factions/leaderboard)
- vlr.gg (fan site) runs its own pick'ems per event: a bracket with points per matchup and a leaderboard showing the share of users at each score — [vlr.gg VCT 2025 Americas Kickoff pick'em](https://www.vlr.gg/event/pickem/2274/vct-2025-americas-kickoff)

### Inferences
- Riot's own page set is far smaller than FPL's: no separate Transfers, Points, Fixtures, Stats or Prizes pages were named. Market replaces Transfers because the squad is rebuilt weekly against a weekly budget.
- League page needs are similar to FPL's (create, join, rename, leave, delete), and Riot shipped them late — kick, leave and delete were missing at launch.

### Gaps
- No source reached for ltafantasy.com's actual navigation, player card, points page or schedule page (rules page returned 403; no screenshots found).
- No official CBLOL, LEC or LCK fantasy found for 2024–2026. The CBLOL and any 2026 Riot fantasy were not confirmed either way.
- E-GO's page set and roster format not reached.
- HLTV Fantasy league types (private, global) and its pages not reached (hltv.org 403).
- Fantasy LCS's page set (draft room, trades, free agents, weekly head-to-head) not confirmed from a reachable source.

## How per-series and per-game points are shown

### Takeaway
LTA Fantasy averages a player's results across his matches in a weekend and sums players for the team; HLTV scores per match from HLTV rating. No source showed a game-by-game breakdown screen.

### Cited Findings
- LTA: player points average their results across matches in a weekend or round; team points total player points; weekend results final on Mondays at 7:00am PT; all phases including playoffs count — [Riot support: LTA Fantasy FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq)
- LTA Split 3 added +2 for each game won against an opponent the manager picked, a gold-lead-at-14-minutes bonus, and objective-steal bonuses (Baron, Elder, Dragon, Herald, Atakhan) — [lolesports.com Split 3 article](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)
- LTA Split 3 variety bonus: +5% for players from two teams up to +20% for five; single-team lineups allowed but get none — [lolesports.com Split 3 article](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)
- A substituted player scores what his substitute scores but keeps his price — [lolesports.com Split 3 article](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)
- HLTV: base points from HLTV rating each match, +6 for a win, -3 for a loss, plus role and booster bonuses; scored per match, not per map — [pley.gg](https://pley.gg/cs2/hltv-fantasy-season-kicks-off-with-blast-bounty-finals/)

### Inferences
- Averaging over a weekend is LTA's answer to teams playing different numbers of games; per-game detail is then hidden behind one figure.
- The +2 per game won against a picked opponent implies a per-game count is held, even if not shown.

### Gaps
- No screen found showing a series broken down game by game, with champion and KDA per game, in any of these products.

## What's unusual (role locks, team slots, coach, draft leagues, pick'em brackets)

### Takeaway
LoL fantasy has used role-locked slots (Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC, Support) plus a flex and a team slot (Fantasy LCS); LTA added a picked opponent and a variety bonus; CS2 replaces positions with assigned roles and one-match boosters; Valorant and Korean products are prediction games, not squads.

### Cited Findings
- Fantasy LCS lineup: one each of Top, Jungle, Mid, ADC, Support, plus a flex slot (any role) and a real LCS team slot — [Inquirer Esports (search snippet)](https://esports.inquirer.net/?p=4276); [r/FantasyLCS glossary (search snippet)](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/FantasyLCS/wiki/glossary)
- Fantasy LCS alternates: three subs; sources differ on the exact wording — [GameSkinny (search snippet)](https://www.gameskinny.com/news/league-of-legends-to-offer-official-fantasy-lcs-beta/)
- Fantasy LCS was draft-based (leagues draft before the split) — [GameSkinny (search snippet)](https://www.gameskinny.com/news/league-of-legends-to-offer-official-fantasy-lcs-beta/)
- LTA: inactive players go to the bench; lineup editable until the market closes (9:00am PT, when LTA South starts) — [Riot support: LTA Fantasy FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq)
- LTA: one Favourite Team, changeable once per stage, used for prizes — [Riot support FAQ](https://support.riotgames.com/en-us/league-of-legends/events/lta-fantasy-faq); [lolesports.com Split 3](https://lolesports.com/en-US/news/fantasy)
- HLTV: one role per player for bonus points; boosters apply to one match and carry no penalty, unlike roles — [Dot Esports (search snippet)](https://dotesports.com/?p=626308); [pley.gg](https://pley.gg/cs2/hltv-fantasy-season-kicks-off-with-blast-bounty-finals/)
- VCT: bracket pick'em with group promote/eliminate picks and faction leaderboards — [Dot Esports (search snippet)](https://dotesports.com/valorant/news/vct-champions-2025-pickems-guide); [valorantesports.com](https://valorantesports.com/en-US/pickems/113482263742879102/factions/leaderboard)

### Inferences
- The faction leaderboard (fans grouped by team, average of top 75%) is the nearest esports analogue to an FPL supporter league.
- No coach slot was found in any product reached.

### Gaps
- LTA's exact roster slots (whether role-locked, whether a coach or team slot exists) could not be confirmed: rules page 403.
- Fantasy LCS trades, waivers and weekly head-to-head format not confirmed from a reachable source.

## App-only features

### Takeaway
No product reached has an app-only feature on record; LTA Fantasy is a web site played on mobile or PC.

### Cited Findings

## Korean LCK fan games

### Takeaway
No LCK fantasy game was found. Korean LCK fan games are match predictions (승부예측) and Player of the Match votes on the broadcaster SOOP, paid for in SOOP's own currency (gems), tied to team support and live-stream rewards.

### Cited Findings
- SOOP, 2025 LCK Playoffs and Play-In (3–28 Sep 2025): weekly and period prediction sets; gifticon rewards by accuracy and participation; 10,000 gems for predicting every match; gem codes dropped in LCK live streams (10,000 users a day); season-end ranking prizes (iPad Pro for first, mice for 2nd–6th) — [Dailian, 2 Sep 2025](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1543367/)
- SOOP: Player of the Match votes on the LCK official broadcast, with gifticons drawn among correct voters — [Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1543367/)
- SOOP, 2026 LCK Cup (page opened 13 Jan 2026): missions (sign up, log in, bookmark the LCK channel, pick a favourite team) earn gems; gems buy prize entries, emoticons and predictions; fans pick a team and see live counts of fans per team; supporter streamers for seven clubs (T1, Gen.G, KT, DK, BFX, DRX, DN SOOPers) — [Smart Today, 12 Jan 2026](https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/100792)
- SOOP opened a 2026 Worlds event page on 15 Oct 2026 with Hextech Chest and Key giveaways tied to watch time — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27230/soop-to-open-2026-worlds-event-page-on-october-15)

### Inferences
- Korean features to note: a favourite-team pick with a live fan count per team, gems as a currency, rewards tied to watching the broadcast, PoM votes, and club-linked supporter streamers.

### Gaps
- No LCK-run pick'em, and no Chzzk, Naver or AfreecaTV fantasy, was found. Inven and Namu Wiki were not reached.
- SOOP's prediction page layout not described in any source reached.
- A search summary said SOOP's 2026 LCK Cup had a prediction against a hermit crab's win rate; the fetched article did not say so, so it is unconfirmed.
