# Page and screen inventory: season-long fantasy games outside FPL

**Contents:** top nav and core pages · chips and boosters · unusual or better than FPL · app-only features

Games covered (five): F1 Fantasy 2026, AFL Fantasy Classic 2026, Bundesliga Fantasy Manager 2025/26, ESPN Fantasy Football 2025, Six Nations Fantasy Rugby 2026. Research ran on 13 Nov 2026 within a 15-call budget. Every page here sits behind a login or inside an app, and the help pages could not be fetched (fantasy.formula1.com/en/game-rules returned only a header). The evidence is therefore launch articles, guides and app listings, not screenshots. The nav groupings below are mostly gaps.

## Top nav and grouping, and the core page set (home, team, transfers, chips, points, history, stats, fixtures, leagues, other managers, help, onboarding, settings)

### Takeaway
The sources describe features, not the menu. What can be reconstructed is the same core as FPL in every budget game: a team page (pick and lineup), transfers or trades, captain or boost, leagues with private, public and head-to-head kinds, and live points. The variety is in leagues, history and benchmarking. No source gave a confirmed top-nav grouping for any game.

### Cited Findings

**F1 Fantasy 2026**
- Budget: a $100m cost cap for 5 drivers and 2 constructors. Up to three teams per account, and all three can win in the Global League (new for 2026). — [Formula1.com, 24 Feb 2026](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/boost-the-fun-with-friends-and-family-this-season-f1-fantasy-is-back.2xVBBN90riZiaYXeYhOe0Z)
- Transfers: two free per round per team. The new "Net Transfers" rule counts only final changes, "not every tap". — [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/boost-the-fun-with-friends-and-family-this-season-f1-fantasy-is-back.2xVBBN90riZiaYXeYhOe0Z)
- Transfer limits: unlimited for the first race. After that, 2 free per race, one unused transfer carries over (3 at most), and each extra transfer costs -10. — [FanAmp beginner guide, Feb 2026](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/f1-fantasy-beginner-guide)
- Prices and the cap: values are reassessed after each weekend, and the cap moves with team value, for example $100M to $101M after a $1M gain. Caps can reach about $130M by season's end. — [FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/f1-fantasy-beginner-guide)
- Deadline: lineups lock at the start of Qualifying, or at the start of the Sprint on Sprint weekends. — [FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/f1-fantasy-beginner-guide); changes are allowed "right up until Qualifying begins" — [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/boost-the-fun-with-friends-and-family-this-season-f1-fantasy-is-back.2xVBBN90riZiaYXeYhOe0Z)
- Leagues: entry to the Global, Country, Constructor (favourite team) and Driver (favourite driver) leagues is automatic. Private leagues, head-to-head, followable leagues (e.g. a Presenter League) and themed mini-leagues come during the season. — [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/boost-the-fun-with-friends-and-family-this-season-f1-fantasy-is-back.2xVBBN90riZiaYXeYhOe0Z). Survey questions at registration place you in leagues. "Battlemode" is a season-long 1v1. Mini leagues last 1 to 3 races and are hosted by teams. — [FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/f1-fantasy-beginner-guide)
- Leagues tab: a Reddit user said the tab showed leagues to join rather than their own (reported second-hand through search results; the thread was not fetched). — [r/F1Fantasy mirror](https://reddit.sudovanilla.org/r/F1Fantasy)
- Global League prizes: 2027 GP tickets with VIP extras, two grandstand tickets, and a £500 store voucher. — [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/boost-the-fun-with-friends-and-family-this-season-f1-fantasy-is-back.2xVBBN90riZiaYXeYhOe0Z)
- Scale: more than 2.75 million teams in 2025, a record. — [FanAmp](https://www.fanamp.com/f1-news/mini-leagues-thrive-in-another-successful-season-for-f1-fantasy-2025)

**AFL Fantasy Classic 2026**
- Squad of 30: 22 on field (8 defenders, 10 midfielders, 3 rucks, 8 forwards, 1 utility) and a bench of 8, 4 of them emergencies. Cap $18.3m, prices from $230k to $1.22m. — [AFL.com.au, 21 Jan 2026](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459342/pick-your-team-now-play-afl-fantasy-in-2026/amp)
- Leagues: head-to-head with 4 to 20 friends, or open with unlimited entrants. The creator is the "commissioner". — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459342/pick-your-team-now-play-afl-fantasy-in-2026/amp)
- Named pages: My Team, a trades flow (now also editable from My Team), a match-ups page (opponent view), a rankings page, the Stats Centre, league history, and "Foes". — [AFL.com.au, Classic 2026](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- Site menu entries: "Play AFL Fantasy", "Fantasy Hub", "Play AFLW Fantasy", "FANTASY LATEST". — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- Off-site pages: the Fantasy Hub (afl.com.au/fantasy) carries advice, and Match Centre and the AFL Live app show live Fantasy stats. — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459342/pick-your-team-now-play-afl-fantasy-in-2026/amp)
- Bye rounds: 2 trades in early byes, 3 in rounds 12 to 16. Prices are fixed after Opening Round, breakevens still update, and prices first change after round 1 (search summary of AFL.com.au coverage). — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait); [DreamTeamTalk review](https://dreamteamtalk.com/2025/12/22/warnies-take-on-the-new-afl-fantasy-classic-features)

**Bundesliga Fantasy Manager 2025/26**
- Squad: 2 GK, 5 DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD; positions fixed all season; at most 3 per club. — [Bundesliga.com, 8 Jul 2025](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- Starting squad: assigned randomly at 150m market value. Late joiners get an adjusted random squad and unlimited transfers until the next matchday. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- Saving: changes save automatically, with "no save button". Formation and XI lock at the first kick-off of the matchday. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- Transfers: 5 per matchday, cancellable until the first kick-off. Undoing one means re-picking the XI and star players. Transfers are unlimited during the winter and international breaks. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- Leagues: overall, matchday and team rankings; a Second Chance league after the winter break; a late-comer league; unlimited open and private leagues with a creator-set start date and a join code; a sponsor league (FC 26). — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- H2H modes: league (3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, byes), knockout (one or two legs; tie-breaks star points, then squad value, then random), and league then knockout. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987); [Bundesliga.com H2H](https://amp.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-season-2025-2026-return-head-to-head-mode-league-mode-knockout-33100)

**ESPN Fantasy Football 2025 (draft and waiver, not budget)**
- Screens named in the release: a personalised Home, the Roster (with a "Dynamic Roster Dashboard"), Matchup, Players (add/drop), and Player Cards. — [ESPN Press Room, 7 Aug 2025](http://espnpressroom.com/press-release/espn-fantasy-football-30th-anniversary-new-design-new-features-all-new-fantasy-app-for-2025/)

**Six Nations Fantasy Rugby 2026**
- Rules: a fixed budget, at most 3 players per nation, a double-points captain and a triple-points supersub. Played on the Six Nations site, with supporter leagues such as the Irish Rugby Supporters league. — [IRFU, 29 Jan 2026](https://www.irishrugby.ie/2026/01/29/six-nations-fantasy-rugby-is-back/)
- Runs 5 Feb to 14 Mar 2026, online or in the official Six Nations app (search summary). — [Six Nations Rugby](https://sixnationsrugby.com/en/m6n/news/guide-to-six-nations-fantasy-rugby-2025)
- From earlier seasons: the supersub must start on the bench, a supersub who starts earns half points, and transfers between rounds were unlimited (search summary of 2024 guides; may be out of date). — [Planet Rugby](https://www.planetrugby.com/news/a-starters-guide-to-six-nations-fantasy-rugby-2023)

### Inferences
- Pages common to all four budget games: Team (lineup plus captain or boost), Transfers or trades, Leagues (private, public, H2H, an auto-entered global league), live points, and a rules or how-to page.
- Bundesliga and F1 both auto-enter managers into leagues by attribute: Country, Constructor and Driver leagues in F1; Second Chance, late-comer and sponsor leagues in the Bundesliga. Flol's supporter leagues map onto F1's Constructor League, which is made from the favourite team picked at signup.
- Several games put news and advice off-site, outside the game: AFL's Fantasy Hub, F1's articles, ESPN's Draft Guide.

### Gaps
- No primary source describes the real top nav or tab bar of any of the five games. Help pages were blocked or empty, and no screenshot walkthroughs were found within budget.
- Not found for any game: the onboarding flow, settings, the points-per-round page layout, or a past-seasons history page (AFL's league history excepted).
- The player stats list and player detail page were named only for AFL (Stats Centre) and ESPN (Player Cards); no layouts were found.
- Yahoo, NBA, La Liga Fantasy and Super Rugby were not covered.

## Chips and boosters: activation and confirmation flow

### Takeaway
F1 has the most FPL-like chip system: six chips, one per weekend, each final once played. Bundesliga has no chips; its 1.5x "star players" (one per line) replace a captain. Rugby uses a captain and a supersub, with no chips confirmed. No source describes a confirmation screen.

### Cited Findings
- F1 has six chips: Limitless, Extra DRS (3x Boost), No Negative, Autopilot, Final Fix and Wildcard. Each is used once per season, at most one per weekend. — [Motorsport Magazine, updated Nov 2025](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/f1-fantasy-chips-what-they-are-and-when-to-use-them/); [FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/f1-fantasy-beginner-guide)
- At the first race only No Negative, Autopilot and Extra DRS are open. The rest unlock once your first lineup completes a race weekend. — [Motorsport Magazine](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/f1-fantasy-chips-what-they-are-and-when-to-use-them/)
- A chip, once activated, "cannot be cancelled" and is gone for the season. — [Motorsport Magazine](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/f1-fantasy-chips-what-they-are-and-when-to-use-them/)
- Final Fix is played after the qualifying deadline and before the GP: a chip played after the deadline. — [Motorsport Magazine](https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/articles/single-seaters/f1/f1-fantasy-chips-what-they-are-and-when-to-use-them/)
- Wildcard: its logo appears at the top of the lineup page once selected (FanAmp guide, seen only in a search summary). — [FanAmp Wildcard](https://www.fanamp.com/f1-news/wildcard-chip-explained)
- 2x Boost: a weekly pick of one driver; negatives double too (a GP DNF -20 becomes -40). — [FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/f1-fantasy-beginner-guide)
- Bundesliga: one star player per line at 1.5x; if a star misses the Best 11, no replacement is made. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- Bundesliga Best 11: the top 11 scorers always count, so there are no manual subs. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)

### Inferences
- F1's staggered unlock (three chips first, three after the first weekend) and Final Fix are chip-calendar ideas FPL lacks.

### Gaps
- No source shows the chip activation screen or confirm dialog in any game.

## Unusual or better than FPL

### Takeaway
The strongest ideas: AFL's benchmarking against the top 1%, 5%, 100 and 1000, and its "Foes" list. F1's three teams per account and auto-made fan leagues. Bundesliga's Daily Bonus, Best 11 auto-subs, H2H knockout mode, Second Chance league and autosave. ESPN's day-aware action dashboard.

### Cited Findings
- AFL "Foes": track any team's weekly score without sharing a league. — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- AFL: compare yourself with the top 1% and 5%, plus "real-time comparisons to the top 100 and top 1000 coaches each round". — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- AFL: a "Fantasy hat icon" on rankings marks past top-100 finishers, a lasting badge. — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- AFL: trade history shows how each player brought in has scored since joining. League history shows past winners, ladders and results from earlier seasons. — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- AFL: My Team and the Stats Centre can be set to show the stats you choose. — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1460108/afl-fantasy-classic-2026-bigger-better-and-worth-the-wait)
- AFL: a time-on-ground rule swaps in a higher-scoring emergency for anyone under 50% game time. Reviewers also mention projections, a popular-trades widget, team similarity ratings and a bye detector. — [DreamTeamTalk](https://dreamteamtalk.com/2025/12/22/warnies-take-on-the-new-afl-fantasy-classic-features)
- F1: three teams per account, all prize-eligible. Net Transfers count only final changes. — [Formula1.com](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/boost-the-fun-with-friends-and-family-this-season-f1-fantasy-is-back.2xVBBN90riZiaYXeYhOe0Z)
- Bundesliga Daily Bonus: daily activity adds transfer budget on a 7-day chain that resets if a day is missed. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987). Sources disagree on the cap: one says up to 500,000 a week — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-new-feature-daily-bonus-additional-transfer-funds-33074)
- Bundesliga: dynamic market values update when each transfer market opens. — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- ESPN's Dynamic Roster Dashboard lists action items by weekday and league activity (waiver reminders, trade offers). The Matchup view swipes between all league matchups and pins the live score. Live projections update in-game. Auto-Reactivate restarts a league each season. A "Find a Bet" icon links to ESPN BET. — [ESPN Press Room](http://espnpressroom.com/press-release/espn-fantasy-football-30th-anniversary-new-design-new-features-all-new-fantasy-app-for-2025/)
- ESPN league chat exists but is long-standing, not new in 2025 (search summary). — [ESPN Africa](https://africa.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/45844949/2025-fantasy-football-where-play-new-features-espn)

### Inferences
- For Flol, AFL's "Foes" (follow a rival without a league) and top-1%/top-100 benchmarking are cheap, distinctive additions to a points page.
- AFL's hat icon is the only trophy or badge found. No "wrapped" season recap was found in any of the five games.

### Gaps
- No evidence of season recap or "wrapped" pages, achievements beyond AFL's hat, or in-game league chat for F1, AFL, Bundesliga or Six Nations.

## App-only features

### Takeaway
Bundesliga's game is app-only. AFL launched on web first and the app followed. F1 and Six Nations run on both web and app.

### Cited Findings
- Bundesliga Fantasy is "available exclusively on the official Bundesliga app". — [Bundesliga.com](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-everything-you-need-to-know-32987)
- AFL Fantasy 2026 opened on web and mobile web, with the app "next week" (21 Jan 2026). — [AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459342/pick-your-team-now-play-afl-fantasy-in-2026/amp)
- The AFL app listing covers registration through AFL iD, team management, leagues, live scores and live draft screens. It is rated 4.4 from 76 ratings, with reviews critical of the redesign and crashes. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id796588195)
- ESPN Fantasy app only: the "Find a Bet" icon. — [ESPN Press Room](http://espnpressroom.com/press-release/espn-fantasy-football-30th-anniversary-new-design-new-features-all-new-fantasy-app-for-2025/)

### Gaps
- No source lists features that exist in the F1 or Six Nations apps but not on their websites.
