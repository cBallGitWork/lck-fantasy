# Team page benchmark: official season-long fantasy games

Researched 19 Nov 2026. How the team page (starters, bench, captain) and the frame around it look, game by game.

## Contents

- How this was gathered
- 1. Fantasy Premier League (FPL)
- 2. UEFA Champions League Fantasy
- 3. Bundesliga Fantasy Manager
- 4. LaLiga Fantasy
- 5. Serie A: Fantacalcio (the official game)
- 6. Ligue 1: MPG (Mon Petit Gazon)
- 7. F1 Fantasy
- 8. LTA Fantasy (Riot, 2025)
- Comparison
- What a Summoner's Rift version could borrow

## How this was gathered

- The best evidence is the App Store screenshots, which are current marketing images of the real screens. I pulled the screenshot links from the App Store lookup service and looked at each one.
- Each listing's date is the latest app version date. Screenshots can be older than that; where the screen itself shows a season, I say so.
- Help pages, launch articles and guides gave almost no visual description. Most official news pages have no screenshots of the team page.
- Image links below are mzstatic (Apple) or the source site. Size in the link can be changed (e.g. `600x1300bb.jpg`).
- I saw no desktop screenshots for most games. Desktop notes are thin and say so.

## 1. Fantasy Premier League (FPL)

Source: Premier League official app, App Store GB, v5.9.0, 11 Nov 2026 ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id1138895159)). FPL lives inside this app. FPL 2026/27 added extra squad views (fixture difficulty, ownership, next opponent, form, price change) on Pick Team, Points and Transfers, each in Pitch or List ([Fantasy Football Scout, 21 Jul 2026](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/07/21/fpl-2026-27-new-ways-to-view-your-fpl-squad)).

**Pitch**
- Bright, flat grass green with faint lighter mowing bands. White lines: box, six-yard box, centre circle.
- Seen top-down, slightly tilted at the very top (goal net drawn at the top edge). Goal at the top, forwards at the bottom.
- An ad-board strip across the top of the pitch: cyan-to-violet gradient with "Fantasy" and the PL lion, repeated. Looks like stadium LED boards.
- The pitch fills the full width and most of the height on mobile.

**Players**
- Club shirt (kit render), not a photo. Goalkeeper in keeper kit.
- Each shirt sits on a small dark green square tile; a white name plate below with the surname in bold dark purple, and a second grey row with the position (GKP/DEF/MID/FWD) on Pick Team. Other views swap that row for fixture, points etc.
- Formation in rows: GK, then DEF, MID, FWD.

**Captain, vice, bench, chips**
- Captain: a small black circle with white "C" on the shirt tile's top-left corner. Vice: same with "V".
- Bench: not visible in the screenshot (below the fold). By FPL convention it is a separate strip of four under the pitch.
- Chips: a row of four cards above the pitch (Bench Boost, Triple Captain, Wildcard, Free Hit). Each has an icon in a ring, the name, and a "Play" button outlined, or the word "Unavailable" greyed out.
- Under the chips, a Pitch / List pill toggle.

**Frame**
- Deep plum-purple ground (very dark, near black-purple), white text, back arrow in a purple circle. Title "Pick Team" centred.
- Accents: cyan, violet and green gradients (the PL's 2025 brand). Promo art for 2025/26 shows player cards on a cyan-violet gradient ground with name and price in a white rounded plate ([PL, 11 Aug 2025](https://www.premierleague.com/news/4364856)).
- Typeface: the Premier League's own geometric sans, bold, round. Not named in any source I found.
- Corners: rounded throughout (cards, pills, tiles). Medium density.
- Deadline and Save: not visible in the screenshot.

**Mobile vs desktop**: no current desktop screenshot found.

**What makes it the PL**: the lion and the cyan-violet "Fantasy" boards round the pitch; deep plum ground.

## Comparison

**What all of them share (the genre's conventions)**
- A top-down, flat pitch with players in formation rows. No one uses true 3D. Only the third-party Tattico tilts it.
- One small token per player, then a name plate under it. The plate's second line is the one that changes by context: position, next fixture, price or points.
- Captain as a tiny letter badge ("C") pinned to a corner of the player token. Vice as "V" where it exists.
- Bench as a separate row under the pitch, visually lower status (smaller or on a different ground).
- A header that carries the week: round number, a deadline (date or live countdown) and budget or points.
- Rounded corners, white plates, one strong brand accent. Mobile first; all evidence is phone screens.

**Where they differ**
- Player token: club shirt (FPL, UEFA, Tattico), photo card (LaLiga, MPG, F1), pennant (Bundesliga), cartoon caricature (Fantacalcio).
- Pitch: bright flat grass (FPL), mint teal (UEFA), real grass photo (Bundesliga), near-black outline (LaLiga), sage plus jokes (MPG), mint panel with no pitch (F1).
- Direction: FPL, LaLiga, MPG put the keeper at the top; UEFA and Bundesliga put him at the bottom.
- Ground: dark is now the norm (FPL plum, UEFA navy, LaLiga black, F1 black). Bundesliga's team page is the light one.
- Boosts: FPL puts chips in a card row above the pitch; MPG puts bonuses on the pitch as cards; F1 uses a separate sheet; UEFA keeps them off the team screen.
- Brand marks on the pitch: FPL and Fantacalcio wrap the pitch in ad boards; MPG names the stadium on a side sign.

## What a Summoner's Rift version could borrow

Read with Craig's rule of 19 Nov 2026: the Rift is sacred, drawn from the real pictures in
`docs/design/references/rift/` and the same in every theme. Where a line below says to simplify the map,
his rule wins. This file is background research, not the product.

- **The map as the pitch, flat and top-down.** Every game keeps the pitch flat and plain so tokens read. Draw the Rift as a simplified diagonal map (three lanes, river, two bases) in one or two tones, not a painted render.
- **Fixed spots, not formation rows.** Put each role on its natural spot: Top lane top-left, Jungle in the jungle, Mid in the centre, Bot and Support together bottom-right. This replaces formation rows the way UEFA's fixed rows do.
- **Token plus two-line plate.** Keep the genre's plate: name on line one; line two switches by view (next series, price, points), as FPL's 2026/27 views do and UEFA's fixture bar does ("v T1").
- **Team colour behind the player**, as F1 does, so a five-man roster mixing teams reads at a glance. The Bundesliga pennant with a crest in the corner is the closest football analogue.
- **Captain as a corner badge.** A small letter or mark in the token's corner. F1 shows the badge can carry the multiplier ("2X") instead of a letter.
- **Bench off the map**, in a strip below or beside it, on a darker ground, like UEFA's bench row.
- **Boosts on the map as items (MPG)** or as a card row above it (FPL). On the Rift, item or summoner-spell style slots near the base would read as LoL.
