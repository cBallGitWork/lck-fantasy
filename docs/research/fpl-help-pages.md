# FPL's Help pages, read 17 Nov 2026

What FPL's Help area holds and how it is laid out, read in Chrome for the Help `/design` round. These are notes in our own words, not FPL's text: their copy is theirs. Background only, never the product (`CLAUDE.md`).

Sources: [Help](https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help), [Rules](https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help/rules), [FAQs](https://fantasy.premierleague.com/en/help/faqs), [Basics: managing your team](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2174899/fpl-basics-managing-your-team).

## Layout

- One Help page with its own tabs: **Help**, **Rules**, **FAQs**, **T&Cs** (and a "New" tab for the season's changes).
- **Help** is a beginner's guide. A short intro (what the game is, what you do, where to read more), then sections that open and close, each a heading with a few lines and a bullet list. A sticky **Help menu** on the right links to every section.
- **Rules** is the full rulebook. Group headings, each with items that open and close (one question or topic each). No side menu.
- **FAQs** is the same accordion, grouped by topic, each item a question a manager actually asks.

## Help (the guide), section by section

1. **Intro**: what FPL is in one sentence, what you do over a season, that there's a lot to learn and where to go next.
2. **In 60 seconds**: budget and squad, what scores, how gameweeks group the fixtures, starters, bench and captain, transfers, chips, and the aim (most points, climb the ranks, beat friends).
3. **Step 1, build your squad**: the size and budget, then a bullet list of what a good first squad usually has (regular starters, players likely to score, a strong starting side with cover, one or two players to captain).
4. **Step 2, pick your line-up**: you choose your starters each week; the bench comes on in your order if a starter doesn't play; questions to ask yourself (who will start, who has a good fixture, is the bench in order).
5. **Step 3, captain**: why it matters most, what makes a good pick, and the vice-captain.
6. **Step 4, how points are scored**: the main ways to score as a short list, with a paragraph each on the less obvious ones.
7. **Step 5, transfers**: what they're for (injuries, fixtures, form, moving money), then the free transfer, the hit and saving transfers.
8. **Step 6, chips**: one a week; each chip in one line; two sets a season, and unused ones are lost.
9. **Step 7, leagues**: the ones you're put in automatically, private leagues, and the exact clicks to create or join one.
10. **Gameweek checklist**: when the deadline is and where to see it, then a list of questions to tick off before it.
11. **Glossary**: every term in one line (gameweek, deadline, squad, bench, captain, transfer, hit, each chip, form, fixture, double and blank gameweeks, price change).
12. **Ready for more**: where to go next (Rules, FAQs, page help, stats).

Each step ends with a link to a longer "Basics explained" article. Those articles explain what a gameweek is (usually 10 fixtures, every club plays once), when the deadline is, and give the exact clicks on Pick Team (which button swaps, where captain is ticked, Save My Team).

## Rules, the groups and items

- **Selecting your initial squad**: squad size by position, budget, players per team.
- **Managing your squad**: choosing your starting 11 (the formation rules), captain and vice-captain (what happens if either doesn't play), bench order and automatic substitutions (what counts as playing, the order they're tried in).
- **Transfers**: transfer rules (unlimited before the first deadline, 1 free a week, the 4-point hit, saving up to 5, a cap of 20 in a week), Wildcards (points to chips), player prices (move on popularity; you keep half of any rise when you sell).
- **Chips**: a table of each chip and its effect, when each set is available, which can be cancelled, and that saved transfers are kept through a Wildcard or Free Hit.
- **Deadlines**: what must be in by the deadline, how far before kick-off it is, that it won't move within 24 hours, then a table of every gameweek's deadline.
- **Scoring**: a points table by action and position, then one item each for the tricky lines (defensive contributions, clean sheets, red cards, assists, bonus points, how bonus is calculated).
- **Leagues**: joining, league types (invitational, public, global and the special ones), classic and head-to-head scoring, ties, phases (monthly tables).
- **Cups**: what they are and how a round works.

## FAQs, the groups

Account; choosing your squad; managing your team; offensive names; chips; leagues; cups; transfers; Wildcards; double and blank gameweeks; player data (updates, prices, form, positions); defensive contribution; bonus points; assists; automatic substitutions; real-world transfers; prizes; the app; contact.

## What Flol takes from it

- Two tabs, Help and Rules, with Help's sticky menu on the right.
- Help explains what a week is in real terms, says where to click, and gives "what to look for" lists, not just rules.
- Rules is grouped like FPL's, one item per topic, each opening on its own.
- A deadlines item in Rules, from the competition's schedule data.
- FAQs wait: the beta has no questions-and-answers section (spec/12, Help).
