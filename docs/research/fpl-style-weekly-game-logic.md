# One deadline, public prices: LCK weekly logic

An FPL-style LCK game should keep FPL's single weekly deadline and its free-transfer economy, but should not copy FPL's prices or its blank-and-double planning. **FPL locks everything once a week, 90 minutes before the first kick-off** ([Goal](https://www.goal.com/en-gb/news/when-next-fpl-deadline-dates-times-premier-league-fantasy-team/blt3ada2bac6c9821f9)). UEFA, FIFA, Sofascore's MLS game and the Australian games instead lock each player when his own match starts, and let managers swap in or re-captain players who have not played yet. For the LCK, the same 90-minute rule gives a deadline of **Wednesday 06:30 UTC (15:30 KST)**. Rolling lockout would add little: every team plays its first series by about Friday. It would also hand an edge to managers awake during Korean evening matches, which run from 08:00 UTC (00:00–01:00 on the US west coast). Prices should not copy FPL either. FPL's prices are secret and move on transfer demand, which fits a pool of about 600 players. **UCL, MLS, Bundesliga, F1 and SuperCoach all price on recent performance**, and that fits a pool of about 50 LCK starters far better. A public formula also lets the game publish breakevens and a price predictor of its own. The LCK regular season has **no blanks or doubles at all**, since every team plays two Bo3 series a week. FPL's main source of planning depth is therefore missing, so blank-week rules matter only around Road to MSI and the playoffs. Weekly depth has to come from opponents, form, prices and chips. Of the reverting chips, a budgeted Free Hit ("Stand-in") suits a small pool better than an unlimited-budget Limitless. And a two-player chip such as Eliteserien's "Two Captains" maps neatly onto a **bot-lane chip that also lifts supports**. **Caveat: the network blocked almost every primary source (premierleague.com, uefa.com, bundesliga.com, afl.com.au, nbafantasy.nba.com, Fantasy Football Scout and others) and all of Reddit, so every rule below comes from search-result snippets, not full pages. Each exact number needs checking against the live rules before it goes into a spec.** Every recommendation is labelled as inference.

## The LCK gives the game even weeks, two series and a morning slot

Three LCK facts drive almost every recommendation below. First, the regular season is even. A ten-team double round robin of Bo3s, played Wednesday to Sunday at two series a day, means **every team plays exactly two series a week**. A team plays four to six games depending on series length, and nobody blanks or doubles until the knockouts ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCK/2026/Rounds_1-2); [Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCK/2026/Rounds_3-4)). In 2026, **Road to MSI took only the top six** in Bo5 knockouts, so four teams blanked ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/t1-and-hanwha-life-esports-with-a-double-chance-to-qualify-road-to-msi-bracket-settled/en)). Second, matches start at **17:00 KST (08:00 UTC) every day**, including weekends, after a 2026 change aimed at European viewers ([esports-news.co.uk](https://esports-news.co.uk/?p=174580)). The second series follows at about 19:00 KST, so a three-game Bo3 ends near 22:00 KST (13:00 UTC). Korea has no daylight saving, so a deadline stored in UTC never moves in Korea, but it shifts by an hour in the UK and the US when their clocks change. Third, the pool is small: **about 50 starters**, most of them reshuffled each winter. These facts differ from the Premier League's in kind, not degree. So the question for each FPL mechanic is whether it solves a problem the LCK actually has.

## Deadlines: keep one weekly lock, 90 minutes before the first series

**What FPL does.** One deadline per gameweek, **90 minutes before the first kick-off** (it was 60). Because it keys off the first fixture, a Friday-night match means a Friday deadline, and rescheduled fixtures move it ([OnsideArena](https://onsidearena.com/guides/fpl-deadline-explained); [Goal](https://www.goal.com/en-gb/news/when-next-fpl-deadline-dates-times-premier-league-fantasy-team/blt3ada2bac6c9821f9)). Changes not confirmed by the deadline are lost, and the team saved at the previous deadline plays. Only auto-subs happen after the lock ([OnsideArena](https://onsidearena.com/guides/fpl-deadline-explained)). Transfers are unlimited until the GW1 deadline ([Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/articles/making-transfers-in-fantasy-premier-league)). Fan sites publish whole-season deadline calendars, which shows there is demand for a deadline list shown in local time ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/fpl-deadlines-2026-27-every-gameweek)).

**What others do differently.** Almost every official game except FPL now uses some form of rolling lock. In **UCL Fantasy** a matchday runs Tuesday to Wednesday or Thursday. Once it is live, you can sub in, or make captain, any player whose match has not kicked off. "You can't make a player captain, or sub one in, after their match in that matchday has been played", and confirmed changes cannot be undone ([UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/news/025f-0fd4b42cc0a7-74498b7df63b-1000/)). Switching captain removes the old captain's double points and doubles the new one's. **Any manual change during a matchday turns off auto-subs for that matchday** ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/08/31/how-to-play-uefa-champions-league-fantasy-2026-27-rules-scoring-chips)). UEFA bans in-round changes on matchdays where every fixture kicks off together ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/09/02/ucl-fantasy-2026-27-chip-strategy-when-to-use-wildcard-limitless)). Euro 2024 handled a captain who did not play in two ways. If the manager had made no manual changes, the captain was auto-subbed out and the replacement got no double points. If the manager had made manual changes, the captain stayed in and nobody scored double ([UEFA](https://www.uefa.com/uefaeuro/history/news/0268-121c6d842f48-45f4db182115-1000--euro-2024-fantasy-football-rules/)). FIFA's 2026 World Cup game allowed unlimited captain changes, with the same loss of the old captain's double points ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/05/26/how-to-play-the-fifa-world-cup-2026-fantasy-game)). Sofascore's MLS game uses rolling lockouts with a deadline 30 minutes before the first match ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/02/18/mls-fantasy-sofascore-how-to-play)). Eliteserien locks 5 minutes before the first kick-off ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/03/18/eliteserien-fantasy-2025-preview)), and Ligue 1 1 minute before ([Ligue 1](https://ligue1.com/fantasy/rules)).

**AFL Fantasy** runs the fullest rolling lockout. Trades, subs, captaincy and emergencies stay open for each player until his own club plays, and the round's last match brings full lockout ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/562880/how-best-to-deal-with-the-rolling-lockout-in-fantasy)). It also shows the cost. In the **vice-captain loophole**, a manager makes a Thursday player vice-captain. If he scores well, the manager names as captain a not-yet-locked player who will not play, so the captain scores zero and the vice-captain's score doubles. In the **emergency loophole**, a manager drops an unlocked on-field player so that an emergency who has already scored well counts instead ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/562880/how-best-to-deal-with-the-rolling-lockout-in-fantasy); [Coaches Panel](https://coachespanel.tv/?p=2974)). The AFL treats both as skill. Some private leagues avoid them with a single "first-Saturday" lockout ([UltimateFooty](https://ultimatefooty.com.au/help/lineups)). **NBA Fantasy** splits the week instead: transfers are weekly, but the starting five is set for each Gameday and locks 30 minutes before that day's first tip ([NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/how-to-play-nba-fantasy-salary-cap-edition-2026-27)).

**For the LCK (inference).** Use a **single weekly deadline at Wednesday 06:30 UTC (15:30 KST)**: FPL's 90 minutes before the first series. Store it per gameweek as data, so a Super Week or knockout week that starts on another day simply carries its own time. A rolling lock would fit the LCK worse than it fits UCL, for three reasons. The first is that the live window is short. Each team plays its first series by about Friday, so a player locks then and in-round swaps die midweek, leaving nothing open for the weekend. Under UCL's rule the lock is per player, so once his team has played its first series he is locked for the week even though he plays again.

The second reason is fairness across time zones. Matches run 08:00–13:00 UTC. That is the UK's working morning, the US's night (the deadline itself falls at 22:30–23:30 Tuesday on the US west coast) and Korea's evening. UCL's matches all fall in the European evening, so its audience can watch, then react. An LCK game with in-round decisions would reward whoever is awake at 17:00 KST, and the stated audience is worldwide. The third reason is the loopholes. They reappear in LoL form as soon as the pool includes substitutes and sixth men. A manager captains a Saturday team's sub who will not play, after his Wednesday vice-captain has scored well, and the vice-captain doubles.

The one live decision worth keeping is a single bounded one. F1's **Final Fix** chip allows one swap after qualifying, before the race ([FanAmp](https://fanamp.com/f1-news/final-fix-chip-explained)). An LCK version would allow one swap or one captain change for a player whose team has not yet played that week. It would be a chip, not a standing right. Make saving explicit, and show "unsaved changes" loudly, since FPL silently plays the last saved team.

## Transfers: one a week, a lower bank cap, and top-ups as admin events

**What FPL does.** **One free transfer a gameweek, bankable to five** (the cap was two before 2024/25), with **−4 points for each extra** ([Premier League](https://premierleague.com/news/2174907); [AllAboutFPL](https://allaboutfpl.com/2024/07/2024-25-fpl-season-new-rules-changes-whats-new-in-fpl/)). Banked transfers survive a Free Hit: four saved, Free Hit played, still four the week after ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/news/4059225)). Most sources say banked transfers also survive a Wildcard ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/fpl-chips-free-transfers-rules)), though one guide says a Wildcard resets them to one ([OnsideArena](https://onsidearena.com/guides/how-many-transfers-in-fpl)). In 2025/26 FPL treated AFCON as an admin event. Right after the GW15 deadline it **topped every manager up to the five-transfer cap**: a top-up to the cap, not a flat bonus ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4461660/fpl-managers-to-have-five-free-transfers-on-saturday-all-you-need-to-know); [Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/12/08/fpl-gameweek-16-free-transfer-top-up-all-you-need-to-know)).

**What others do differently.** **F1 Fantasy** gives two free transfers a race, with one rollover to a cap of three and **−10 for each extra**. Playing a Wildcard there drops a three-transfer bank back to two ([Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/formula_one/articles/f1_fantasy_2026_the_complete_beginners_guide/s1_17751_43521622); [FanAmp](https://www.fanamp.com/f1-news/wildcard-chip-explained)). **UCL Fantasy** allows two a matchday in the league phase and five before each knockout first leg, unlimited between stages, at −4 each ([FourFourTwo](https://www.fourfourtwo.com/features/how-fantasy-champions-league-works-and-crucial-differences-from-fpl-you-need-to-know)). **NBA Fantasy** allows two a Gameweek, at **−100 points** each extra ([NBA Fantasy rules](https://nbafantasy.nba.com/help/rules)). Sources disagree on whether unused NBA transfers carry over ([NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/nba-fantasy-salary-cap-edition-how-to-play)). Sofascore's MLS game has **no rollover at all** ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/02/18/mls-fantasy-sofascore-how-to-play)). Bundesliga allows **five between matchdays** and opens unlimited windows over winter and international breaks ([Bundesliga](https://fantasy.bundesliga.com/page/rules); [Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/new-rules-bundesliga-fantasy-2024-25-28285)). The Australian games ration trades by the season. **AFL SuperCoach allows 40 a season, at most two a week (three in bye rounds), plus five one-off "trade boosts"** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_SuperCoach)). AFL Fantasy Classic **raises trades from two to three in its mid-season bye rounds** ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459344/how-afl-fantasy-will-tackle-opening-round-early-byes)). IPL's season game gives **160 transfers across the 70-match league stage**, unlimited before Qualifier 1 and 10 for the rest of the playoffs ([Chase Your Sport](https://www.chaseyoursport.com/fantasy-cricket/ipl-2026-fantasy-league-guide-my11circle-rules-team-creation/11994)).

**For the LCK (inference).** The −4 hit is decided; the allowance and the bank cap are not. One free transfer a week is already more generous than it looks. In FPL one transfer changes a fifteenth of the squad. In an LCK squad of seven to nine (five roles, flex, coach, a small bench) it changes an eighth or a ninth, in a pool where good picks converge fast. So keep **one free transfer a week, banked to a cap of three, not five**. A 2027 Split 2 may again be only about four weeks long, and a five-transfer bank there would amount to a second Wildcard. Copy FPL's rule that **banked transfers survive Remake and Stand-in**. It is the cleaner rule, and F1's reset punishes saving.

Treat FPL's AFCON move as a general, data-driven **"top up to cap" admin event**. Fire it when something outside the managers' control wrecks squads: a mid-split roster swap at a team, or the end of the MSI and EWC break. Give unlimited free transfers before the first deadline of each split. As in FPL, top-ups and allowances are league data, never constants.

## Prices: price on performance, publish the formula, freeze the first weeks

**What FPL does.** Prices move in **£0.1m steps, overnight (about 01:30 UK)**, when net transfers pass a secret threshold scaled to ownership. A player moves **at most £0.3m in a gameweek**, and Wildcard transfers do not count ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/07/20/how-do-fpl-price-changes-work); [Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2023/06/01/when-do-fpl-price-changes-take-place); [FantasyFootballReports](https://fantasyfootballreports.com/fpl-price-changes-explained)). Selling price is the purchase price plus half the profit, rounded down, while losses pass through in full: buy at £5.0m, it rises to £5.3m, you sell at £5.1m ([Premier League](https://premierleague.com/news/2174907)). The secrecy fed a tracker industry, led by FPL Statistics' "Target" column ([3 Added Minutes](https://www.3addedminutes.com/gaming/fantasy-premier-league-price-changes-how-work-when-happen-and-how-to-make-a-profit-4282850)). In **2026/27 FPL launched its own Price Change Predictor**, said to update every 15 minutes ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/07/20/fpl-2026-27-5-rule-changes-new-features-announced)).

**What others do differently.** Nearly every other game prices on performance. **UCL** changes prices once between matchdays, at the deadline, by points scored rather than demand. Moves are usually ±0.1–0.2m with a one-matchday lag, and prices are frozen for the first matchday or two ([FantasyFootballReports](https://www.fantasyfootballreports.com/ucl-fantasy-price-changes-explained/)). The old official **MLS** game moved prices in 0.1m steps, **capped at ±0.5m a week**, from round, three-week and five-week averages. A red card cost an automatic −0.2m ([MLS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/bonus-points-explaining-price-change-rule-and-how-it-will-affect-player-values)). **Bundesliga** updates market values after each matchday on performance ([Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/de/bundesliga/news/fantasy-manager-2026-27-regeln-spielerwerte-unbegrenzte-transfers-38324)).

**F1 Fantasy** uses a **three-race rolling points-per-million average** sorted into bands from "Terrible" to "Great". Expensive Tier A drivers (about $18.5M and up) move −0.3, −0.1, +0.1 or +0.3. Cheaper Tier B drivers move −0.6, −0.2, +0.2 or +0.6. There is also a price floor ([FanAmp](https://www.fanamp.com/articles/f1-fantasy-can-ollie-bearman-avoid-a-price-drop-in-miami-and-canada-2026); [FanAmp](https://www.fanamp.com/articles/f1-fantasy-budgets-explained-f1-fantasy-guide-2025); [FanAmp](https://www.fanamp.com/f1-news/hadjar-and-bortoleto-reveal-f1-fantasy-price-floor)). The 2025 move to an equal three-race window was meant to cut the big swings of 2024.

**AFL SuperCoach**, as the community has reverse-engineered it, sets the new price to **0.75 × the old price + 0.25 × a "magic number" × the average of the last three scores** ([SuperCoachTalk](https://supercoachtalk.com/how-supercoach-pricing-works-2022/)). It is a damped moving average, so anyone can compute a **breakeven**: the score that holds a player's price. Footywire publishes a breakeven and expected change for every player every round ([Footywire](https://www.footywire.com/afl/footy/pr-carlton-blues--campbell-chesser)). "Cash generation" from rising rookies is a core AFL strategy ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1085448/fantasy-rookie-bible-the-cash-cows-ranked)). Prices are frozen early: AFL Fantasy prices do not move between Opening Round and Round 1 ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459344/how-afl-fantasy-will-tackle-opening-round-early-byes)), and NRL SuperCoach reportedly waits for a player's third game ([Marathon Stadium](https://marathonstadium.com/threads/supercoach-2026.18148/)).

**For the LCK (inference).** Demand-based pricing needs a large, varied pool and a large crowd. With about 50 starters and an early, small player base, a few dozen managers could move a price, and a Discord could move it on purpose. **Price on performance, once a week, on Monday after points are confirmed.** Use either F1's banded points-per-억 over the last three weeks or SuperCoach's damped average. Use **two tiers with fixed steps**: stars move in small steps, cheap players in larger ones. This lets cheap rookies become cash cows, the strategy layer AFL players value most.

Add a floor and a per-week cap. **Freeze prices for the first two weeks of each split**, when rosters and roles are still settling. Because the formula is public, the game can show each player's **breakeven** ("needs 9 points this week to hold price"), and that breakeven is itself the price predictor. It costs nothing to build and has none of the secrecy that FPL took years to give up. Performance pricing has a further benefit: Wildcard or Stand-in churn cannot distort prices, so the game needs no rule excluding chip transfers.

On selling, FPL's half-profit rule exists to stop managers hoarding gains from demand-driven rises. Under weekly performance pricing, selling at the current price is simpler and makes cash generation the intended skill. The cost is that team value (and so budget) grows unevenly across managers, as in F1 ([FanAmp](https://www.fanamp.com/articles/f1-fantasy-cost-caps-explained)). The backtest on 2026 Oracle's Elixir data should check how far apart team values drift over a split.

## Captaincy and auto-subs: FPL's rules port almost verbatim

**What FPL does.** The captain scores double. If the captain plays no minutes, the vice-captain doubles; if neither plays, nobody does ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/2174899)). One minute on the pitch keeps the armband ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/fpl-auto-subs-bench-order-rules)). Auto-subs run after the gameweek, in bench order, only for starters who played no minutes, and only if the formation stays legal. The minimum is 1 GK, 3 DEF, 2 MID and 1 FWD, and a goalkeeper swaps only with a goalkeeper ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2023/06/01/how-do-substitutes-work-in-fpl-and-what-are-autosubs)).

**What others do differently.** **Bundesliga** has no bench decisions. It **scores your best eleven within your formation**, and its "star" scores 1.5×, with no transfer if the star misses the top eleven ([Bundesliga](https://fantasy.bundesliga.com/page/rules)). **NBA Fantasy** makes the captain a weekly chip, the **Gameday Captain**: it doubles one player on one Gameday, and since 2025-26 there is no vice-captain ([NBA Fantasy rules](https://nbafantasy.nba.com/help/rules)). This stops teams with more games from multiplying the captain bonus. **IPL's** vice-captain is not a fallback but a standing **1.5×** ([Cric Fantasy Pro](https://cricfantasypro.blogspot.com/2025/03/fantasyguide.html)). **AFL SuperCoach** allows up to four emergencies and, where two cover one position, counts the lower score ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFL_SuperCoach)). Allsvenskan's "Dynamic Duo" triples captain and vice-captain, and Eliteserien's "Two Captains" doubles both, as chips ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2024/03/27/allsvenskan-fantasy-all-you-need-to-know); [Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/03/18/eliteserien-fantasy-2025-preview)).

**For the LCK (inference).** Port FPL's algorithm, defining **"played" as appearing in at least one game of the week's series**. Every LCK starter plays every game his team plays, so auto-subs fire only in two cases: a team rests a starter for a sub or sixth man, or a team blanks in a knockout week. The bench therefore exists mainly for roster risk and Road to MSI. That argues for **a small bench of one or two**, which bears on the open squad-size question. Role legality replaces formation legality: a bench player may come on only into his own role or the flex slot, the way a goalkeeper may replace only a goalkeeper.

Keep a vice-captain under the weekly lock. The AFL loopholes exist only under a rolling lock, so the fallback is harmless here. If the game ever adds in-round captain changes, adopt Euro 2024's rule: manual changes switch off the fallback. A Bundesliga-style best-five would remove even the small bench decision. But the bench is part of the FPL structure already decided, and in an even league it rarely bites anyway.

## Two series a week: sum them, and let uneven weeks be the doubles

**What FPL does.** In a double gameweek a player's two matches simply add up. Postponed fixtures moved out of a gameweek score nothing in it, which creates blanks; rescheduled fixtures pile into later gameweeks as doubles ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com:443/news/1609337); [OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/blank-double-gameweeks-fpl-explained)). These rounds are FPL's main source of planning depth, and chips are saved for them.

**What others do differently.** **MLS Fantasy counts only a player's higher single-match score in a double gameweek** ([MLS](https://www.mlssoccer.com/news/mls-fantasy-back-2020-season-some-major-changes)). The **AFL counts the best 18 of 22 in bye rounds and adds a trade**: two in early byes, three in mid-season byes. Head-to-head leagues play on as normal ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459344/how-afl-fantasy-will-tackle-opening-round-early-byes)). **NBA Fantasy** lets teams with more games simply score more, a strategy known as "schedule streaming" ([NBA Fantasy rules](https://nbafantasy.nba.com/help/rules)). **IPL** drops the weekly round and treats each match as a round under a season transfer pool ([Chase Your Sport](https://www.chaseyoursport.com/fantasy-cricket/ipl-2026-fantasy-league-guide-my11circle-rules-team-creation/11994)). **HLTV** gives seeded Counter-Strike teams "padding" points for skipped rounds ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/news/45436/packed-six-team-blast-playoffs-and-three-more-partner-fantasy-games-open-to-play)).

**For the LCK (inference).** In the regular season, sum versus better-of-two is not a fairness question, because every team plays exactly two series. It is a question of variance and engagement. **Summing** keeps both series worth watching and doubles the signal a manager's choices produce. **Better-of-two** (MLS) halves the cost of a bad series and rewards ceiling, but it makes the second series of the week meaningless for anyone whose player already scored well. So **sum across series**. Do not stack maxima either. If Craig's open per-series toggle picks a player's best game in the series, taking the better of two series on top would make the weekly score a maximum of maxima. That pays pure ceiling, against his aim of not rewarding volatility from long games.

Summing has a useful consequence: in Super Week and knockout weeks, a team that plays twice is a genuine "double" and a team that is out is a genuine "blank". That gives Stand-in and Sixth Man the use they have in FPL. For Road to MSI, four of ten teams blank, so a typical squad holds two or three blank players. AFL's best-N rule does not fix that, because the blanks still score zero. The AFL tools that do port are **an extra free transfer that week**, **bench auto-subs** (because "didn't play" now happens) and **Stand-in** as the natural chip.

If the playoffs become a separate stage game, the earlier recommendation, then Road to MSI is the only blank week left in the main game. The AFL treatment is enough for it. Riot's LCS game averages per weekend ([LCS Fantasy rules](https://lcsofficial.gg/en/rules)), and the earlier research proposed averaging within a series. Both remain compatible with summing across series.

## Chips: a budgeted Stand-in, a bot-lane duo, and blackouts

**What FPL does.** Two sets of four chips (Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost, Triple Captain), one set for each half. The first set must be played by the GW19 deadline and does not carry over. The structure is unchanged for 2026/27 ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4362027); [Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fantasy-premier-league-live-fpl-084500105.html)). Free Hit changes the team for one week, then reverts ([3 Added Minutes](https://www.3addedminutes.com/gaming/fantasy-premier-league-price-changes-how-work-when-happen-and-how-to-make-a-profit-4282850)).

**What others do differently, among reverting chips.** **UCL and Euro "Limitless"** gives unlimited transfers and an **unlimited budget** for one matchday, then the squad reverts ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/09/02/ucl-fantasy-2026-27-chip-strategy-when-to-use-wildcard-limitless); [AllAboutFPL](https://allaboutfpl.com/2024/05/uefa-euro-2024-fantasy-guide-rules-scoring-chips-subs-etc)). **Eliteserien's "Rich Uncle"** is a Free Hit with an unlimited budget ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/03/18/eliteserien-fantasy-2025-preview)). **Sofascore MLS's "Quick Fix"** allows changes for the current gameweek only, then reverts, and its Wildcard is a permanent rebuild, once a half ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/02/18/mls-fantasy-sofascore-how-to-play)). **NBA's "All-Star"** removes the budget for one Gameday ([NBA Fantasy rules](https://nbafantasy.nba.com/help/rules)). A reverting one-week squad is close to universal.

**Role and multi-player chips.** Eliteserien's **"Attack!"** doubles forwards and **"Two Captains"** doubles captain and vice-captain. Allsvenskan's **"Park the Bus"** doubles defenders, and **"Dynamic Duo"** triples captain and vice-captain ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2025/03/18/eliteserien-fantasy-2025-preview); [Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2024/03/27/allsvenskan-fantasy-all-you-need-to-know)). Several games also restrict when chips can be played. **UCL bans chips on matchdays that already have unlimited transfers** ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/09/02/ucl-fantasy-2026-27-chip-strategy-when-to-use-wildcard-limitless)). NBA allows one chip per Gameday, and its Wildcard and All-Star play the moment a point-costing transfer is confirmed, with no cancelling ([NBA Fantasy rules](https://nbafantasy.nba.com/help/rules)). The World Cup game allowed **five boosters in eight rounds** and revealed a Mystery Booster mid-tournament ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/05/26/how-to-play-the-fifa-world-cup-2026-fantasy-game)).

**For the LCK (inference).** Make **Stand-in a budgeted Free Hit (Quick Fix), not Limitless**. With about 50 players and no budget, every Limitless squad becomes the same top five plus captain, so the chip turns into a guaranteed spike, not a decision. With the budget kept, it stays a puzzle. Limitless could live in the playoff stage game, where fewer teams remain and some sameness is expected.

On role chips: when a squad holds one player per role, doubling a role is just a second Triple Captain with a narrower choice. A **two-role chip** does something different. "Bot Duo", say, would double the ADC and support for a week, borrowing "Two Captains". It gives supports, whom kill-based scoring buries, a week where they matter, and it rewards owning a strong bot lane from one team. If Craig wants a fifth chip or a replacement for Sixth Man, this is the candidate the research supports.

Whatever the set, copy three rules. Allow **one chip a week**. **Black out chips in weeks with unlimited transfers**: the first week of each split and any top-up week. And, as FPL does, a Stand-in keeps banked free transfers. Allsvenskan judges its manager of the week without the chip effect ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2024/03/27/allsvenskan-fantasy-all-you-need-to-know)). That is a precedent for the open achievements question too: keep side bonuses in their own column and rank on base points.

## Leagues: classic and head-to-head, an average ghost, a split table

**What FPL does.** Classic leagues rank on total points from a chosen start gameweek. In head-to-head leagues, **an odd number of teams adds an "AVERAGE" opponent** who scores the gameweek average, and the fixture list fixes at the first round, after which nobody can join ([Goal](https://www.goal.com/en-gb/news/fantasy-premier-league-how-do-you-create-join-an-fpl-mini-league-/e4wsppplv8841ofhjnb47n5h6)). Cups are knockouts whose start round depends on entrant numbers, with the final in GW38 ([Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/articles/what-is-the-fantasy-premier-league-cup)). Bundesliga auto-enters every manager into an "FC 27 League" ([Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/de/bundesliga/news/fantasy-manager-2026-27-regeln-spielerwerte-unbegrenzte-transfers-38324)). The AFL keeps head-to-head leagues running normally through byes ([AFL.com.au](https://www.afl.com.au/news/1459344/how-afl-fantasy-will-tackle-opening-round-early-byes)).

**For the LCK (inference).** On top of the decided global and team-supporter leagues, offer private classic and head-to-head leagues, with the average ghost and a locked fixture list, as league data. Add a **per-split table** so Split 2 is a fresh start, matching the chip reset. Run a cup that ends in the last regular week. With about 13 gameweeks, a cup can run only a few rounds before it reaches its final, so its start round should follow entrant numbers as FPL's does. Head-to-head play needs no special handling in Road to MSI week, as AFL leagues show.

## Live features: provisional points, live tables, flags with a news line

**What FPL does.** In 2026/27 **overall rank and mini-league places update live**, including projected bonus. Projected bonus appears once 20 minutes of a match have been played, and **points stay provisional until 09:00 the morning after the final match** while Opta corrections arrive ([Yahoo](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fantasy-premier-league-live-fpl-084500105.html); [Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/articles/fpl-bonus-points)). The provisional-bonus detail comes from one secondary source. The live tables arrived only after years in which LiveFPL supplied live ranks, effective ownership and chip usage ([AllAboutFPL](https://allaboutfpl.com/2022/06/recommended-fpl-tools-accounts-channels-podcasts-websites/)). That gap shows how strong the demand is. The weekly wrap-up shows the Dream Team, the gameweek average, the highest score and the most-captained player ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/dream-team); [fplai](https://fplai.pages.dev/fpl/2025-26/gw-review/gw29/)).

Players carry an availability flag with a chance of playing: **yellow 75%, orange 50% or 25%, red 0%**, plus a news line ([Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/articles/unavailable-players-in-fantasy-premier-league)). Fans find the percentages vague and lean on news sites instead ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/how-to-track-fpl-injury-news)). Bundesliga scores actions live in its app ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1236154617)). It also pays a **daily-login budget bonus**, up to 500k a week ([Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-2025-26-new-feature-daily-bonus-additional-transfer-funds-33074)).

**For the LCK (inference).** Build from day one what FPL added last: **live provisional points, live mini-league tables and effective ownership within your league** ("62% of your league captained Chovy"). Confirm each match day's points at **00:00 UTC (09:00 KST) the next morning**, after the stats source settles, and show a "provisional" badge until then. Because UK fans often watch on VOD, the spoiler-safe "hide scores until I've watched" default from the earlier research comes before any of this.

Flags in esports mean something different from flags in football. Injuries are rare; the real risks are **benched for the sub or sixth man, roster swap, illness or visa**. So use three states (starting as expected, rotation risk, not in the roster) with a **timestamped news line and a source**, not football's percentages. Skip the daily-login budget bonus. It pays activity rather than judgment, and it ties budget to attendance in a free game whose players span every time zone.

## A week in the life of the LCK game

The timeline below is inference. It assumes a regular-season week with series Wednesday to Sunday at 17:00 and about 19:00 KST, and per-gameweek deadlines held as data. UK times run at UTC in winter (the Cup) and UTC+1 in summer (Rounds 1–4). US Eastern runs at UTC−5 and UTC−4.

| When (UTC) | When (KST) | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Mon 00:00 | Mon 09:00 | Sunday's points confirmed; auto-subs and vice-captain applied; gameweek final. Recap: team of the week, average, highest, most captained, achievements column |
| Mon 01:00 | Mon 10:00 | Weekly price change on performance; new breakevens published; next gameweek opens with +1 free transfer (bank cap 3) |
| Mon–Tue | Mon–Tue | Availability flags and news lines updated as teams post line-ups or roster moves; fixture view with opponent strength; transfer planner |
| Tue ~18:00–20:00 local | Wed ~12:00 | Reminder in each user's local evening (Korea gets a midday Wednesday nudge); flag-change alerts for owned players |
| Wed 05:30 | Wed 14:30 | Last-call reminder: one hour to the deadline, with unsaved changes or flagged starters highlighted |
| **Wed 06:30** | **Wed 15:30** | **Deadline: transfers, line-up, captain and chip lock** (90 minutes before the first series) |
| Wed 08:00–~13:00 | Wed 17:00–~22:00 | First two series; live provisional points, live mini-league tables, effective ownership; spoiler-safe mode for VOD watchers |
| Thu 00:00 | Thu 09:00 | Wednesday's points confirmed; the same daily rhythm runs Thursday to Saturday |
| Sun ~13:00 | Sun ~22:00 | Last series ends; the gameweek's provisional final standings |
| Sun–Mon overnight | Mon morning | Data settles; the cycle restarts Monday 00:00 UTC |

The rhythm gives three touchpoints a week without a daily price watch: prices and recap on Monday, the deadline on Wednesday morning, and live matches on five days. That suits a worldwide audience better than FPL's overnight demand-driven price moves. Only US west-coast managers face the awkward slot, since the deadline lands at 22:30–23:30 Tuesday their time.

## Ideas to steal, and traps to avoid

| Steal | From | Why it fits the LCK (inference) |
|---|---|---|
| One deadline, 90 minutes before the first series, stored in UTC per gameweek | FPL | Simple, fair across time zones, already familiar to FPL players |
| Banked free transfers survive Wildcard and Free Hit; "top up to cap" as an admin event | FPL (AFCON 2025/26) | Clean economy; a ready tool for roster swaps and the MSI break |
| Performance prices with tiers, a floor, a weekly cap and early-split freezes | F1, SuperCoach, UCL | A 50-player pool is too small for demand-driven prices |
| Public breakevens as the price predictor | AFL/Footywire, FPL 2026/27 | Turns the price game into a strategy layer at no cost |
| Extra free transfer and auto-subs in blank weeks | AFL | Fixes Road to MSI without new rules |
| Budgeted one-week Stand-in (Quick Fix) | Sofascore MLS, FPL | Keeps a reverting chip a puzzle in a small pool |
| Two-player "Bot Duo" chip | Eliteserien, Allsvenskan | Lifts supports and fits LoL roles |
| No chips in unlimited-transfer weeks | UCL | Stops a free double benefit |
| Head-to-head average ghost, locked fixtures, live mini-league tables | FPL | Cheap, proven, and the feature FPL players most waited for |
| One bounded live change as a chip ("Final Fix") | F1 | Gives some in-week reaction without rolling-lockout loopholes |

The traps are as clear. **Rolling lockout with a worldwide audience** rewards whoever is awake during Korean evenings and reopens AFL's vice-captain and emergency loopholes through subs and sixth men. **Demand-driven, secret prices** in a small pool are noisy and easy to game. **A five-transfer bank in a four-week split** works as a free Wildcard. **An unlimited-budget Limitless chip** in a 50-player pool hands everyone the same squad. **Stacking "best game" with "better series"** pays only ceiling. **Single-role doubling chips** add a second Triple Captain rather than a decision. **Football's injury percentages** do not describe esports availability. And **daily-login rewards** pay attendance, not skill.

## Conclusion

The research turns the question round. FPL's week is built to absorb an irregular calendar, and the LCK's regular season is perfectly regular. Most of FPL's planning machinery therefore matters only at the edges of the season: blanks, doubles, best-N scoring, extra transfers and chips saved for special weeks. What carries the weekly decision in the LCK is the economy: performance prices with public breakevens, cash-cow rookies, a tight transfer bank and chips that reshape a small squad. This favours open, computable rules over FPL's secret ones, which is also the shape this repo already prefers ("the league's rules are data"). Every threshold, allowance, cap and deadline above belongs in league data, ready to be tuned after the LCK Cup beta.

Three open items move because of this research. The **playoffs** look better as a separate stage game, which leaves Road to MSI as the main game's only blank week. **Bench size** can be small, since auto-subs fire only for rotations and blanks. And the **chip set** gains a strong, LoL-native candidate in a bot-lane duo, while Stand-in should keep its budget. None of the numbers here should go into a spec until someone has read the primary rules pages that this session could not open.
