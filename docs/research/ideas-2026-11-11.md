## Top 8

1. **Fearless captain.** You cannot captain the same player twice in a stage. ([Weekly rhythm](#weekly-rhythm))
2. **Weekly Challenge mode.** A fresh five each week under one twist rule, no season squad needed. ([Weekly rhythm](#weekly-rhythm))
3. **Elder v Baron managers' war.** Every manager is drawn into one of the Cup's two groups. ([Social and leagues](#social-and-leagues))
4. **Champion-pool point.** A point for winning on several different champions in a week. ([Scoring](#scoring))
5. **Split Crystal Ball.** Stat predictions locked at the start of a split, settled at the end. ([Weekly rhythm](#weekly-rhythm))
6. **Pick in pairs.** Build a first squad in three taps: a bot lane, a top and jungle, a mid. ([Onboarding](#onboarding))
7. **Draft card with burned champions.** Live bans and picks, with what Fearless has removed for the next game. ([Live experience](#live-experience))
8. **Team identity.** One split-long modifier the manager picks for the whole squad. ([Chips](#chips))

## Scoring

1. **Champion-pool point.** +1 when a player wins on three different champions in a gameweek. Riot's own Crystal Ball already asks "largest champion pool" ([win.gg](https://win.gg/league-of-legends-msi-2026-pick-ems-crystal-ball/)). Fearless Draft makes pool depth the skill LCK fans argue about, and it does not reward long games.
2. **POM point.** The LCK's official Player of the Match gets a bonus point. "Most POMs" is a real LCK award ([lolesports.com](https://lolesports.com/ko-KR/news/2026-lck-awards-voting-method-and-criteria)). It is a bonus the broadcast announces for you, so fans see it land live.
3. **Role floor.** A capped +1 or +2 when a support or jungler passes a threshold of unglamorous work (vision, objective takes). FPL's defensive contribution points did this for centre-backs and made cheap defenders worth owning ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4361991/whats-happening-with-defensive-contribution-points-in-202627-fantasy), unverified). It gives supports a steady floor without kill points.
4. **Coach comeback point.** The coach slot scores when the team wins a game it trailed at 15 minutes. The LCK tested Coach Voice in the 2026 Cup, letting coaches speak to players mid-game ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19953/lck-embraces-structural-change-ahead-of-2026-season)); the three 45-second calls per game are unverified ([lolesports.com](https://lolesports.com/ko-KR/news/lck-coach-voice)). The coach now does something on stage, so the slot can score on it.
5. **Draft-win point for the coach.** The coach scores when the team wins a game where the opponent held First Selection. From 2026 the team with First Selection takes either side or pick order and the other team gets what is left ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/77812-lol-esports-2026-introduces-new-draft-rule-first-selection-and-a-bo5-heavy-first-stand)). Winning from the leftover option is a coaching result no other sport has.

## Weekly rhythm

1. **Fearless captain.** Each player can wear the armband once per stage (once in the Cup's group weeks, say). It copies Fearless Draft, where a champion is gone for the series once played ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/77812-lol-esports-2026-introduces-new-draft-rule-first-selection-and-a-bo5-heavy-first-stand)). It ends the "captain Chovy every week" template with a rule LoL fans already understand.
2. **Weekly Challenge mode.** A separate, optional game: pick a fresh five each week under one twist. FPL Challenge does this with rules like "away players score double" and "players with five years at one club score double", and scores reset weekly so skipping costs nothing ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/fpl-challenge-2026-27-explained)). LCK twists write themselves: red-side week, one-club veterans week, rookies week.
3. **Split Crystal Ball.** Before a split locks, answer a dozen stat questions: most-picked champion, most POMs, shortest game, "will Teemo be picked". Riot added Crystal Ball to MSI 2026 with exactly these categories, scored apart from match picks ([win.gg](https://win.gg/league-of-legends-msi-2026-pick-ems-crystal-ball/)). It gives a manager something still open in the last week.
4. **Super Week as the headline gameweek.** The Cup's week 3 is all Bo5, same seed against same seed, with wins worth double to the group ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19860/riot-outlines-2026-lck-format-changes-adds-super-week-to-lck-cup)). Give it its own banner and doubled series-win bonus. The real league has already built the beta's big week.
5. **Beat-the-line streak.** Each week has a points target; clearing it extends a streak shown on your profile. Sorare's Hot Streaks is this "beat the game" format and Sorare says it was popular ([egamers.io](https://egamers.io/sorare-27-adds-laliga-ea-sports-with-boosted-streaks-and-arena-rewards-to-start-the-season/), unverified). A manager ranked 40,000th still has something to win on Sunday.
6. **Predicted fives from Tuesday.** Show each team's expected starting five before the deadline. Bundesliga Fantasy now publishes predicted line-ups from the Tuesday before each matchday ([Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-season-2026-27-new-features-38255)). Sixth-man swaps are the LCK's version of team news.
7. **Chopped league.** A private-league type where the lowest scorer each week is out. Sleeper, Yahoo and others all added it under different names ([Sleeper](https://sleeper.com/blog/guillotine-fantasy-football), unverified). A ten-manager league lasts exactly one nine-week stage.

## Chips

1. **Team identity.** At the start of a split, pick one style for the whole squad: "Early game", "Objective control", "Teamfight". It adds a small bonus when your players' teams win that way. Dota's TI 2026 fantasy added a coach layer where the manager sets modifiers on the whole team ([Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/dota2/news/the-international-2026-compendium)). It is how LCK fans already describe teams.
2. **First Selection chip.** One chip, two uses, pick one: move your captain after the week's first series, or take one free transfer mid-week. It mirrors the 2026 rule of choosing side or pick order, not both ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/77812-lol-esports-2026-introduces-new-draft-rule-first-selection-and-a-bo5-heavy-first-stand)). The name teaches the real rule.
3. **Mystery chip.** One chip stays hidden until Super Week. The 2026 World Cup game held back a Mystery Booster until the knockouts and fans spent weeks guessing it ([Fantasy Football Scout](https://www.fantasyfootballscout.co.uk/2026/06/24/fantasy-fifa-world-cup-fantasy-mystery-booster-revealed), unverified). A beta needs a reason to come back mid-Cup.
4. **Chips arrive in two drops.** Half the chips at the start, the rest after week one. F1 Fantasy is reported to hand out three before the season and three after the first race ([Yardbarker](https://www.yardbarker.com/formula_one/articles/f1_fantasy_2026_the_complete_beginners_guide/s1_17751_43521622), unverified). A newcomer who picked a squad before signing up is not then met with every chip at once.

## Social and leagues

1. **Elder v Baron managers' war.** On sign-up each manager is drawn into the Elder or Baron group, and the two groups' totals race through the Cup, with Super Week counting double. The real Cup is a group-against-group contest with those names ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19860/riot-outlines-2026-lck-format-changes-adds-super-week-to-lck-cup)). Every beta player has a side from day one, even with no friends in the game.
2. **Rookie League.** A global table only for first-season managers. FPL added one for 2026/27 ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679873/all-you-need-to-know-about-changes-to-fpl-for-202627)). In year one everyone is a rookie; from Split 2 it gives new arrivals a table they can win.
3. **A small public league for everyone.** Auto-place each manager in a public league of about 18. Bundesliga Fantasy does this by league type ([Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-season-2026-27-new-features-38255)). A global table of thousands is not a race; a table of 18 strangers is.
4. **LCK-shaped league play-offs.** A mini-league's top six go into a seeded bracket for the last weeks, shaped like the LCK's own play-offs. Bundesliga added seeded head-to-head play-offs, 1st v 8th ([Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/official-fantasy-manager-season-2026-27-new-features-38255)). The league's story and the fantasy league's story end the same way.
5. **Legend and Rise tables.** After Rounds 1–2, each mini-league splits into its top half (Legend) and bottom half (Rise), each with its own winner. The LCK itself splits this way ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19860/riot-outlines-2026-lck-format-changes-adds-super-week-to-lck-cup)). The bottom half gets a trophy worth chasing.
6. **Career page.** One page with every stage you have played (Cup, Split 1, Split 2), your average and your percentile against all managers. FPL added career history with percentile for 2026/27 ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679873/all-you-need-to-know-about-changes-to-fpl-for-202627)). The LCK year has three short stages, so a career builds fast.
7. **Ballot reveal.** Each week, publish the squads of a few casters and creators after the deadline. The LCK publishes its award votes, and Faker's ballot (all five Gen.G players) was a news story ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/de/articles/faker-votes-for-all-five-gen-g-players-for-the-2026-lck-first-all-pro-team/en), unverified). LCK fans like seeing who voted for whom.

## Onboarding

1. **Pick in pairs.** The first squad is three choices: a bot lane, a top and jungle, a mid. Dota's TI 2026 fantasy drafts in pairs from one team plus a mid ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/dota2/news/78889-dota-2-drops-patch-7-41e-supporter-bundles-fantasy-and-predictions-ahead-of-the-international-2026)). It suits picking before sign-up: three taps, and fans already think in lanes.
2. **Three questions, one squad.** Ask team, favourite role, and "safe or bold", then build the squad. FPL's 2026/27 guided picking asks beginners a few questions and picks for them ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679873/all-you-need-to-know-about-changes-to-fpl-for-202627)). A Worlds-only viewer may know three LCK players.
3. **Start from your bias.** Pick your favourite player (최애) first; the squad is built round him, and he drives your alerts and a profile badge. Korean LoL fandom uses K-pop's "bias" vocabulary ([RFT.GG](https://rft.gg/news/idol-kpop-lck-fans)). It asks the question LCK fans most enjoy answering.
4. **A stamp for turning up.** Locking a first squad fills the first page of the achievement book. Riot gives an emote for entering Crystal Ball ([win.gg](https://win.gg/league-of-legends-msi-2026-pick-ems-crystal-ball/)) and Valve gives rewards to anyone who touches TI fantasy ([Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/dota2/news/the-international-2026-compendium)). The book is not empty on day one.

## LCK flavour

1. **Awards-night ballot.** At the end of the regular season, vote your All-LCK first team, MVP and Rookie of the Year; matching the real panel earns a stamp. The LCK announces these right after the regular rounds and publishes the vote ([lolesports.com](https://lolesports.com/ko-KR/news/2026-lck-awards-voting-method-and-criteria)). It turns the awards show into a game result.
2. **Pocket-pick stamp.** An achievement when your player wins on a champion nobody else in the LCK has played that split. Fifteen of 173 champions went unpicked in tier 1 in 2026 ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/15-out-of-173-lol-champions-went-unpicked-in-tier-1-ahead-of-worlds-2026/en), unverified), and Crystal Ball asks "will Teemo be picked" ([win.gg](https://win.gg/league-of-legends-msi-2026-pick-ems-crystal-ball/)). Odd picks are the moments LCK clips are made of.
3. **Birthday week.** A player's birthday shows as a banner on his card, with a stamp for owning him if he wins that week. Fans buy subway and bus-stop ads and run birthday cafés for LCK players ([RFT.GG](https://rft.gg/news/idol-kpop-lck-fans)). It is fandom the Korean scene already runs on.
4. **Call-up stamp.** An achievement for owning a Challengers League player in the week of his LCK debut. KT Rolster promoted a CL bot laner mid-split to debut against T1 ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/kt-rolster-promote-fenrir-to-the-main-roster-set-to-debut-against-t1/en), unverified). Spotting a rookie early is the LCK fan's proudest claim.
5. **Send a coffee truck.** League-mates can send a virtual coffee truck to the week's top manager; it parks on his team page. Fan groups send real coffee trucks to LoL Park ([RFT.GG](https://rft.gg/news/idol-kpop-lck-fans)). It is praise in the scene's own language.
6. **Hangul slogan banner.** A team-page banner with your team name in hangul and your club's colours, like the hand-made placards and light sticks at LoL Park ([RFT.GG](https://rft.gg/news/idol-kpop-lck-fans)). It is the Korean theme a non-Korean fan can wear.
7. **Hall of Legends chapter.** A page of the achievement book for each Hall of Legends inductee, filled by your players matching his feats. Riot inducts one legend a year, with a third due in 2026 ([GosuGamers](https://www.gosugamers.net/lol/news/77812-lol-esports-2026-introduces-new-draft-rule-first-selection-and-a-bo5-heavy-first-stand)). It ties LCK history to an official, celebratory list.

## Live experience

1. **Draft card with burned champions.** During a series, show bans and picks in order, flag your players' champions, and list what Fearless has removed for the next game. CHZZK's 2026 broadcast added a pick-and-ban timeline ([Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/44411/naver-chzzk-links-live-match-data-to-official-lck-lol-broadcasts)). "What can he still play?" is the question of every game three.
2. **Swing line.** In a head-to-head week, draw your chance of winning the matchup as a line that moves with each game. CHZZK now shows a live win-probability graph on LCK streams ([Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/44411/naver-chzzk-links-live-match-data-to-official-lck-lol-broadcasts)). Korean viewers already watch one for the real match.
3. **Projected bonus from 15 minutes.** Show likely bonus points once a game reaches 15 minutes, updating to the end. FPL adds projected bonus after 20 minutes of each match ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679873/all-you-need-to-know-about-changes-to-fpl-for-202627)). Fifteen minutes is the game's own checkpoint for who is ahead.
4. **Objective ticker.** A strip of first blood, dragons, Barons and towers as they fall, marked when one scores for your coach or jungler. CHZZK's "early control" panel logs key objective takes ([Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/44411/naver-chzzk-links-live-match-data-to-official-lck-lol-broadcasts)). Objectives are the live events between kills.

## Retention

1. **Weekly recap card.** After each week is final, a shareable card with your score, rank move and top player, styled like an LCK broadcast graphic. FPL added Gameweek Recaps for 2026/27 ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4679873/all-you-need-to-know-about-changes-to-fpl-for-202627)). LCK talk lives on X, Reddit and Discord, where a card travels.
2. **Points milestones.** A stamp at every round number of season points, whatever your rank. Valve gives a small reward for every 1,000 fantasy points regardless of placement ([Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/dota2/news/the-international-2026-compendium)). Progress that does not depend on beating anyone.
3. **Internationals Crystal Ball.** During First Stand, MSI and Worlds, a short prediction sheet on how the LCK teams do abroad. Riot ran Crystal Ball at MSI and Worlds 2026 ([win.gg](https://win.gg/league-of-legends-msi-2026-pick-ems-crystal-ball/)), and the LCK's Player of the Year counts international events ([lolesports.com](https://lolesports.com/ko-KR/news/2026-lck-awards-voting-method-and-criteria)). It fills the six-week breaks when no LCK is played.
4. **Invite stamp that waits.** The inviter's stamp lands when the friend finishes three gameweeks, not on sign-up. Sorare moved its referrals to rewards tied to the friend's progress ([cryptogames3d](https://cryptogames3d.com/?p=26192), unverified). It rewards bringing someone who stays.
