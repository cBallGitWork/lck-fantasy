# The 2026 LCK season, week by week (not the Cup)

Read from Liquipedia's LCK 2026 pages on 29 Oct 2026, through its API. Times are Korean (KST, UTC+9). Series counts are whole series, not games.

| Stage | Dates | Teams | Format | Series | Weeks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rounds 1–2 | Wed 1 Apr – Sun 31 May | 10 | double round robin, all Bo3 | 90 (10 a week) | 9 (weeks 1–9) |
| Road to MSI | Sat 6 – Sun 14 Jun | top 6 of Rounds 1–2 | double elimination, Bo5; top 2 go to MSI | 5 | 2 weekends |
| (break: MSI) | mid Jun – late Jul | | | 0 | about 6 |
| Rounds 3–4 | Wed 29 Jul – Sun 23 Aug | 10, in two groups of 5 | double round robin inside each group, Bo3; records carry over | 40 (10 a week) | 4 (weeks 10–13) |
| Play-In | Wed 26 – Fri 28 Aug | 4 | double elimination, Bo5, Fearless Draft; top 2 to the playoffs | 3 | part of one week |
| Playoffs | Sat 29 Aug – Sun 13 Sep | 6 | double elimination, Bo5, Fearless Draft; top 3 go to Worlds | 10 | 3 (with 7–11 Sep empty) |

**The split.** After Rounds 1–2 the top 5 form the **Legend Group** and the bottom 5 the **Rise Group**. From Legend, places 1–2 go straight to playoffs round 2, places 3–4 to playoffs round 1, and 5th to the Play-In. From Rise, 1st goes to Play-In round 1, 2nd–3rd to Play-In round 2, and 4th–5th are out.

**Series per team per week.** In both regular stages every team plays exactly two Bo3 series a week (Wednesday to Sunday, 17:00 and about 19:30 KST). Road to MSI and the post-season are uneven: four teams sit out Road to MSI, and in the playoffs a team can play none, one or several Bo5s in a week.

**Checks and gaps.** Liquipedia lists 9 week blocks of 10 series for Rounds 1–2 and gives the stage an end date of 31 May. The rendered page only had times up to week 7 (17 May), so weeks 8–9 falling on 20–24 and 27–31 May is inferred from the end date. Rounds 3–4 lists weeks 10–13 with 40 series, matching the times found. Hanwha Life Esports won MSI 2026, so if they finished top 3, 4th place also went to Worlds. Finals MVP was Ruler (13 Sep).
