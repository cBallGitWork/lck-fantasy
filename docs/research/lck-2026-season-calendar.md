# The 2026 LCK season, week by week (not the Cup)

Read from Liquipedia's LCK 2026 pages on 29 Oct 2026, through its API. Times are Korean (KST, UTC+9). Series counts are whole series, not games.

**The split.** After Rounds 1–2 the top 5 form the **Legend Group** and the bottom 5 the **Rise Group**. From Legend, places 1–2 go straight to playoffs round 2, places 3–4 to playoffs round 1, and 5th to the Play-In. From Rise, 1st goes to Play-In round 1, 2nd–3rd to Play-In round 2, and 4th–5th are out.

**Series per team per week.** In both regular stages every team plays exactly two Bo3 series a week (Wednesday to Sunday, 17:00 and about 19:30 KST). Road to MSI and the post-season are uneven: four teams sit out Road to MSI, and in the playoffs a team can play none, one or several Bo5s in a week.
