# The LCK leaves FPL-style fantasy wide open

Yes, there is room, and the LCK is a sound first league. As of 28 October 2026 the searches found **no free, season-long fantasy game for the LCK in any language**: not in English, and not in Korean, where fans instead play free match-prediction events (승부예측) run by the streaming platform SOOP, Riot's Worlds Pick'Ems, and buy collectible player cards. Riot's own budget fantasy game covers only the Americas. The LCK's 2026 calendar suits an FPL-style game better than the LEC's three splits: two regular-season blocks of **about 9 and 4 even gameweeks**, in which every team appears to play two Bo3 series a week, Wednesday to Sunday, from **17:00 KST (08:00 UTC, 09:00 UK in summer)**. Unevenness comes from Bo3 length (4–6 games a team a week) and from the knockout stages, not from fixture quirks. The hard design problems are elsewhere. The player pool is only about 50 starters, and nine of ten teams changed their line-ups for 2026. One team (Gen.G) dominates, which makes stacking a single team the obvious exploit. Default kill-based scoring buries supports. And history is harsh: Riot's season-long Fantasy LCS lost **more than 90% of its players** before it closed, partly because leagues died when their organiser left. The game must stay free, with no cash prizes, no team logos and no invented sponsor that echoes the banks, telecoms, insurers, food firms and carmakers that own LCK teams. **Read every specific number below as provisional: this session's network blocked nearly every primary source (Leaguepedia, lolesports, Liquipedia, Inven Global, Sheep Esports, Riot's own sites) and Reddit entirely, so the findings rest on search-result snippets and need checking against the pages before anything is built.**

**Contents:** the gap · the calendar and deadlines · the player pool and role stats · scoring, pricing and role balance · why fantasy games lose players · the Korean fan side · data, legal and second-screen background · recommendations · conclusion.

## Nobody runs season-long LCK fantasy, in English or Korean

Riot's only live fantasy product is in the Americas. **LTA Fantasy launched as a free open beta on 2 April 2025**: a weekly line-up of LTA pros on a **50-gold budget**, prices that move after each weekend on performance, and shared leagues for "teams, streamers, friends" ([Riot Support](https://support-leagueoflegends.riotgames.com/hc/en-us/articles/40441802355603-LTA-Fantasy-FAQ); [Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2025/04/lta-fantasy-sign-up-rules)). When the LTA was dissolved, the game split into LCS and CBLOL versions for 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/League_of_Legends_Championship_of_The_Americas); [LCS Fantasy rules](https://lcsofficial.gg/en/rules); [CBLOL on X](https://x.com/CBLOL/status/2011166468579467604)). Its best scale anchor is a Brazilian report of **about 75,000 active players after the first round** ([JWave](https://www.jwave.com.br/2025/04/lta-fantasy-estreia-75-mil-jogadores-9-mil-ligas/)), backed by Riot's promotion and skin prizes. Outside the Americas, Riot runs only Pick'Em brackets for international events ([EarlyGame](https://earlygame.com/news/gaming/grab-skins-and-emotes-worlds-2025-pickems-are-here-heres-everything-you-need-to-know)). The only LCK fantasy found at all is US real-money daily fantasy: DraftKings slates that mixed LCK and LPL games, mostly documented in 2020–22 ([RotoWire](https://www.rotowire.com/esports/article/dfs-101-how-to-play-league-of-legends-on-draftkings-50118)).

The Korean side confirms the gap rather than filling it. Korean LCK engagement is **match prediction attached to the stream**. SOOP runs 승부예측 ("match-outcome prediction") every season. In 2025 the top predictor in Rounds 3–5 won a Logitech keyboard and the top predictor in each of the Legend and Rise groups won a mouse ([SmartToday](https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/86387)). The playoff event had weekly and season-long tables, an iPad Pro for first place, and platform gems for anyone who predicted every match ([SmartToday](https://www.smarttoday.co.kr/ko-kr/articles/90118); [Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1543367/)). For the 2026 LCK Cup SOOP added watch-to-earn drops and missions ([Dailian](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1595373)). Naver's CHZZK leaned on drops and, from 1 April 2026, a live win-probability and objective panel built on GRID's official data ([Pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/gaming/news/grid-teams-with-naver-to-bring-lol-esports-data-to-chzzk-platform-ec2afd64-b99b-430e-b072-d93f46baf70f)). Demand for player cards is proven: **KOMSCO's official LCK 2026 silver player cards (50 cards, 100 of each) sold out online**, and a 1,000-unit gold run followed Gen.G's title ([Hankyung](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202605183790h); [Insight](https://www.insight.co.kr/news/576128)). The nearest domestic fantasy template is the football league's **K리그 판타지**, which uses graded player cards with random bonus stats ([Goal Korea](https://www.goal.com/kr/%EB%89%B4%EC%8A%A4/k%E1%84%85%E1%85%B5%E1%84%80%E1%85%B3-%E1%84%91%E1%85%A1%E1%86%AB%E1%84%90%E1%85%A1%E1%84%8C%E1%85%B5-%E1%84%87%E1%85%A6%E1%84%90%E1%85%A1%E1%84%89%E1%85%A5%E1%84%87%E1%85%B5%E1%84%89%E1%85%B3-16%E1%84%8B%E1%85%B5%E1%86%AF-%E1%84%89%E1%85%B5%E1%84%8C%E1%85%A1%E1%86%A8/blt8cc55e95dfaa81b7)).

Two caveats weaken "none found". Reddit was unreachable and few Korean-language queries were run, so a small indie game could exist unnoticed. And Riot could bring its LCS app model to Korea at any time, which would be the biggest single threat. The English-speaking audience is the realistic target: **overseas LCK average minute audience rose 20% year on year in 2026** ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/de/articles/lck-viewership-doubles-six-years-post-franchising/en)), and the league moved weekend start times later specifically for European viewers (below).

## Thirteen even gameweeks sit inside an eight-month season

The 2026 LCK ran **14 January to 13 September** in five blocks ([Wikipedia mirror](https://wiki.krisyotam.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic_2026-03/2026_LCK_season)). Only two of them look like FPL.

| Block (2026) | Dates | Format | Fantasy shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| LCK Cup | 14 Jan – ~1 Mar | Two drafted groups play the other group once; weeks 1–2 Bo3, week 3 "Super Week" Bo5; Play-In and playoffs Bo5 double elimination; Fearless draft | Pre-season mini-game; 9 of 10 teams still in the group stage |
| Break | Mar | First Stand, 16–22 Mar | Pause |
| **Rounds 1–2** | **1 Apr – 31 May** | **10-team double round robin, all Bo3** | **~9 even gameweeks** |
| Road to MSI | 6–14 Jun | Top 6, Bo5 knockout | Uneven; 4 teams blank |
| Break | mid-Jun – late Jul | MSI (Daejeon), EWC | Pause, wildcard window |
| **Rounds 3–4** | **29 Jul – 23 Aug** | **Legend (top 5) and Rise (bottom 5) groups, double round robin, Bo3, records carried over** | **~4 even gameweeks** |
| Play-In / Playoffs | 26–28 Aug / 29 Aug – 13 Sep | Byes for Legend 1–2, eliminations | Uneven; blanks grow each week |

Sources: Cup format ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCK/2026/Cup/Group_Battle); [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/20044/2026-lck-cup-kicks-off-jan-14-with-kt-rolster-vs-dn-supers-new-rosters-and-super-week-format)); Rounds 1–2 ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCK/2026/Rounds_1-2)); Road to MSI ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/t1-and-hanwha-life-esports-with-a-double-chance-to-qualify-road-to-msi-bracket-settled/en)); Rounds 3–4 and the advancement rules ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCK/2026/Rounds_3-4)); Playoffs ([rdy.gg](https://rdy.gg/en/lol/news/gen-g-lck-2026-champs)). Gen.G won the Cup undefeated, topped the regular season at **19–7** for a third straight year, and beat HLE 3–1 in the final, its sixth LCK title in four years ([pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/gaming/news/rulers-pentakill-propels-undefeated-geng-to-lck-cup-2026-glory-98a3d032-de9d-4f3b-8e50-686dfc7101d6/); [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/25047/geng-claims-1st-place-in-2026-lck-regular-season-top-spot-for-3-consecutive-years)).

The "two series per team per week" claim is arithmetic, not a published rule. A double round robin of 10 teams is 90 Bo3 series; over nine Wednesday–Sunday weeks of two matches a day that is 10 series a week, or two per team. Rounds 3–4 add eight series per team over four weeks, again two a week, which matches Gen.G's 26 series (18 + 8). So in the regular season **no team gets a "double" or "blank" in FPL's sense**. The variance is inside the series: a Bo3 runs two or three games, so a team plays **four to six games a week**. The LCK also plays heavily: **204 games in Rounds 1–2 alone and about 502 across the whole year**, at roughly 32 minutes and 28–29 kills a game ([gol.gg R1–2](https://gol.gg/tournament/tournament-stats/LCK%202026%20Rounds%201-2/); [gol.gg R3–4](https://gol.gg/tournament/tournament-stats/LCK%202026%20Rounds%203-4/); [Outdrafted](https://outdrafted.lol/q/average-game-length-lck)). Rounds 3–4 were cut from a triple to a double round robin in 2026 to make room for Asian Games preparation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_LCK_season)); with no Asian Games in 2027, a return to three rounds (about two extra gameweeks) is plausible but unannounced. **No 2027 format, schedule or roster list had been published by late October 2026**; only the Naver/SOOP 2026–2030 broadcast deal and MBC's rights to the final through 2027 are confirmed ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/19982/lck-secures-landmark-20262030-partnership-with-naver-and-soop-rebrands-lol-park-and-locks-in-exclusive-broadcast-deal); [rft.gg](https://rft.gg/news/mbc-will-broadcast-the-lck-final-until-2027-as-the-finals-returns-to-the-kspo-dome)). If 2027 repeats the shape, the Cup starts in mid-January, about eleven weeks from now.

### Deadlines are fixed in Korea and drift in the UK

For 2026 the LCK moved weekend starts from 15:00 to **17:00 KST to match weekdays**, a change Korean media attributed to overseas, especially European, viewers ([esports-news.co.uk](https://esports-news.co.uk/?p=174580); [Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/articles/lol-the-lck-is-expected-to-keep-the-same-format-in-2026-integrating-the-asian-games-into-its/en)). The second series starts at about 19:00 KST; sources put it between 19:05 and 19:30, and one Leaguepedia snippet gives earlier times that conflict ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/LCK/2026/Rounds_1-2); [Leaguepedia](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/LCK/2026_Season/Rounds_1-2)). Final-week fixtures on 21–23 August ran at **09:00 and 11:00 BST** ([Gamereactor](https://www.gamereactor.eu/lcks-summer-regular-season-comes-to-a-close-this-weekend-here-are-the-final-fixtures-1762053/)).

| Moment | KST (UTC+9, no DST) | UTC | UK winter (GMT) | UK summer (BST) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Suggested deadline, Wed (inference) | 15:30 | 06:30 | 06:30 | 07:30 |
| First series | 17:00 | 08:00 | 08:00 | 09:00 |
| Second series | ~19:00–19:30 | ~10:00–10:30 | ~10:00–10:30 | ~11:00–11:30 |
| Last game ends, Bo3 goes to three (inference) | ~22:00 | ~13:00 | ~13:00 | ~14:00 |

Korea has no daylight saving, so a deadline stored in UTC or KST stays fixed while its UK time moves by an hour at the UK clock changes. In practice the Cup (January–March) runs on GMT and Rounds 1–4 (April–September) on BST. A 06:30–07:30 UK deadline means most UK managers will set line-ups the evening before, so a **Tuesday-evening UK reminder** matters more than it does in FPL. The Super Week and knockout weeks may start on other days and need per-gameweek deadlines held as data.

## Fifty players, and nine of ten line-ups changed

The 2026 teams were Gen.G, Hanwha Life Esports (HLE), T1, KT Rolster, Dplus KIA, BNK FearX, Nongshim RedForce, DN SOOPers (formerly DN Freecs), HANJIN BRION (formerly OKSavingsBank BRION, tri-code BRO) and Kiwoom DRX (tri-code KRX) ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/20340/lck-reveals-2026-regular-season-rosters-as-brion-and-drx-rebrand)). The starting pool is therefore **about 50 players, five per role**. Roster churn between seasons is heavy: sources disagree on whether only Gen.G or also BNK kept its 2025 five, but either way **at least eight teams changed**, and HANJIN BRION replaced all five ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/en/lol/articles/all-the-new-lck-players-for-2026/en); [rft.gg](https://rft.gg/news/hanjin-brion-part-ways-with-fisher-sign-loki-through-2027)). HLE alone added Kanavi from the LPL and Gumayusi from T1, while KT's bot lane moved wholesale to DN SOOPers. No reliable full 2026 roster table could be assembled: the one table that surfaced is partly stale and conflicts with transfer reports, for example on whether Diable or Taeyoon played ADC for Nongshim ([esports.net](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/lol-esports-2026-rosters/); [bo3.gg](https://bo3.gg/da/players/40bbf535/maps)). The 2027 off-season is already moving; Leaguepedia listed a Kellin move from BNK FearX to KT on 18 October 2026 ([Leaguepedia](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/Roster_Swaps/Current/Korea)).

Mid-season churn concentrates at the bottom of the table. BNK FearX named academy mid Daystar a sixth man and later reinstated VicLa ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/de/articles/lol-bnk-fearx-s-completed-2026-lck-roster/en); [pulse.bot](https://www.pulse.bot/gaming/news/vicla-returns-to-bnk-starting-lineup-against-dplus-kia-ccce53c6-bc89-4d50-90f7-1b1b9a225b8b/)), DN SOOPers signed jungler Sharvel from DK's academy mid-season ([cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/dn-soopers-signs-sharvel-lck-roster/)), and Sheep Esports describes main-to-academy swaps as "common in the LCK" ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/lol-lck-vital-to-replace-jiwoo-in-nongshim-redforce-s-roster-today-against-kt-rolster/en)). The top three teams barely rotated. A game therefore needs three things FPL handles quietly: a rule for a picked player who does not play (Rifty scores such a player zero ([Rifty rules](https://rifty.lol/rules))), a way to add newly called-up players to the pool mid-season, and a stable player key. Player tags change and teams rebrand, so the repo's "persist the season-stable code" rule maps to the lolesports `esportsPlayerId` or a Leaguepedia page, not to a name or a per-season ID; that mapping is reasoning, not something verified this session.

### Role stats show why supports fall behind

Only per-player figures were retrievable, not league-wide role averages. Gen.G's top laner Kiin held a **7.0 KDA in the Cup but 4.1 in Rounds 1–2**, with a 21.8% damage share ([gol.gg Cup](https://gol.gg/players/player-stats/1115/season-ALL/split-ALL/tournament-LCK%20Cup%202026/champion-ALL/); [gol.gg R1–2](https://gol.gg/players/player-stats/1115/season-ALL/split-ALL/tournament-LCK%202026%20Rounds%201-2/champion-ALL/)). Faker ran a 62.1% kill participation and 21.7% damage share in the playoffs ([gol.gg](https://gol.gg/players/player-stats/48/season-ALL/split-ALL/tournament-LCK%202026%20Season%20Playoffs/champion-ALL/)). The support Life sat at **57.6% kill participation but only 10.9% damage share** ([gol.gg](https://gol.gg/players/player-stats/1906/season-ALL/split-ALL/tournament-LCK%20Cup%202026/champion-ALL/)). The pattern is the familiar one: carries share kills and damage roughly equally at about 21–22%, supports match their kill participation but take half the damage and few kills, and KDA swings hard with team results. Any scoring table built on kills will rank supports last unless it pays for assists, vision or kill participation. The fix is to pull the Oracle's Elixir 2026 LCK CSV or gol.gg role pages and simulate candidate weights before launch; no published study of role balance under current scoring was found.

## Riot's newer scoring flattens carries and averages per game

Two scoring families exist. The daily-fantasy family (DraftKings, HotStreak, Sleeper, the old Fantasy LCS) pays roughly **kill +3, assist +2, death −1, CS +0.02**, plus +2 for 10 or more kills or assists in a game ([FanSided](https://fansided.com/2019/05/12/draftkings-league-legends-introduction/); [HotStreak](https://help.hotstreak.gg/en/articles/9656535-league-of-legends-how-are-fantasy-scores-calculated)). Under it, mid and ADC score most and support least; one analysis found ADC highest and support lowest in both wins and losses, with support averaging 27.3 points in wins and 15.1 in losses ([numberFire](https://numberfire-zack-attack-webile.use1.pdm-prd.com/esports/news/31389/introduction-to-daily-fantasy-league-of-legends-basics-strategy-stats-stacking-and-more)), and captaincy advice converges on ADC or mid ([RotoGrinders](https://rotogrinders.com/articles/league-of-legends-dfs-quick-tips-for-draftkings-contests-3330886)). One older source disagrees, ranking supports second under ESL's scoring ([ESL](https://esl.com/article/the-top-players-of-esl-fantasy-lcs/)), which shows the ranking is a product of the table, not the role.

Riot's own 2025–26 games use a flatter base, **kill +1.5, assist +1, death −1, first blood +1, win +1**, then add two or three threshold bonuses per role worth about 1.5–3 points each. Top gets solo kills and damage-share or tanking bonuses; jungle gets dragons, Barons and 75% kill participation; mid gets 30% damage share and CS pace; ADC gets CS pace and damage per minute; support gets 10+ assists, 75% kill participation and first dragon ([Strafe](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/lta-fantasy-returns-for-split-3-after-its-successful-debut/)). Sources conflict on whether the kill-participation threshold is 70% or 75%. Split 3 added a continuous lane-lead bonus of **0.001 points per gold ahead at 14 minutes** and objective-steal points (Baron or Elder +4, Atakhan +3, Dragon or Herald +2) ([LoL Esports](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/lta-fantasy---split-3-whats-new/); [Strafe](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/lta-fantasy-split-3-new-team-diversity-bonus-enhanced-scoring-system-and-more/)), and the 2026 LCS game pays supports their vision score per minute ([LCS Fantasy rules](https://lcsofficial.gg/en/rules)). Threshold bonuses are easy to explain but create cliff edges; the continuous ones avoid that.

**Per game or per series is the decision that matters most for the LCK.** Riot's LCS game **averages each player's points across the games they played that weekend** to cancel out series length ([LCS Fantasy rules](https://lcsofficial.gg/en/rules)). DraftKings sums every map; Pick6 counts only the guaranteed maps of a series ([DraftKings Pick6](https://pick6.draftkings.com/pick6-rules-and-scoring-league-of-legends)). Summing in the LCK rewards a player whose team goes to three games, including in a loss, and turns a 2–0 sweep into a worse fantasy week than a 2–1 defeat. Pure weekly averaging removes that luck but also erases the knockout-stage difference between a team that plays and one that does not.

On pricing, Riot's game moves prices on **performance after each weekend**, and a manager's budget rises or falls with roster value ([Esports Insider](https://esportsinsider.com/2025/04/lta-fantasy-sign-up-rules)). FPL moves prices on net transfers in roughly £0.1m steps ([Goal](https://www.goal.com/en/lists/explained-how-fantasy-premier-league-prices-change/bltdfa936ba3ddb7dcf)). Rifty uses a Spanish-style market with buyout clauses between managers ([Rifty on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.galan.rifty&hl=en_US)). With five roles and Riot's 50-gold budget, the average player costs 10. The exact LTA price formula, step size and cap were not found.

The **team slot** is the LoL analogue of an FPL defence: DraftKings pays turret +1, dragon +2, Baron +3, first blood +2, win +2 and +2 more for a win under 30 minutes ([FanSided](https://fansided.com/2019/05/12/draftkings-league-legends-introduction/)), and the old Fantasy LCS had a smaller version ([GameSkinny](https://www.gameskinny.com/news/league-of-legends-to-offer-official-fantasy-lcs-beta/)). CBLOL's 2026 game added a **coach slot scored as the team's average** ([Pichau Arena](https://www.pichauarena.com.br/lol/fantasy-lol-o-que-mudou-cblol/)). The LoL captain norm is **×1.5, not ×2**, at 1.5× salary on DraftKings ([Sports Gambling Podcast](https://www.sportsgamblingpodcast.com/2020/04/07/league-of-legends-draftkings-picks-4-8-20-introduction-to-lol-dfs/)); a ×2 captain on carry-heavy scoring would make "always captain the ADC" even more automatic. **No LoL product found has FPL-style chips**, so chips are untested territory rather than a known failure.

### One-team stacks are the exploit, and the LCK makes it worse

Riot's Split 2 leaderboards were topped by line-ups drawn mostly from a single team, because five teammates share wins, objectives and assists. Riot's fix for Split 3 was a **team-diversity bonus from +5% for players from two teams up to +20% for five** ([Strafe](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/lta-fantasy-split-3-new-team-diversity-bonus-enhanced-scoring-system-and-more/)). The LCK amplifies the problem: Gen.G went undefeated through the Cup and has won six titles in four years, so a Gen.G five-stack would be the default template in a game with no counter. FPL's "maximum three per club" is the natural equivalent; with a five-player starting line-up, a cap of two per team is the tighter option.

## Season-long games die when organisers leave and weeks feel settled

Riot's own post-mortem is the clearest evidence. When it closed Fantasy LCS for 2019, Riot said the experience was "far from where it wanted it to be", **users had fallen more than 90% from year one**, leagues depended on one organiser so that **when that person dropped out the friend group went too**, and an in-house rebuild had been shut down because running fantasy cost too much outside Riot's core business ([Riot Nexus Dec 2018](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2018/12/the-future-of-fantasy/); [Riot Nexus Jan 2019](https://nexus.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/2019/01/the-future-of-fantasy/); [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/riot-games-plans-to-have-something-in-place-for-fantasy-lcs-for-spring-2020)). Neglect and fading interest cannot be separated in that record, and Riot blamed itself. A hobby project has the same exposure: if maintaining it becomes a chore, it dies the same way.

General fantasy data points the same way. A 2024 poll of US fantasy-football players put **week 7 as the average point of quitting**, with 16% gone by week 6 ([Talker Research](https://talkerresearch.com/fantasy-football-struggle-most-give-up-before-week-10/)). At VALORANT's LOCK//IN 2023 Pick'Ems, **11.3% of entries scored zero**, largely from people forgetting to pick, which is a deadline failure rather than a loss of interest ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/?p=1309743)). The LCK's 06:30–07:30 UK deadline makes that failure mode likelier. On the positive side, FPL's 2026/27 headline feature is **league rankings that update in real time** ([Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4680230/whats-new-in-202627-fantasy-league-rankings-change-in-real-time)), and third-party FPL tools sell live provisional points, rival tracking and per-event push notifications ([OneFPL](https://onefpl.com/blog/fpl-live-ranks-onefpl); [Fantasy Football Hub](https://www.fantasyfootballhub.co.uk/whats-new/coming-soon-live-notifications-and-the-feed)).

The esports games that kept players show what fans responded to. Riot changed LTA Fantasy in Split 3 explicitly on fan feedback: the diversity bonus, a fixed substitution system instead of a bench, league admin tools, a choice between **continuous and limited scoring** per league, and a "sell all" button for weekly rebuilds ([LoL Esports](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/lta-fantasy---split-3-whats-new/)). CBLOL's 2026 relaunch leaned on light progression: missions that unlock titles and icons, achievements, and a **competition between fan bases** ([CBLOL on X](https://x.com/CBLOL/status/2011166468579467604)). Brazilian fans framed the game as "Cartola do LoL", after their national football fantasy game ([Donos do League on X](https://x.com/donosdoleague/status/1878871182621216976)), which suggests a familiar football-fantasy frame helps adoption; for UK players that frame is FPL itself. Rifty, the one European season-long app, has too few App Store ratings to show a score ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/rifty/id6744029216)). No usable app-store review text, Reddit threads, YouTube comments or co-streamer reactions were retrievable, so the specific complaints of LoL fantasy players remain unknown.

## Korean fans watch on two platforms and cheer like idol fandoms

Since 2026 Korean-language LCK streams are **exclusive to Naver's CHZZK and SOOP for 2026–2030**; YouTube keeps only delayed highlights, and Naver bought naming rights to the arena, now "CHZZK LoL Park" in Jongno, Seoul ([Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/912/lck-leaves-youtube-soop-vs-chzzk-lock-in-battle); [Sports Khan](https://sports.khan.co.kr/en/article/202512161324297); [Byline Network](https://byline.network/2025/12/16_1928873/)). Korean average minute audience was reported at **634,000**, up 42% ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/de/articles/lck-viewership-doubles-six-years-post-franchising/en)), a single unverified figure. Streamers co-stream on SOOP, and SOOP has rewarded viewers for watching co-streams ([iNews24](https://www.inews24.com/view/1678207)). English viewers get Riot's LCK Global feed ([Leaguepedia](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/LCK/2026_Season/Cup)), and one unverified source says Caedrel co-streams the full LCK season ([Wikipedia mirror](https://wiki.krisyotam.com/content/wikipedia_en_all_nopic_2026-03/Caedrel)). Live fandom looks like K-pop fandom: hand-drawn placards made in the venue's communal zone, team light sticks, nickname banners in idol typography, and fan-bought subway and LoL Park ads for player birthdays such as "Chovy Day" ([KOCIS](https://www.kocis.go.kr/eng/webzine/202403/sub05.html); [RFT.gg](https://rft.gg/news/idol-kpop-lck-fans); [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/datalab/articles/16679/happy-chovy-day-scenes-from-how-geng-and-their-fans-help-celebrate-chovys-birthday-at-lol-park)). T1's home-ground cheer kit includes flags, card sheets and chants borrowed from KBO baseball ([T1 bstage](https://t1.bstage.in/contents/667d1a508f06de482fa700d6)).

### Safe themes celebrate the league, risky ones joke about people

The sourced, affectionate material is strong. Faker's nickname **"Unkillable Demon King" (불사대마왕)** is one he has embraced: he called it childish at first but said "it grew on me" ([Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/9329/worlds-2019-skt-faker-on-his-nickname-the-unkillable-demon-king-i-thought-it-was-childish-at-first-but-it-grew-on-me-i-think-it-fits-me-well-especially-when-i-recently-had-0-deaths)). The **Telecom War (통신사 대전)** between SK Telecom's T1 and KT dates to 2013, reached its first Worlds final in 2025 (T1 won 3–2) and opened the 2026 regular season ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/lol-what-is-the-telecom-war/en); [Xportsnews](https://www.xportsnews.com/article/2129814)). Caster Jun (전용준) has cast the LCK since 2012 and is famous for shouted openings ([Liquipedia](https://liquipedia.net/leagueoflegends/Caster_Jun)). Placards, light-stick colours, player birthdays, PC bangs and "롤" as the Korean name for the game are all natural, positive hooks. The theme should still name rivalries and nicknames rather than caricature the people: a "Demon King" badge is homage, a cartoon demon wearing Faker's face is mockery and uses a real person's likeness.

The clear "avoid" is **대리 (boosting)**. It is a misconduct category, not a meme: KT dropped a player over a boosting allegation ([Inven Global](https://labs.invenglobal.com/articles/2000/kt-removes-chan-dong-chance-lee-from-roster-due-to-boosting-allegation-and-player-misconduct)), and Korea criminalised paid boosting (a 2019 law change known from background knowledge, not verified this session). Jokes about bans, fines or toxicity ([esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/league-of-legends/lck-jungler-clid-banned-for-1-year-over-inappropriate-comments/); [Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/ruler-fined-for-verbal-abuse-in-solo-queue/en)) fall in the same bucket. The rest of the avoid list is judgement rather than sourced finding: LCK-versus-LPL nationalist framing, "robotic grinder" stereotypes, fake Hangul, and mixing in Chinese or Japanese visuals. Everyday slang such as 화이팅, 대박, 꿀잼 and 한타, with English glosses, is likely safe flavour but was not verified here, and nor was chimaek as an LCK viewing habit.

### Invented sponsors must not echo the owners of LCK teams

LCK teams are corporate brands, so the list of names to avoid is long. **Woori Bank** has been the LCK's title sponsor since 2019 and renewed on 12 January 2026; it also sponsors T1 and sells T1-fan banking products ([Asiae](https://cm.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026011309063241637); [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/1143/woori-bank-signs-official-sponsorship-deal-with-t1)). The 2026 Cup sponsors were **Woori Bank, Logitech, Secretlab, CGV and Golden Dew** ([Leaguepedia](https://lol.fandom.com/wiki/LCK/2026_Season/Cup)). Team owners and name sponsors cover a telecom pair (SK Telecom, KT), a life insurer (Hanwha Life), a ramen maker (Nongshim), a carmaker (Kia), a regional bank (BNK), a securities firm (Kiwoom), an airline and logistics group (Hanjin) and a streaming platform (SOOP) ([Sheep Esports](https://www.sheepesports.com/es/lol/articles/oksavingsbank-brion-renames-to-hanjin-brion/en); [Twogpedia](https://twogpedia.com/News/Hanwha_Life_Esports_Partners_with_CHZZK)). A fake "___ Bank", a W-shaped mark, a "Tel" or noodle brand, a cinema chain, a peripherals or gaming-chair maker, or anything evoking CHZZK, SOOP or Naver would read as parody of a real backer. Wholly whimsical neighbourhood businesses (a pojangmacha street stall, a bakery, a fictional PC-bang chain) with a visible "fictional sponsors" note are the safer pattern; that is inference, as no source addressed it.

**Riot's "Legal Jibber Jabber" policy** allows free fan projects to use Riot IP with a required disclaimer ("…not endorsed or sponsored by Riot Games"), permits passive ads, forbids paywalls, crowdfunding and a business entity, and can be revoked at any time; Riot has sent cease-and-desists to fan projects before ([Riot Legal](https://www.riotgames.com/en/legal); [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/riot-shuts-fan-made-league-of-legends-legacy-project-denies-complaints-of-extortion)). The policy text found dates from 2018. It does not cover team logos, which belong to the teams and their parent companies; no LCK-specific brand guideline for fans was found. Team names as plain text are the safe default.

## Data, legal and second-screen findings, applied to the LCK

The earlier landscape research still holds and needs only an LCK lens. **Riot's official live data is commercial-only through GRID**, its exclusive partner since a December 2023 deal reportedly for three years ([riotesportsdata.com](https://riotesportsdata.com/en-us/league-of-legends/); [Sportico](https://www.sportico.com/business/tech/2023/riot-games-grid-data-deal-1234748582/)), and GRID's free tier excludes LoL ([GRID](https://grid.gg/what-is-grid-open-access/)). Naver's CHZZK panel runs on that GRID feed, which confirms the official data covers the LCK in depth. A free build relies on the unofficial lolesports feed for live frames ([openapi.yaml](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vickz84259/lolesports-api-docs/master/openapi.yaml)) and on Oracle's Elixir daily CSVs, which include the LCK ([Oracle's Elixir](https://oracleselixir.com/tools/downloads)), plus Leaguepedia for rosters. The live feed lacks vision score, absolute damage and first-blood flags, so Riot-style support vision points and first-blood bonuses must be settled post-match. Whether the feed's shared key still worked in October 2026 was not tested. Riot's developer policies ban betting functionality ([Riot General Policies](https://developer.riotgames.com/policies/general)), and UK prize-competition law draws the line once entrants pay ([Irwin Mitchell](https://www.irwinmitchell.com/business/licensing/betting-gaming/prize-draws)), so "free, no cash prizes" remains the safe shape. Korea regulates betting strictly, and its prize-backed predictions run on licensed platforms; the Korean legal position was not researched.

The LCK changes the second-screen calculation. Matches run during the UK working morning, so a large share of UK fans will watch on VOD later in the day; the earlier research found no evidence for or against a spoiler-free mode, but **for the LCK a "hide scores until I've watched" default looks essential rather than optional** (inference). Live broadcasts run on a delay of a few minutes ([PandaScore](https://developers.pandascore.co/docs/low-latency-feed-overview)), the LCK's actual delay was not found, and Document Picture-in-Picture gives a pop-out live window on desktop Chrome and Edge without an extension ([Chrome docs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/web-platform/document-picture-in-picture)).

## Recommendations for an LCK-first design

Everything in this table is design inference built on the findings above, except where the "Why" column cites a sourced fact.

| Priority | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Verify before building**: probe the lolesports endpoints, read the Leaguepedia and Oracle's Elixir terms, pull a full 2026 LCK roster and fixture list from Leaguepedia, search Reddit and Korean sites for competitors, and watch for the 2027 LCK calendar (Riot usually publishes in December or January) | Every figure here came through search snippets; the 2027 Cup likely starts mid-January |
| 2 | **Run the main game on Rounds 1–2 and 3–4** (~13 gameweeks), with a Wednesday 06:30 UTC deadline stored in UTC, per-gameweek deadline overrides as data, and a Tuesday-evening UK push reminder | Even two-series weeks fit FPL; the deadline lands at 06:30–07:30 UK, and forgotten picks are a known churn source |
| 3 | **Treat the Cup as a pre-season mini-game and the knockouts as optional bonus rounds**, with a free wildcard in the June–July break | The Cup is Fearless with Bo5 Super Week and full rosters reset; knockouts create blanks that suit Free Hit-style chips |
| 4 | **Score per game, average within a series, sum across series**, so a 2–0 sweep is not worse than a 2–1 loss and a team that plays twice in a knockout week still counts twice | Riot averages per weekend to cancel series length, but pure averaging erases knockout blanks |
| 5 | **Start from Riot's flatter table** (kill 1.5, assist 1, death −1) with a few role bonuses, prefer continuous ones (lane gold lead, vision per minute) over cliffs, and tune weights on the 2026 Oracle's Elixir LCK data until role means sit within about 10–15% | Kill-based tables rank supports last; Riot moved this way; League Next may shift the meta, so weights stay league data |
| 6 | **Squad of five roles plus a team or coach slot, captain ×1.5, at most two players per LCK team** | Stops the Gen.G five-stack; ×2 captaincy would make "captain the ADC" automatic |
| 7 | **Performance-based price changes, set once a week**, with the first prices hand-set after the off-season | Rosters reshuffle almost completely each winter; FPL's demand-driven model needs a larger pool |
| 8 | **Handle non-starters and call-ups**: a fixed-substitute rule, zero for a picked player who does not play, a mid-season pool-add process, and player identity keyed on a stable ID | Sixth men and academy swaps are common at the bottom of the table |
| 9 | **Build for retention, not reach**: live provisional points with confirmed scores after the match, a spoiler-safe "hide scores" default, public and matchmade leagues so no league depends on one organiser, and per-league continuous or limited scoring | Fantasy LCS collapsed partly through organiser-dependent leagues; week 7 is the typical quit point |
| 10 | **Add a light 승부예측-style match prediction side mode and fan-base rivalry**, with cosmetic rewards only | It is the format Korean fans already play, and CBLOL leaned on missions and fan-base competition |
| 11 | **Keep the Korean theme as a removable skin**: placards, light-stick colours, player-birthday days and the Telecom War as content; no 대리 or scandal jokes, no team logos, wholly original sponsors outside banking, telecoms, insurance, food, cars, airlines, peripherals and streaming; the Jibber Jabber disclaimer; and a paid or credited Korean LoL fan reviewing all copy and Hangul | Theme breakage must never touch scoring; team owners and Woori Bank make most sponsor categories risky |
| 12 | **Stay free, with no entry fees or cash prizes**, and get legal advice before any money or sponsor enters | Riot's betting ban, Jibber Jabber's monetisation limits and UK prize-competition law all change at that point |

## Conclusion

Choosing the LCK changes the shape of the problem more than the size of the opportunity. The calendar becomes easier: two clean, even regular-season blocks replace three short LEC splits, and the uneven weeks are confined to knockouts, where they make chips interesting instead of unfair. The game becomes harder in two other places. The small, dynasty-dominated pool means template squads and one-team stacks arrive by week two unless the rules (team caps, a team slot, captaincy at ×1.5, per-game averaging) create the decisions that FPL's 600-player pool creates by itself. And the UK-morning schedule turns two features that were nice-to-haves for an evening LEC game, deadline reminders and spoiler-safe scoring, into the core of retention.

The Korean evidence adds a further insight: Korea's existing engagement habit is a quick prediction attached to the live stream, not season-long management. An English-language LCK game will win on depth for overseas fans who already know FPL, and a light prediction mode is the cheap bridge to the habit Korean fans already have. None of this is safe to build on until the primary pages, which this session could not open, confirm the rosters, fixtures, times and Riot's data and fan-content terms for 2027.
