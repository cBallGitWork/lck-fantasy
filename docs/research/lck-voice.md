# The Broadcast voice: how LCK fans and casters talk

Research for Flol, 18 Nov 2026. Background for the writer agent, not product. Nothing here is decided: Craig picks what Flol keeps.

## Summary

1. The LCK's English coverage counts in series and games: "2-0 sweep", "full-set thriller", "reverse sweep", "took game 1". Headlines name a player, a champion and a number.
2. Inven Global, translating Korean press, says "set" for a game ("won the first set"). Flol says game, so the writer must not copy "set".
3. The LCK's per-series award is POM (Player of the Match); POG (Player of the Game) is the older per-game award and is still common in fan talk. Flol says series, so "POM" needs a Flol word or Craig's call.
4. Fans speak in short verdicts: "bot diff", "jungle gap", "throw", "clutch", "Worlds buff". Insults about players ("int", "washed") are common but unsafe on a product.
5. Korean words the English scene really uses are few: hyung, "fighting", Faker's "Unkillable Demon King", 중꺾마 ("the unbreakable spirit"). K-pop words (daebak, maknae, aegyo, oppa) are widely known but rarely LCK.
6. FPL's site voice is flat and exact: "Make Transfers", "Confirm", "Deadline: Fri 6 Nov, 18:30", "The game is updating and will be available soon". No jokes in buttons or errors.
7. Riot's client words ("Lock In", "Victory", "Defeat", "Honor", "Summoner") read as a game menu, not a broadcast.

## Fans in match threads

Tone: blunt, fast, a verdict in two or three words, irony on top. Fans rank players and laugh at losses, their own team's included.

- **Thread format.** r/leagueoflegends post-match threads are titled "Team vs. Team / Event - Stage / Post-Match Discussion", with a "Winner:" line and game time. Source: archived thread, [DWG KIA vs. Cloud9 / MSI 2021](https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/3739/feed_items/3021376). The LCK ones read "T1 vs. KT Rolster / LCK 2024 Spring - Week 5 / Post-Match Discussion". Source: search listing of [r/leagueoflegends 1ayoshl](https://lr.eu.psf.lt/r/leagueoflegends/comments/1ayoshl/t1_vs_kt_rolster_lck_2024_spring_week_5_postmatch/krw3yfv).
- **Diff and gap.** "bot diff", "jungle diff", "mid gap": one side's player was much better. Source: esports.net, [LoL terminology](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/league-of-legends-terminology/); Dignitas, [League of Lingo](https://dignitas.gg/articles/league-of-lingo-a-glossary-of-league-of-legends-slang).
- **Throw.** "Losing a game you had clearly won." Source: same glossaries.
- **Copium, hopium.** Defending a losing team is being "high on copium"; hopium mocks false hope. Source: esports.net, [copium meaning](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/copium-meaning/); Win.gg, [copium and hopium](https://win.gg/news/what-are-copium-and-hopium-on-twitch-we-explain-for-you/).
- **Worlds buff.** T1 plays far better at Worlds than in the LCK. Fans also say "T1 Clutch". Source: Fragster, [why T1 get stronger at Worlds](https://www.fragster.com/why-are-t1-becoming-stronger-when-they-enter-league-of-legends-world-stage/).
- **Korean fan jokes, translated.** "Chovy, stop bullying the elderly." (Chovy beating Faker.) "If the LPL is DUDUDUNGA, LCK is WASSA WASSA." "Thank you, Clid. I believed in you." Source: Inven Global KR Reactions, [17791](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/17791/2022-lck-summer-finals-kr-reactions-chovy-stop-bullying-the-elderly), [11301](https://www.invenglobal.com/lol/articles/11301/msc-finals-kr-reactions-if-the-lpl-is-dududunga-lck-is-wassa-wassa), [13767](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/13767/lck-finals-kr-reactions-bdd-and-clid-the-look-alikes-threw-together).
- **Moment memes.** Doran's "Krug lord" (dying to jungle camps), later put on a Worlds skin. Source: esports.gg, [T1 Worlds 2025 skin references](https://esports.gg/guides/league-of-legends/all-references-t1-lol-worlds-2025-skins/). Gumayusi's ramen analogy. Source: Dexerto, [ramen analogy](https://dexerto.com/league-of-legends/t1-gumayusi-sets-league-community-ablaze-with-bizarre-ramen-analogy-2273809).
- **Fan culture is idol-like.** "In Korea, fans treat us like K-pop idols. In Europe, we're treated like friends." Fans bring plushies, banners and team-coloured light sticks. Source: RFT, [More idol than athlete](https://rft.gg/news/idol-kpop-lck-fans).

Safe enough for a product (light, about play, not people): sweep, reverse sweep, full set, diff, gap, hard carry, clutch, throw, Worlds buff, POG, copium (in fun, about your own picks).

Avoid on a product:

- **int, inting** ("intentional feeding"): an accusation of cheating, and "a serious accusation" even between players. Source: esports.net, LoL terminology above.
- **washed**: an insult to a named player. K.
- **truck** aimed at a player: tied to real harassment of Gumayusi. Source: [truck-culture.md](truck-culture.md).
- **KEKW and Twitch emotes**: tied to one streaming site and a real man's face. Source: AFK Gaming, [what KEKW means](https://afkgaming.com/esports/news/6592-what-does-kekw-mean-in-twitch-chat-and-where-did-it-originate).
- Anything about a player's looks, age or mental health. The Faker breakdown clip drew both "#We_Stand_For_Faker" and mockery. Source: Sportskeeda, [Internet rallies in support of Faker](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/we-ve-there-internet-rallies-support-of-t1-faker-post-game-breakdown).
