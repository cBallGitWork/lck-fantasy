## G2 Esports

**Kit:** 2026 Main Jersey (regular season). **Saw a photo:** yes.

1. **Base.** Red #C0262A with a tonal print of darker red (#8E1A1E) thin lines that split the front into big irregular plates, like samurai armour or cracked lacquer.
2. **Panels.** Black side panels (#111111) from the armpit to the hem, about 15% of the shirt's width each side, curving in at the waist. Black angular shards cut into the shoulders and outer sleeves. A thin white piping line runs from each side of the collar over the chest in a curve and down to the hem, separating red from black.
3. **Collar and cuffs.** Black crew collar with a small V-split at the front and a white inner neck panel; black cuff bands.
4. **Crest and maker.** Crest small, in white outline, on the left chest (viewer's right). Maker's white wing-shaped "V" mark on the right chest.
5. **Sponsors.** No big chest sponsor: the front is clean. Red Bull patch (red bulls, yellow sun, red text: all Red Bull's colours) on the right collarbone; Logitech G in white on the left collarbone; white sponsor text on the sleeves. Keep the two small collarbone patches, redraw as made-up marks.
6. **Spot first.** Red with black sides; the cracked armour-plate lines; the bare chest with just two small marks.
7. **Alternate.** Prestige jersey for international events: same cut in dark green (#1F4A3E), black shards and sides, pink (#E8A0B8) fade at the hem and sides.

**Unsure.** Which sponsor sits on the sleeves (text too small).
