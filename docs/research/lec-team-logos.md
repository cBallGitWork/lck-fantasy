# LEC logos, described for a blind redraw

For a designer who has never seen the logos: enough to draw a PES-style "unlicensed" badge for each team. Read 17 Nov 2026 by eye from the files in `scratchpad/lec-crests/logos/` (fetched from the Wikimedia and Liquipedia URLs listed per team), with SVG paths read for proportions and PNG colours counted pixel by pixel. Positions are given as a share of the logo's own width (x, from the left) and height (y, from the top).

## G2 Esports

**Saw it:** yes (Liquipedia PNG, 1715×2010).

1. **Silhouette.** A hooded/helmeted head-and-shoulders bust, about 1.17× taller than wide. Wide flat brim across the upper third, shoulders flaring to full width at y 50–55%, then tapering to a single sharp point at bottom centre. Reads as a pentagon-ish shield with a hat on.
2. **Elements, top to bottom.**
   - *Helmet crown and horns* (y 0–35%, x 25–75%): two thick black horns rise from the crown's sides and curl inward at the top like a crescent, their tips (at x 37% and 63%, y 0%) not meeting; a gap of white between them. Line weight about 6% of width.
   - *Crest* (y 11–21%, x 37–63%): a black bow-tie/butterfly shape hangs from the centre top between the horns: two triangular lobes meeting at a point at x 50%, y 14%.
   - *Brim* (y 22–35%, full width x 6–94%): wide, flat, thin black-outlined brim with pointed upturned ends at the far left and right, like a samurai kasa or wide hat.
   - *Face mask* (y 34–80%, x 26–74%): a tall white shield-shaped mask outlined in black (about 1.5% line), split down the exact centre by a thin vertical black line from y 47% to the chin point at y 80%. The brim's underside forms a downward V over the mask, meeting at x 50%, y 45%, like a scowling brow.
   - *Letters in the mask*: left half holds a black angular **G** (x 34–43%, y 43–66%); right half holds a black angular **2** (x 56–70%, y 39–66%), drawn with slashing diagonal cuts so it doubles as a scar.
   - *Red eye* (x 59–65%, y 44–51%): a single slanted almond eye on the right side only, filled red, with a black pupil dot at its inner corner; the 2's diagonal slashes through it.
   - *Shoulders/collar* (y 35–100%): thick black outline (about 5.5% width) running from the brim ends out to the shoulder corners (x 0% and 100%, y 54%) and down in straight lines to the bottom point. Two black hooks (collar ends) come in from the lower sides at y 72–90% and meet near the bottom centre, leaving a small notched white "M" collar at y 88–93%.
3. **Colours.** Black #000000 for every line and filled shape; white #FFFFFF for the mask and fills (transparent in the light-mode file); red #EE3D23 for the eye only. No background.
4. **Lettering.** "G2" built into the face, angular and slashed, not a typeface.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** The single red eye; the crescent horns with the bow-tie crest between; the face mask split down the middle into G and 2.
6. **Home.** Founded 24 Feb 2014 in Spain as Gamers2; now based in Berlin, Germany (Wikipedia infobox "Location: Berlin, Germany"). Not Japanese: the samurai is a mascot.
7. **PES version.** Keep a black-and-white horned warrior bust with one red eye; swap the bow-tie crest for a different crest shape, close the face (no letters, or a single "G" mark), and change the brim to a rounded hood or straight-bar helmet.

**Unsure.** Whether the white fill is pure white in the official dark-mode version; the file I saw has it transparent.

Sources: <https://liquipedia.net/commons/images/d/d3/G2_Esports_2019_lightmode.png>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G2_Esports>

## Fnatic

**Saw it:** yes (Wikipedia SVG, paths read).

1. **Silhouette.** A bare glyph, no frame, about 1.53× wider than tall (path bounds 3000×1960). Like a Japanese-style roof or a stylised lower-case "fn": two big sweeping blades and a short bar between them.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Left blade* (x 0–55%, y 0–100%): a thick curved band (about 12% of width thick) starting at the top centre, running left as a near-horizontal top edge, then sweeping down and out in a concave curve to end at the lower left (x 10–14%, y 75–100%) with a flat vertical cut. Its top-left tip is cut on a bevel, so the top-left corner points down-left like an eave. The band is thick at the top and narrows towards the bottom.
   - *Right blade* (x 53–100%): the left blade mirrored. The two blades' top inner ends nearly touch at the centre top, separated by a very thin gap (about 1% of width) at x 50%.
   - *Centre bar* (x 44–56%, y 56–100%): a short vertical block, flat top at y 56%, left edge straight to the bottom, right edge cut diagonally from y 67% down to a point at the bottom left, so it looks like a dagger or a flag pennant pointing down-left.
3. **Colours.** Orange #FF5900, the whole glyph. No background (usually shown on black or white).
4. **Lettering.** None in the mark; the FNATIC wordmark is separate.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** The two big roof-like arcs flaring outward; the short angled bar between them; flat bright orange.
6. **Home.** Founded 23 Jul 2004; headquarters London, England (Wikipedia infobox).
7. **PES version.** Keep orange-on-black and a symmetric two-arc "roof" shape; change the arcs to curve upward (a bowl or wings) or put the shape inside a roundel, and replace the dagger bar with a dot or small diamond.

**Unsure.** Whether the official orange is #FF5900 or a slightly different value; the SVG says #FF5900.

Sources: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/4/43/Esports_organization_Fnatic_logo.svg>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fnatic>

## Karmine Corp

**Saw it:** yes (Wikimedia SVG, paths read; Liquipedia cyan PNG).

1. **Silhouette.** Roughly square (SVG 1078×1078; cyan PNG about 1.05× wider than tall). A letter K with a fat tilted oval ring wrapped round it, the ring's long axis running from upper-left to lower-right at about 50° below horizontal.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Ring, upper-left part* (x 0–29%, y 0–74%): a thick crescent (up to 13% of width thick) curving round the top-left and down the left side like a "C", its thick part at the top-left. It stops at the K's stem with a flat vertical cut on both ends (top at y 0–15%, bottom at y 62–74%).
   - *K stem* (x 31–46%, y 1–100%): a tall vertical bar, about 15% of width wide. Its top and bottom ends are cut on a 45° slant: top end slopes down to the right, bottom end slopes down to the right, so the stem is a long parallelogram.
   - *K arm* (from stem middle, y 40–58%, up to a sharp point at x 86%, y 17%): one thick diagonal blade rising up-right at about 30° from the stem, with a sharp pointed tip at the top right. Only one arm: the K's lower leg is not drawn as a bar.
   - *Ring, right and lower part* (x 46–100%, y 30–98%): the ring continues behind the arm round the right side and swoops down to the bottom, a thick crescent widest at the lower right (about 18% thick). It is cut where it meets the stem and arm, with thin white gaps (about 1.5% of width) separating every piece.
   - *Ring, top-right sliver* (x 33–74%, y 1–29%): a thin tapering strip runs from the stem's top along the upper edge to meet the arm, completing the oval.
   - *Small wedge* (x 47–60%, y 85–100%): a small tapered piece under the arm, right of the stem's bottom, where the ring passes.
3. **Colours.** Cyan #00CCFF in the Liquipedia "allmode" PNG; the Wikimedia SVG is plain black. No background. The brand is often shown in white on dark navy.
4. **Lettering.** The K itself; with the ring it also reads as "KC". No wordmark in the icon.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** A K with an orbit-like tilted ring; the knife-blade arm pointing up-right; single flat light blue.
6. **Home.** Founded 30 Mar 2020 as Kameto Corp, renamed Karmine Corp 16 Nov 2020; based in Paris, France (Wikipedia infobox).
7. **PES version.** Keep a single blue letter-in-orbit; change the ring to a full unbroken circle or a horizontal ellipse, and use a plain block letter with no blade arm.

**Unsure.** Which colour is "official" today (cyan, white on navy, or black); I saw both cyan and black files.

Sources: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/96/Karmine_Corp_logo.svg>, <https://liquipedia.net/commons/images/d/d1/Karmine_Corp_allmode.png>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karmine_Corp>

## Movistar KOI

**Saw it:** yes (Liquipedia PNG, 2431×4461).

1. **Silhouette.** A tall rectangle, about 1.84× taller than wide, like a bookmark or a tall card.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Frame*: a thin rectangular outline (about 3.5% of width thick) round the whole logo, starting at y 9%. Its top edge is broken in the middle (x 22–76%) where the M sits.
   - *M* (x 24–75%, y 0–18%): Movistar's rounded, blobby "M": two soft bulbous humps, the right hump taller than the left, with a curved dip between, like a hand-drawn wave. It overlaps the frame's top edge, half above and half below it.
   - *K* (x 15–85%, y 26–49%): the letter K lying on its side. A wide bar across the top (y 26–36%) with small concave scoops cut into its two upper corners; a short vertical neck from its centre; below that, a wide downward-pointing chevron (an upside-down V, y 37–49%) spanning the full letter width. Heavy, chunky, slab-like.
   - *O* (x 15–85%, y 46–73%): a flattened hexagon, pointed top and bottom (points at x 50%), flat vertical sides; thick wall (about 9% of width) round a hexagonal hole.
   - *I* (x 15–85%, y 73–83%): a wide horizontal bar, with the same concave scoops on its two upper corners.
3. **Colours.** Movistar blue #009DE1 for everything; white #FFFFFF inside the frame. No other colour.
4. **Lettering.** K, O, I stacked top to bottom in a heavy custom slab style, each laid on its side; the M is Movistar's logo.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** The tall thin frame with the soft M breaking out of its top; the hexagon O; all one bright blue.
6. **Home.** KOI founded 15 Dec 2021; based in Madrid, Spain (Wikipedia infobox).
7. **PES version.** Keep a tall blue card with stacked heavy letters; drop the Movistar M (use a small wave or carp fin on top instead), make the O round or octagonal, and stack the letters upright.

**Unsure.** Nothing major; colour sampled, not from a brand page.

Sources: <https://liquipedia.net/commons/images/7/77/Movistar_KOI_allmode.png>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Movistar_KOI>

## Team Vitality

**Saw it:** yes (Liquipedia PNGs: icon 2474×3000 and icon-plus-wordmark).

1. **Silhouette.** A geometric bee face front-on, about 1.21× taller than wide: two tall wings at the top, a mask in the middle, a stinger point at the bottom centre. Overall like a "V" with ears.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Wings* (left x 0–35%, right x 65–100%; y 0–64%): two tall leaf/almond blades, each with a curved outer edge and a straight inner edge, tilting outward at about 20°. Each comes to a point at the bottom inside (x 23% and 77%, y 64%). Each is about 35% of the width at its widest.
   - *Head diamond* (x 40–60%, y 33–59%): a vertical diamond in the centre between the wings.
   - *Feelers* (x 3–19% and 81–97%, y 49–66%): small bent shapes on each side, like hooked brackets angled inward.
   - *Mask band* (x 16–84%, y 50–79%): a wide shape like a broad "W" or bat-wing: its top edge rises to two peaks (at x 37% and 63%) on either side of the diamond and dips to a V at the diamond's foot (x 50%, y 65%); its bottom edge is a shallow V; at each end a thin leg drops to y 79%.
   - *Stinger* (x 33–67%, y 77–100%): a downward triangle, pointed bottom at x 50%, its top edge a shallow V.
   - All pieces are separated by even white gaps about 2.5% of width.
3. **Colours.** Black #000000 shapes; shown black on Vitality yellow or yellow on black. No frame.
4. **Lettering.** None in the icon. The lockup adds "TEAM VITALITY" in two lines to the right: heavy, squared, stencil-like capitals; the A has no crossbar (like Λ) and several letters have small rounded notches where strokes join.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** The two tall tilted wing blades; the diamond between them; the stinger point. Black and yellow.
6. **Home.** Founded 5 Aug 2013; based in Paris, France, headquarters V.Hive on Boulevard de Sébastopol (Wikipedia).
7. **PES version.** Keep black-and-yellow and a front-on insect read; make the wings short and round (not tall blades), drop the diamond, and set it inside a hexagon (honeycomb) frame.

**Unsure.** The exact Vitality yellow; the PNG I saw is black only. #FFF000 in briefs.md is a stand-in, not checked.

Sources: <https://liquipedia.net/commons/images/e/e4/Team_Vitality_2023_lightmode.png>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Vitality>

## Team Heretics

**Saw it:** yes (Liquipedia 2024 PNG, 2400×3000; also the old pre-2021 logo, for contrast).

1. **Silhouette.** A shield about 1.25× taller than wide: gently domed top (highest at the centre), straight-ish sides bulging slightly outward, curving in to a single point at bottom centre.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Rim*: a gold border about 2.5% of width all round, with a thin inner black line then the black field.
   - *Hood* (x 17–83%, y 4–79%): a big white pointed-arch hood, like an egg or a gothic arch, peak at x 50%, y 4.5%. Its sides run down and curl inward at the bottom into hooked ends (at x 26% and 74%, y 79%).
   - *Face* (x 24–76%, y 25–100%): a black pointed arch inside the hood, the faceless dark interior, which flows down into the black body and the bottom of the shield.
   - *Eyes* (x 36–46% and 54–64%, y 43–46%): two small white slanted slits, thicker at the outer ends and tapering to points at the inner ends, angled down toward the centre: a frown.
   - *Cape panels* (x 10–29% and 71–90%, y 10–50%): two tall white curved blades along the upper sides, like the cape's collar edges, separated from the hood by black bands.
   - *Lower drapes* (x 11–35% and 65–89%, y 56–79%): white curved shapes on each lower side, hooking inward, like the cape's folds over the shoulders.
3. **Colours.** Black #000000 field (fading to about #1A1A1A at the lower right); white #FFFFFF hood (slight grey gradient to #F0F0F0); gold rim as a left-to-right gradient from #978753 to #C8B06E. Orange is not in the current mark.
4. **Lettering.** None. (The old logo had "HERETICS" and "E-SPORTS CLUB" and a sword; both are gone.)
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** A faceless hooded figure with two narrow white eyes; white hood on a black shield; gold rim.
6. **Home.** Founded 24 Aug 2016; based in Madrid, Spain (Wikipedia infobox).
7. **PES version.** Keep a black shield with a gold rim and a mysterious figure; show the hood from the side or as a monk's cowl with a lantern glow instead of eyes, and use a rounded-top shield.

**Unsure.** Whether brand guides call the gold a flat colour; the file uses a gradient.

Sources: <https://liquipedia.net/commons/images/a/a9/Team_Heretics_2024_allmode.png>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Heretics>

## SK Gaming

**Saw it:** yes (Wikipedia SVG, paths read).

1. **Silhouette.** Square (path bounds about 464×464 in a 500 box). An S and K monogram made of thick strokes, with 180° rotational symmetry in its outer bars.
2. **Elements.** Stroke width about 12.5% of width throughout.
   - *Top hook* (x 4–100%, y 4–55%): a thick bar along the top, its right end cut on a 45° slant (sloping down-left, ending at x 75% at the stroke's bottom edge, like a blade tip); it runs left, rounds the top-left corner with a large radius (about 25% of width), and runs down the left side, ending flat at y 55%.
   - *Bottom hook* (x 0–96%, y 45–96%): the top hook rotated 180°: a bar along the bottom, its left end cut on a 45° slant, rounding the bottom-right corner and running up the right side to y 45%.
   - *K arm* (x 29–65%, y 24–45%): a thick diagonal bar running from the upper centre (x 50–65%, y 24%) down-left to the left middle (x 29%, y 45%), like the upper arm of a "<".
   - *K leg* (x 29–68%, y 45–84%): from the arm's bottom, a thick bar runs right, then bends down with a rounded corner into a vertical stroke ending flat at y 84%. Together, arm and leg read as a K and the middle stroke of an S.
3. **Colours.** Black, flat (#000000; the SVG sets no fill, so it defaults to black). Shown white on dark too. No background.
4. **Lettering.** The S and K themselves; no wordmark in the mark.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** Two mirrored rounded-corner hooks with slashed ends; the angled K inside; plain black.
6. **Home.** Founded 1997 in Oberhausen as the Quake clan Schröt Kommando; now based in Cologne, Germany (Wikipedia).
7. **PES version.** Keep a black square monogram with mirrored rounded corners; use square ends instead of 45° slashes, and replace the K arm with a single horizontal bar so it reads as an S only.

**Unsure.** SK's own brand colours (Wikipedia lists black, grey, white); I saw only black.

Sources: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/4/4d/SK_Gaming_logo.svg>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SK_Gaming>

## GIANTX

**Saw it:** yes (Wikipedia SVG, paths read).

1. **Silhouette.** A pointy-topped hexagon, about 1.15× taller than wide (284×328), drawn as a thick outline that opens on the right to make a G.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Hexagon outline*: thick (about 12% of width). Vertices at top centre, upper left and right (y 25%), lower left and right (y 75%), bottom centre.
   - *Star notches* (top and bottom vertices): at the top and bottom points the outline is split by a sharp V-notch cut in from inside, so each vertex becomes a pointed four-point star tip, about 13% of height deep (inner points at y 13% and 87%).
   - *G opening* (right side, y 31–44%): the upper-right edge stops short, leaving a gap between it and a block below.
   - *G jaw* (x 76–100%, y 44–75%): a horizontal block at the right sticking out past the hexagon's edge, flat right side; a small inner wedge sits just inside it (x 63–81%, y 45–65%).
   - *X* (x 19–81%, y 35–65%): a wide, flattened X made of two blades: a wide chevron across the top (y 35–44%, both arms pointing up-outward, meeting in a notch at x 50%), and a diagonal blade from upper right to lower left (x 19–60%, y 44–65%). Wider than tall, about 2:1.
3. **Colours.** Black #000000, flat. No background in the SVG.
4. **Lettering.** G (the hexagon) and X (inside). No wordmark.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** The hexagon that opens into a G; the flat wide X inside; the star-like notched top and bottom points.
6. **Home.** Formed 14 Dec 2023 by merging Excel Esports (UK) and Giants Gaming (Spain); based in London, United Kingdom and Málaga, Spain (Wikipedia infobox).
7. **PES version.** Keep a black hexagon with an X inside; close the hexagon (no G opening), drop the star notches, and use a tall narrow X.

**Unsure.** Any brand accent colour; the SVG is black only.

Sources: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0e/GiantX_logo.svg>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GiantX>

## Natus Vincere

**Saw it:** yes (Wikimedia SVG, paths read).

1. **Silhouette.** A black parallelogram slanting up to the right (213.7×188.1, about 1.14× wider than tall overall). Left and right edges vertical; top and bottom edges rise at about 19.5° to the right. The band is about 60% of the overall height.
2. **Elements.** Four yellow letters inside, each leaning with the band, all about 42% of overall height tall, each about 13% of width wide.
   - *N* (x 7–46%): left stem (x 7–21%), a diagonal piece (x 23–45%) running from the stem's top-right down to the bottom right, and a short right stem (x 33–46%) that stops where the diagonal meets it. The diagonal is cut from the stems by black slices.
   - *A* (x 48–76%): a legless wedge: a left upright, a diagonal sloping down to the right, and a small triangular notch where the crossbar would be; no crossbar, no right leg.
   - *V* (x 64–77%): an upright whose lower part is cut by a diagonal, overlapping the A's right side.
   - *I* (x 80–93%): a plain upright bar.
   - Letters are separated by thin black diagonal slices, so the word reads as one cut-up block.
3. **Colours.** Yellow #FDF135 letters on black #000105. No other colour.
4. **Lettering.** "NAVI" in heavy, squared, sliced capitals. Often "NATUS VINCERE" in small type below (not in this file).
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** The slanted black bar; bright yellow sliced letters; yellow on black.
6. **Home.** Founded 17 Dec 2009; headquartered in Kyiv, Ukraine, with an office in Berlin, Germany (Wikipedia infobox).
7. **PES version.** Keep yellow on black and a slant; use a horizontal bar or a shield, and write the letters whole (no slices), or use initials "NV".

**Unsure.** Nothing major.

Sources: <https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/52/NAVI-Logo.svg>, <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natus_Vincere>

## Shifters

**Saw it:** yes (Liquipedia PNG, 3000×2938).

1. **Silhouette.** Nearly round (about 1.02× wider than tall): a disc made of **three** thick swirling blades, not four, with white gaps between them that run in towards the centre.
2. **Elements.**
   - *Three blades*, set 120° apart (top right, left, bottom), each a thick curved band (about 25% of width) sweeping clockwise round the centre. Outer corners are slightly rounded.
   - *Hooks*: each blade's leading tip ends in a sharp hooked barb, like an arrowhead or a fish hook, bending back inward (for example the top-right blade's barb at x 85%, y 47%).
   - *Notches*: each blade's trailing end has a small inward-pointing V-notch where the next blade's hook tucks in.
   - *White gaps*: the curved white channels between blades swirl in to the centre and together form an "S" shape through the middle.
3. **Colours.** Purple #7A52B9, flat, all blades. No background; white is empty space.
4. **Lettering.** None; the S is in the negative space.
5. **Recognisable at a glance.** A purple disc that looks like a turbine or camera shutter; three hooked arrow blades; the S in the gaps.
6. **Home.** Founded 2018 as Team BDS; renamed Shifters 16 Dec 2025; headquarters Geneva, Switzerland (Bel-Air district). The team says the logo focuses on the letter S, with arrows converging on the centre for motion and union.
7. **PES version.** Keep a purple round mark with swirl motion; use four or six plain curved blades with no hooks, or a single S-shaped arrow.

**Unsure.** Nothing on the logo; whether Shifters play in 2027 (see briefs.md).

Sources: <https://liquipedia.net/commons/images/e/e2/Shifters_allmode.png>, <https://www.hotspawn.com/league-of-legends/news/team-bds-rebrands-to-shifters>, <https://www.sheepesports.com/articles/lol-team-bds-rebrand-to-shifters/en>

## Corrections to briefs.md

- **Shifters**: three hooked blades, not four.
- **Team Heretics**: the gold rim is a gradient (#978753 to #C8B06E); the current mark has no orange.
- **G2**: the head wears a wide-brimmed hat with crescent horns; the bow-tie crest sits between the horns, not on the brim. G is on the left cheek, 2 on the right, the red eye on the right.
- **Karmine Corp**: the K has one arm, a blade pointing up-right; the ring forms the lower leg.
- **Fnatic**: founded 2004, based in London.
- **GIANTX**: based in London and Málaga.
