# Truck culture in the LCK (트럭 시위, 커피차)

Research for Flol, 14 Nov 2026. Background only, not product.

**Contents:** [Summary](#summary) · [Method](#method) · [Protest trucks](#1-protest-trucks-트럭-시위) · [Coffee trucks](#2-support-and-coffee-trucks-커피차-서포트) · [Brand and joke trucks](#3-brand-and-joke-trucks) · [The meme abroad](#4-the-meme-abroad) · [For the game](#5-for-the-game) · [Sensitivities](#sensitivities) · [Sources](#sources)

## Summary

1. Korean fans rent LED trucks to drive or park outside a team's office or LoL Park, with rotating messages. It started in K-pop (late 2019) and reached LoL in November 2020 (T1 and KT).
2. T1 gets almost all of them: 2020, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. DRX and KT fans have sent them too. Most target coaches and management; some target one player.
3. The tone is formal and blunt: a demand, a named target, a "take responsibility" line. A few are sarcastic (the 2023 Gumayusi truck).
4. The positive twin is the coffee truck (커피차) and birthday ad, also from K-pop. Coffee trucks reach LoL Park, but no named LCK case turned up.
5. Abroad, "the truck" is a running joke ("truck meta", "send a truck"). It is also tied to real harassment of Gumayusi (2025), so a game must keep it light and never aim it at a player.

## Method

- **Checked** means I read the page (through a fetch summary). **Second-hand** means search snippet or a search tool's summary only.
- Several pages blocked fetching (Sheep Esports, esports.gg, Dot Esports, Fanlore, the HobbyDrama mirror). Facts from those are second-hand.
- Dates are the article date unless stated.

## 1. Protest trucks (트럭 시위)

### How it works

- Fans raise money on a forum (DC Inside galleries), send it to one volunteer, who rents the truck. Checked: Korea JoongAng Daily via Daum, 19 Jan 2021.
- It became popular in the pandemic because it needs no police protest filing and no crowd. Checked: same.
- Cost: one 2021 game protest raised 9.5m won for three trucks over five days, roughly 630,000 won per truck per day. Checked: same.
- First K-pop case cited: Blackpink fans at YG, 16 Dec 2019. First game-fan cases: T1 and KT fans, Nov 2020. Checked: same.
- Legal in Korea if peaceful; T1 cannot stop them. Second-hand: Sheep Esports, 24 Jul 2026.
- Often run by a small, loud group (the "galleries"), not the fanbase. Second-hand: Sheep Esports, 24 Jul 2026.
- One estimate: up to 15 trucks, 6 LED boards, 50 funeral wreaths sent to T1's offices over the years. Second-hand: Sheep Esports, 24 Jul 2026. Another source tallies 15 trucks, 6 LED boards, 100+ banners and 50 wreaths against Gumayusi in one year. Second-hand: search summary, source unclear.

### Reactions

- **Joe Marsh, T1 CEO (Aug 2022):** tweeted that about 100 "fans" wasted money on a truck and do not speak for the other million. Checked: Inven Global, 4 Aug 2022.
- **LS, caster and later coach (Aug 2023):** called the people behind the Gumayusi truck disturbing, for harassing players at their practice place. Checked: Dexerto, 22 Aug 2023. Mein-MMO reports harsher words. Checked: Mein-MMO, 23 Aug 2023.
- **LS (Apr 2024):** posted on X that the "truckening" had begun again. Checked: esports.net, 6 Apr 2024 (quoting his post; I did not see the post).
- **Bwipo (Nov 2020):** told unhappy T1 fans to reconsider why they follow T1, and defended LS. Checked: NME, 9 Nov 2020.
- **T1 (Aug 2022):** banned fans from its office, practice rooms and players' homes, citing COVID. Checked: esports.net, 22 Aug 2022.
- **DRX (2025):** no official answer to the town-hall truck. Coach Cho Jae-eup took the blame for 9th on the fan cafe. Checked: Inven Global, 14 Sep 2026.
- **Players:** no player I found publicly answered a truck. Doran stayed silent. Checked: Insider Gaming, 10 Jul 2026.
- **Faker:** a Dot Esports headline says Faker sympathised with fans over a truck protest; I could not open it. Second-hand: dotesports.com/?p=649842.

### Memes from them

- "Truck meta", "the truckening", "dodge the truck memes". Checked: Dexerto, 22 Aug 2023; esports.net, 6 Apr 2024.
- Joe Marsh's "100 fans" line became a joke in itself. Second-hand: search summary.
- After T1 lost to Gen.G, Reddit joked about sending Gen.G a congratulations truck so they don't feel left out. Second-hand: HobbyDrama recap, Aug 2022.
- Some suspected one truck was sent by Chinese fans, not T1's. Second-hand: same.

## 2. Support and coffee trucks (커피차, 서포트)

- **K-pop origin:** fans and fellow stars send coffee or snack trucks (커피차, 간식차) to film sets and studios, with a banner thanking the star and crew. Second-hand: isplus, Sep 2026 (actors Suzy and Kim Seon-ho).
- **In the LCK:** fan agencies arrange coffee trucks at LoL Park, as they do at K-pop studios. Fans also buy LED birthday ads in Seoul subway stations. Checked: RFT, 6 Apr 2026. No named player, date or truck found, in English or Korean.
- **Birthday ads:** Chovy's birthday, 3 Mar 2022: fan ads at bus stops and the subway station by LoL Park; Gen.G goods for ticket holders. Checked: Inven Global, 3 Mar 2022.
- **Faker's birthday:** a Times Square billboard from a Chinese fan union. Second-hand: snippet. T1 gave Faker a car in May 2025. Second-hand: One Esports.
- **Overseas birthday cafes:** 100+ fans held a Doran birthday cafe in Hanoi. Second-hand: Korea Times, 2 Aug 2025.
- **Good-deed karma:** T1 fans did charity to "give T1 karma" in 2023; Gumayusi said it shows how much fans want them to win. Checked: blog repost of a Worlds 2023 media-day item.
- **Support trucks after a protest:** in K-pop, rival groups of fans send trucks with opposite messages (NewJeans 2025). Second-hand: Korea Times, 28 Mar 2025. I found no LCK counter-truck.

## 3. Brand and joke trucks

- **G2, Sep 2022:** brought a truck to the LEC Xpo in Malmö as a nod to the Korean "truck meta". The screen showed its CEO asleep with a sign, roughly "wake me when G2 plays". Second-hand: AFK Gaming (redirects).
- **G2, Jul 2026:** "When does G2 play?" campaign: four LED trucks through Paris to the offices of Karmine Corp and Vitality, ahead of the EWC. Checked: G2 press release, 15 Jul 2026.
- **G2 in Seoul:** a search summary claims a G2 LED truck stopped outside T1 and Gen.G. Undated and unconfirmed. Second-hand.
- No case found of an LCK team or Riot sending a truck back, or of a player reacting on stream. Flag as unknown, not as "never happened".

## 4. The meme abroad

- English fans say "send a truck", "the truck is coming", "truck meta", "the truckening", usually after a T1 loss. Checked (terms): Dexerto 2023, esports.net 2024. Phrasing beyond that: second-hand.
- Typical jokes: a truck for the winners too ("congratulations truck"); a truck for any small mistake; asking which player gets the truck this week. Second-hand: HobbyDrama recap.
- Western press frames it as both funny and toxic, often in one article. Checked: Mein-MMO 2023; esports.net 2022.

## 5. For the game

### Truck types

- **Protest truck** (트럭): demands a sacking, a benching or an answer.
- **Coffee truck** (커피차): thanks, birthday, "fighting".
- **Congratulations truck**: the joke reply, sent to the winner.
- **Funeral wreath** (근조화환): real, but dark; leave it out.

### How often

- T1 gets about one to three a year, clustered after an early exit or a lost final. Checked across the table.
- Others: DRX roughly yearly when bad; KT once. Most teams never.

### What makes one funny, not mean

- Funny: aimed at the fan's own choices, at coaches as a role, at the team, at fate. Sarcasm about stats. A demand that is absurd or tiny.
- Mean: names one player, mocks his play, says "out", goes to his home or family. The 2023 and 2025 Gumayusi trucks are the line not to cross.

### Original lines in the style (not copies)

Protest, aimed at the user's own fantasy team or at "management":
- MANAGEMENT MUST EXPLAIN THIS CAPTAIN PICK.
- WE DID NOT PAY 9.5 FOR A BENCH WARMER. (the price is the user's own)
- ANSWER US: WHY NO TRANSFER THIS WEEK?
- THIS SQUAD HAS NO ROADMAP.
- STOP CLINGING TO LAST WEEK'S RANK.
- THE DRAFT IS NOT A STRATEGY. IT IS A PRAYER.
- WE WANT A TOWN HALL. TODAY.
- PAST GLORY IS ALL THIS LEAGUE HAS LEFT.

Coffee truck, cheer:
- COFFEE FOR THE BOTTOM LANE. YOU EARNED IT.
- CAPTAIN, THIS LATTE IS ON US.
- 화이팅! THE WHOLE LEAGUE BELIEVES IN YOU.
- HAPPY BIRTHDAY. PLEASE PENTAKILL.

League banter, to a rival manager:
- CONGRATULATIONS ON YOUR GREEN ARROW. ENJOY IT.
- THIS TRUCK WAS FUNDED BY YOUR CAPTAIN'S BLANK.
- PARKED OUTSIDE UNTIL YOU MAKE A TRANSFER.

## Sensitivities

- **Gumayusi, 2025:** months of harassment from a T1 forum group: doxxing, billboard trucks circling T1 and his address, death and acid-attack threats, wreaths to work and home. Lawmaker Jeon Yong-gi asked KeSPA, Riot Korea and the clubs to act. Checked: esports.net, 21 Nov 2025; RFT, 6 Apr 2026.
- **Legal action:** Faker and Gumayusi's agency filed civil and criminal charges in April 2026. Checked: RFT, 6 Apr 2026. Oner's agency confirmed an arrest over malicious posts. Second-hand: Sheep Esports.
- **Earlier:** death threats to players, families and staff in Nov 2020. Checked: NME, 9 Nov 2020. A weapon was sent to KT's facility in 2022. Second-hand: AFK Gaming.
- **Players asking fans to stop:** none found. Team voices (Joe Marsh, LS, Bwipo) and a politician did. T1 banned fans near practice and homes.
- **Rules for Flol, from the above:** never let a truck name a real player in a negative line; no free text on a public truck (or moderate it); no wreaths; keep protest trucks pointed at managers (the users) and at made-up "management", not at pros; keep coffee trucks free to point at real players.
