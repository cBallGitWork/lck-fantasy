# src/rift

The Rift view and Card view: the map, the tiles, the shirts, the role icons, the lineup cards and the bench. Import them from `src/rift` (`index.ts`). The Rift builds on `src/parts`, through its `index.ts`; parts never import the Rift.

- `RiftMap.tsx`: Summoner's Rift from the game's own minimap, as the approved picture draws it (`docs/design/approved/pick-team/map.js`): walls, lanes, the river and its pits, a stone court in each base, the lanes' towers, inhibitors and nexus. Its plan is `riftPlan.ts`, in its own 720 square (`PLAN`); its colours are the game's (`src/theme/game.ts`), the same in every league.
- `Tile.tsx`: `Tile`, one player on the Rift or the bench, as the approved picture (`docs/design/approved/pick-team/`): a price chip over it (Transfers) and `×` to sell, `↺` to put a sold one back; on the Rift, his role strip (`Top`, `Flex`, `Coach`); his team's kit on a solid card, C past its top right corner and a yellow warning triangle for his news; team code and name on a white plate with your dot; then a dark band: points and series, or fixtures; selected, fit, or dim (darkened). `Rift`, the map with the tiles stood on it: square, as big as the first screen allows, its tiles sized as shares of it; narrower than `measure.riftPhone` it takes the phone's tile sizes and places.
- `slots.ts`: where each starter stands, in the 720 square, on a desk and on a phone: `RIFT_SLOTS` (Pick Team, Points, Perfect game): Top level with Flex, Jungle and Mid in their places, Coach under Top, Bot beside Support under Flex. A tile stands at the same share of the Rift at any width.
- `Lineup.tsx`: Card view: `Lineup`, the seven `LineupCard`s (not the Player card screen) in the broadcast's order, with photo or crest, role head (Flex plays X, Coach 감독), C and a footer, dim or fit.
- `MarkBadge.tsx`: the Captain's C, round and filled, one way everywhere, `small`, `card` or `sheet`; `Mark` and `MARK_NAME`. The page places it.
- `SlotName.tsx`: a slot's label on a tile or a lineup card; the Flex's with its hover, Any role.
- `RoleIcon.tsx`: the role icons: top, jungle, mid, bot, support, coach, flex.
- `Shirt.tsx`: a team's kit (`data/lck/shirts/`), as a tile wears it, or the plain shirt with a team code.
- `roles.ts`: role names, the five player roles (`ROLE_KEYS`), the lineup order, the squad's size, each slot's icon and tag, the role a Flex plays (`flexPlays`); a player's role or coach (`PersonRole`), and the squad's nine places (`SquadSlot`, `BENCH_SLOTS`, `isBench`).
- `views.ts`: the two views' names, `Rift view | Card view` (`VIEWS`, `RIFT_VIEW`, `BoardView`).
- `BenchBar.tsx`: the bench, a panel on the Rift's ground, its tiles (`BenchTile`: the Rift's own `Tile` under its order and role, `1. Mid`, as FPL's bench and its `1. DEF`; it can be chosen). No swap key: tap one bench player, then the other (Craig, 16 Nov 2026).
- `RiftCard.tsx`: the player's card under the Rift, one part for Points and Transfers (plate.md, The player's card): its head on the blue band read as a tile (crest, role strip, team bar and code, name, the page's marks, the key figure at the right); `ResultChip` (`W` and `L` outlined, `Live` filled) and `CardPoints` (boxed when counted) for its body.
- `PlayerPanel.tsx`: a tapped player's panel: his crest at 64 or a shirt, name, meta, ✕, then the page's body. The body's pieces: `PanelSeries` (`v HLE`, its result, its games), `PanelGame` (a game, counted points boxed), `PanelSum` with `PanelSumLabel` (the sum), `PanelFoot` (`Full stats ›`).
